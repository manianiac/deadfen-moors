Here is a 2d6 rumor table tailored specifically to the starting town (Hex N.10) and the immediate surrounding hexes in the Deadfen Moors.

To use this table, roll 2d6. The first die determines the tens digit (10-60), and the second die determines the ones digit (1-6), giving you 36 unique results ranging from 11 to 66.

**Legend:**

* **(T) True:** Accurate information, though perhaps colored by peasant superstition.
* **(PT) Partially True:** There is a kernel of truth, but the townspeople have misunderstood the cause, the scale, or the culprits.
* **(F) False:** Complete fabrications, outright lies, or dangerous misconceptions.

### 2d6 Deadfen Moors Rumor Table

**11-16: The Fens & Flora**

* **11 (T):** "Don't go near the water at The Red Hole (N.9). The giant rats there are completely bald, and their bite gives you a fever that rots you from the inside out."
* **12 (PT):** "The mud in the western bogs (M.10) is cursed! It'll swallow a man whole if he steps off the roots." *(The mud itself isn't cursed, but the Copper-Grubber scavengers dig hidden pit-traps under the water).*
* **13 (PT):** "If you can harvest the glowing moss off the old metal ruins, it'll heal any wound in seconds." *(Eldritch moss does have potent properties, but eating it raw without a trained Herbalist or Alchemist is highly toxic).*
* **14 (F):** "The Ash-Rats are just normal rats that got into a careless wizard's alchemy supplies." *(They are mutated by the ancient necrotic ash of the Scourge wars).*
* **15 (T):** "If you drink the water pooling near the Spire of Ugallu, it tastes like old copper and gives you nightmares of drowning in black sludge."
* **16 (F):** "The fog that rolls in from the moors at night is actually the breath of the Withered King, looking to claim new souls." *(It's just natural, if foul-smelling, swamp gas).*

**21-26: The Copper-Grubbers (The Low Mud - M.10)**

* **21 (T):** "Those desperate exiles who live in the western mud have banded together. They aren't just sifting for scrap anymore; they're ambushing lone travelers."
* **22 (F):** "I heard the Copper-Grubbers dug up a working Ascended wand, and they're planning to blast the town walls down!" *(They are barely armed with slings and clubs).*
* **23 (PT):** "Old man Higgins was murdered by the Grubbers just for his leather boots!" *(Higgins was actually killed by Ash-Rats, but the Grubbers found his body and stole his boots).*
* **24 (T):** "The Grubbers are cowards. They won't fight you face-to-face, but they string up tripwires right under the murky water to drown you."
* **25 (F):** "You can buy safe passage through the Low Mud by just tossing the Grubbers a few fresh apples." *(They want your silver, your steel, and your life).*
* **26 (PT):** "The scavengers worship a giant toad out in the bogs." *(They don't worship a toad, but they do aggressively guard a strange, smooth-stone Nabu ruin they treat as a "sacred" claim).*

**31-36: The Iron Circle (O.10)**

* **31 (T):** "There's a scholar in town paying triple the going rate for any un-tarnished 'silver' you can drag out of the ruins east of here."
* **32 (PT):** "The Iron Circle is packed to the brim with ancient gold, completely unguarded!" *(It contains valuable Nabu tech, but it is swarming with lethal Scrubber drones).*
* **33 (F):** "If you whistle a high C note, the metal snakes in the ruins will fall asleep and let you walk right past them." *(Scrubbers are deaf, mindless constructs that never sleep).*
* **34 (T):** "The 'metal snakes' in the ruins don't bite to eat you. They inject a blinding chemical solvent that makes you violently ill for days."
* **35 (PT):** "The town miller accidentally dug into an ancient treasure vault under his root cellar!" *(He dug into a Nabu ventilation shaft, but it's empty of treasure and currently leaking Ash-Rats into his grain).*
* **36 (F):** "The Iron Circle is perfectly safe during the day. The ancient magic only turns on when the sun goes down."

**41-46: The Iron-Dogs & The Necromancer**

* **41 (T):** "A company of heavily armored mercenary rogues have set up a barricade on The Thundering Ridge (P.12) and are extorting merchants."
* **42 (PT):** "Kaelen the Breaker, the bandit lord, wields a magical spear that can pierce through solid stone." *(It is a masterwork Nabu Halberd, not a spear, but it is indeed magically sharp).*
* **43 (F):** "The Iron-Dogs are actually secretly on the Town Guard's payroll to collect extra taxes." *(The Town Captain hates them and wants them gone).*
* **44 (T):** "A perfectly pale, hairless wizard has moved into the Shrouded Crystal ruins (K.11). The trees are dying in a perfect circle around his camp."
* **45 (PT):** "The dead are rising in the western woods to exact a bloody revenge on the living!" *(Silas the Pallid is raising the dead, but they don't want revenge; they just want to enforce perfectly silent, motionless order).*
* **46 (F):** "If you pay the Iron-Dogs enough coin, you can hire them to clear out the local dungeons for you." *(They will simply take your coin, slit your throat, and take your boots).*

**51-56: Goblins, Dwarves, and Elves**

* **51 (T):** "There are pale, hairless goblins with too many joints stealing sheep and dragging them screaming toward The Muck's Rock (L.8)."
* **52 (PT):** "Those freaky goblins are made of mud! When you kill them, they just melt away." *(They do dissolve into gray sludge, but they are biological homunculi spawned by a Scourge, not mud spirits).*
* **53 (T):** "If you need masterwork plate armor and have a cart full of raw copper ore, Clan Zabar at The Copper Cave (N.8) will trade with you."
* **54 (F):** "The Zabar dwarves will teach you how to build a steam-powered suit of armor if you buy them enough ale." *(They are deeply paranoid constructs who share no secrets).*
* **55 (PT):** "The ghost of an ancient Elven general haunts the fens, looking for his lost magic sword." *(The fractured ghost of Enlil does haunt several ruins, but he is looking for his sanity and his fallen comrades, not a sword).*
* **56 (F):** "The pale goblins are working with Kaelen's bandits to sack the town before winter!" *(The Flesh-Weaver's drones are mindless and would just try to eat the bandits).*

**61-66: The God-Kings & Cosmic Lore**

* **61 (T):** "The Clerics of the Bright Knight are strict. If they let a single speck of rust touch their maces, they have to fast and pray to get their magic back."
* **62 (PT):** "The Withered King, Alaric, is buried right under the town square. That's why the fens are so rotten." *(Alaric is buried far away, but his Withered Path cultists do pass through, drawn by the rampant decay).*
* **63 (F):** "If you sleep a foot off the ground, priests of the Ascendant Sky will bless you with a bag of gold." *(They will just assume you know your place as a lowly peasant).*
* **64 (T):** "Don't mess with the Twin Vanguard clerics. Their religion forces them to hunt down the biggest monster they can find every season to prove their worth."
* **65 (PT):** "There is a massive, fire-breathing dragon sleeping directly beneath the moors, waiting to burn the world." *(Azag the First Dragon does exist, but he is sealed far away at The Doomed Mountain, not right under the town).*
* **66 (F):** "The Ascended Ones are coming back tomorrow at dawn to whisk all the righteous up into the sky!" *(A favorite local doomsday panic that never comes true).*
