# Dumuzi

The preserved, tragic offspring of either a late-stage [[Nabu|Nabu]] or an early Inhuman [[God-Kings|God-King]]. Hidden away in a stasis pod to survive the Eldritch war, but never awoke. His resting places are heavily defended.
