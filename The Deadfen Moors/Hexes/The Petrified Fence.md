# The Petrified Fence

**Hex Label:** F.14
**Terrain Type:** Plains (with scrub)


## Main POI: The Petrified Fence
- The Petrified Fence is a roughly 30-foot high, continuous wall constructed of petrified wood, stretching approximately 1.5 miles from the western edge of the hex towards a small, dry riverbed. The wood is a dark, almost black, color and incredibly dense to the touch. It displays swirling patterns resembling ancient tree roots.
- At the fence’s center, approximately halfway to the riverbed, lies a circular clearing choked with strangely iridescent moss. The air within the clearing is noticeably colder and carries a faint, metallic scent. Three, weathered stone markers stand within the clearing, each bearing a single, stylized eye.
- Scavenged remains of a humanoid settlement – mostly shattered ceramic shards and rusted metal – are scattered along the fence’s base, suggesting a hasty abandonment. Tracks of large, bipedal creatures, currently unidentifiable, are faintly visible in the dust near the eastern edge.



## Additional POI 1: Whispering Stones
- Located 2 miles north of the Petrified Fence, a cluster of standing stones emits a low, mournful hum, audible only at twilight.
- The stones are covered in faded glyphs depicting stylized wolves and a celestial body.
- Locals claim the stones reveal glimpses of past events when touched by a willing mind.

## Additional POI 2: Dry Creek Crossing
- Situated 3 miles southeast of the Petrified Fence, a partially collapsed stone bridge spans the dry riverbed. 
- The riverbed itself is remarkably clean, displaying a layer of smooth, grey pebbles.
- Several skeletons, clad in scavenged leather armor, lie near the bridge’s foundations, indicating a possible ambush site.
