# Spire of Ugallu

**Hex Label:** B.4
**Terrain Type:** Hills


## Main POI: Spire of Ugallu
- The Spire of Ugallu dominates the central hills, a jagged obsidian structure rising 300 feet. Its surface is slick with an oily residue and etched with unsettling, swirling glyphs believed to be the source of the corruption emanating from within.
- At the base of the Spire lies a circular clearing choked with twisted, black vegetation and pools of stagnant, crimson water. The air here vibrates with a low, unsettling hum.
- Within the Spire's lower levels, a labyrinth of narrow corridors and echoing chambers pulsates with dark magic. The source of Ugallu’s power—a massive, pulsating heart of corrupted shadow—is rumored to be located at the Spire’s summit.



## Additional POI 1: Cliff of the Devil Ugallu
- The Cliff of the Devil Ugallu is a sheer drop of volcanic rock, perpetually wreathed in swirling mist and echoing with the sounds of demonic laughter.
-  Scattered amongst the base of the cliff are the bleached bones of countless victims, frozen in expressions of terror. 
-  A narrow, treacherous path clings to the cliff face, said to be a favored route for Ugallu’s lesser servants.



## Additional POI 2: Ugallu’s Spring
- Ugallu’s Spring is a small, circular pool of unnaturally warm water, bubbling constantly with a viscous, dark liquid. 
- The water is said to grant visions - often of madness and despair - to those who drink from it. 
-  Strange, phosphorescent fungi thrive around the spring’s edges, casting an eerie glow in the twilight.
