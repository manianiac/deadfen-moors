# The Ruin of Anu

**Hex Label:** E.6
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Ruin of Anu
- The Ruin of Anu dominates the center of the hex, a cyclopean structure of black obsidian partially buried in the plains. Geometric patterns, impossibly precise, cover the walls, radiating a faint, pulsing heat. 
- Twisted metal constructs, clearly Nabu in origin, litter the plaza surrounding the main structure, many damaged and fused with the obsidian. Strange, resonant hums emanate intermittently from these remains.
- A shallow, circular basin at the center of the plaza contains a pool of viscous, silver liquid – believed to be a remnant of Anu’s power, radiating potent energy but also exhibiting unpredictable temporal distortions.



## Additional POI 1: Echoing Cairns
- Scattered across the western edge of the hex are seven stone cairns, each roughly 10 feet tall.
- The cairns resonate with faint echoes of Nabu voices, strongest during the hours of dusk and dawn.
- Local tribes whisper that disturbing the cairns summons fragmented memories of the Ascension.


## Additional POI 2: The Scarred Mesa
- Located on the northeastern border of the hex, a mesa stands isolated from the plains.
- The mesa’s surface is marred by a massive, radiating scar, the precise origin of which is unknown.
- Analysis of the scar’s material reveals traces of Anu’s energy signature, suggesting it was created during a catastrophic event linked to his time.
