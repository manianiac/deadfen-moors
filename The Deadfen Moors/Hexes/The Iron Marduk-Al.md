# The Iron Marduk-Al

**Hex Label:** G.6
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Iron Marduk-Al
- The Iron Marduk-Al dominates the center of hex G.6: a monolithic structure approximately 300 feet tall constructed from dull, rust-colored metal. Fine, intricate patterns, possibly algorithmic in nature, cover its surface.
- Locals, primarily nomadic tribes known as the Dust Runners, believe the Marduk-Al is a slumbering god, “The Iron God,” and perform regular rituals of offerings – typically metal scraps and oil – at its base. These rituals are often accompanied by unsettling, rhythmic pulses emanating from within the structure.
- The area immediately surrounding the Marduk-Al is devoid of vegetation, the ground scorched black and cracked by unknown heat sources. Sensors detect residual energy signatures consistent with Nabu technology.



## Additional POI 1: Whispering Cairns
- A collection of weathered stone cairns dot the eastern edge of the hex.
- Locals claim the cairns amplify the Marduk-Al's pulses, creating unsettling auditory hallucinations.
- Analysis of the cairns reveals traces of a sealant compound identical to that used in Nabu construction.


## Additional POI 2: The Rust River
- A slow-moving, reddish-brown river cuts through the southern portion of hex G.6.
- The water is exceptionally corrosive, quickly dissolving metal and leaving behind a thick layer of rust.
- Survey data indicates the river originates from a subterranean fissure near the Marduk-Al, potentially a source of the energy fluctuations.
