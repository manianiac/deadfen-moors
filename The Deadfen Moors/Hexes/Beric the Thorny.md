# Beric the Thorny

**Hex Label:** G.12
**Terrain Type:** Plains (with scrub)


## Main POI: Beric the Thorny
- The primary POI is a colossal, partially-buried construct of obsidian-like material, roughly humanoid in shape, dominating the center of the hex. Thick, razor-sharp spines protrude from its body, constantly shifting slightly as if reacting to the wind. 
- The ground surrounding Beric the Thorny is barren, choked with thorny scrub resistant to most fire, and stained a dark, reddish-brown from centuries of unknown seepage.  Scattered amongst the scrub are shattered fragments of similar obsidian material.
-  A faint, rhythmic pulsing emanates from within the construct, accompanied by a low, guttural hum that seems to resonate with the surrounding plains.  Tracks - massive, three-toed prints - lead away from the structure, disappearing into the distance.



## Additional POI 1: Caelen’s Scar
- Located 2 miles northeast of Beric the Thorny, this is a circular depression in the earth, approximately 50 feet across, marked by a shimmering, iridescent film.
- The film is said to be a residual manifestation of Caelen’s energy, constantly fluctuating and occasionally releasing bursts of static electricity. 
- Investigation reveals remnants of advanced technology within the scar, suggesting Caelen attempted to manipulate the structure’s defenses.



## Additional POI 2: The Broken Well
- Situated 3 miles west of Beric the Thorny, this dried-up well is constructed from the same obsidian material as the main construct.
- The well’s rim is fractured and etched with symbols that appear to represent a stylized thorn. 
-  Analysis of the sediment within the well indicates an extremely high concentration of a previously unidentified radioactive isotope.
