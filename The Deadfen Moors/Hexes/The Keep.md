# The Keep

**Hex Label:** D.8
**Terrain Type:** Hills


## Main POI: The Keep
- [[The Keep]] is a crumbling stone structure, partially overgrown with ivy and lichen, situated atop the highest point within the hex. Approximately 70% of the original tower remains, though the upper levels are collapsed and unstable. 
- A deep, dry well lies within [[The Keep|the Keep]]'s courtyard, the water reputedly possessing faintly phosphorescent qualities – though investigations have yielded nothing but stagnant water and unsettling whispers.
- Scattered around [[The Keep|the Keep]]’s perimeter are the remains of a once-extensive defensive wall, constructed from roughly hewn stone and now largely eroded by centuries of wind and rain. Several archer slits remain, though most are blocked by debris.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Cairns
- A collection of six weathered stone cairns marking what locals believe to be the burial sites of ancient hill tribes.
- The cairns are frequently disturbed by strong winds, creating a constant, mournful whistling sound – hence the name.
- Small, intricately carved bone pendants have been occasionally found near the cairns, hinting at a possible religious significance.

## Additional POI 2: The Obsidian Scar
- A roughly circular patch of blackened earth, approximately 30 feet in diameter, devoid of all vegetation. 
- Strange, unsettling geometric patterns can be faintly discerned within the black soil.
- Locals report a feeling of intense cold and dread whenever they approach the Scar, and livestock avoid it entirely.
