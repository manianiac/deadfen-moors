# Tangle of the Shifting Spirit

**Hex Label:** K.11
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: Tangle of the Shifting Spirit
- The core of the hex is dominated by an unnaturally dense and ancient forest, known locally as the “Whisperwood.” Trees here are impossibly tall, their branches intertwined to create a perpetual twilight, and exhibit subtle, shifting patterns – as if subtly altering their forms.
- Within a clearing at the hex’s center lies the Spirit Pool – a stagnant, obsidian-black pool perpetually shrouded in a swirling mist. The mist is said to be the physical manifestation of restless spirits trapped within the forest, and those who gaze into the pool report unsettling visions.
- Scattered around the pool are crumbling, moss-covered stone altars dedicated to forgotten nature deities. Strange, intricately carved wooden masks, perpetually damp and smelling of pine and decay, are placed upon the altars. 

## Additional POI 1: Bracklen’s Watch
- A dilapidated watchtower, constructed of grey stone, offering a partial view of the western forest edge. 
- Bracklen, a hermit known for tracking unusual movements in the woods, maintains a small, stubbornly maintained garden nearby.
- The tower contains remnants of a failed, long-abandoned attempt to ward off the forest’s spirit activity.

## Additional POI 2: Rootbound Glade
- A small, circular glade where the trees grow in a tight, almost geometric pattern, creating a claustrophobic space.
- A network of unusually large, bioluminescent mushrooms thrives in the glade, casting an eerie green light.
- Local legends whisper of a powerful, trapped spirit dwelling within the root system of the trees.
