# The White Sould

**Hex Label:** [[The White Sould|I.14]]
**Terrain Type:** Plains (with scrub)


## Main POI: The White Sould
- [[The White Sould]] is a crumbling, circular stone tower, approximately 60 feet in diameter and 40 feet tall, constructed from a pale, almost luminous limestone. The structure is heavily weathered, covered in creeping vines and lichen, and perpetually shrouded in a thin, unnatural mist. Local legend claims [[The Tower|the tower]] was built by a reclusive order of scholars obsessed with capturing and studying souls.
- Within [[The Tower|the tower]]'s central chamber, a single, large basin carved from the same stone remains filled with a viscous, opalescent liquid – the ‘Sould’. Touching the liquid induces vivid, unsettling visions and a profound sense of melancholy. The air within is noticeably colder than the surrounding plains.
- Scattered around the base of [[The Tower|the tower]] are the remains of a camp – charred timbers, rusted tools, and fragmented pottery – suggesting a recent, hasty abandonment. A narrow, overgrown track leads away from [[The Tower|the tower]], disappearing into the scrubland.


## Additional POI 1: Stone Creek Crossing
- A natural ford across Stone Creek, marked by a precariously balanced collection of large, moss-covered stones.
- Frequent sightings of deer and wild boar in the area.
- Known to be a favored resting spot for traveling merchants.

## Additional POI 2: Whisperwind Ridge
- A low ridge of wind-sculpted sandstone offering a panoramic view of the surrounding plains.
- Contains a small, naturally formed cave used by local hunters for shelter.
- The wind constantly whistles through the ridge, carrying faint, seemingly unintelligible whispers.
