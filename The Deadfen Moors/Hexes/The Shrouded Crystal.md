# The Shrouded Crystal

**Hex Label:** L.13
**Terrain Type:** Plains, River


## Main POI: The Shrouded Crystal
- The Shrouded Crystal is a naturally formed quartz geode, approximately 20 feet in diameter, embedded within a shallow, perpetually mist-filled basin. The crystal itself pulses with a faint, internal luminescence, shifting between shades of indigo and violet.
- The basin surrounding the geode is choked with iridescent moss and strange, bioluminescent fungi, nourished by the slow-moving river that flows through L.13.  Local legends claim the mist is a solidified echo of emotions felt near the Crystal.
- The riverbank contains several crude, stone-built altars covered in faded glyphs, suggesting long-forgotten rituals performed to appease or understand the Crystal’s power.  Scattered around the altars are remnants of bone and polished stones.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Farmstead
- A dilapidated farmhouse sits approximately 1 mile north of the Crystal, consisting of a single-room structure and a crumbling barn. Livestock are absent.
- The surrounding fields are cultivated with a strange, silver-leaved grain, rumored to possess mild hallucinogenic properties when consumed.
- A weathered weather vane, depicting a stylized raven, dominates the roof of the farmhouse.

## Additional POI 2: River’s Bend Watchtower
- The Watchtower is a partially collapsed stone tower built into the riverbank, approximately 3 miles east of the Crystal.
- The tower’s remaining walls are scarred with the marks of numerous battles, suggesting a long history of territorial disputes.
- A small, brackish pool sits at the base of the tower, attracting scavenging birds and insects.
