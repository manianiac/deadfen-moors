# The Temple

**Hex Label:** I.8
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Temple
- [[The Temple]] is a crumbling sandstone structure, approximately 80 feet in height, dominated by a central spire eroded by centuries of wind and sand. Original architectural features suggest a connection to a forgotten sun-worshiping cult.
- Within [[The Temple|the temple]]’s main chamber, a circular dais holds a partially buried obsidian altar, stained with what appears to be dried blood. Strange geometric carvings cover the walls, emitting a faint, unsettling warmth.
- The surrounding plaza is choked with scrub vegetation and scattered fragments of pottery, hinting at a once-thriving settlement that appears to have been abruptly abandoned. A network of shallow, interconnected cisterns lie beneath the plaza floor.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Ridge
- A natural ridge of wind-sculpted sandstone approximately 1 mile east of [[The Temple|the Temple]], offering a vantage point over the surrounding plains.
- Frequent, inexplicable whispers are reported to emanate from the ridge, often described as fragmented pleas or warnings.
- Small, hardy desert fox populations inhabit the ridge, exhibiting unusual intelligence and a seemingly coordinated behavior.

## Additional POI 2: Dust Devil’s Scar
- A deep, linear chasm cutting across the hex’s eastern border, formed by a long-dormant but potentially unstable dust devil.
- The scar reveals strata of unusually colorful rock layers – bands of ochre, crimson, and sapphire – suggesting a significant geological event.
- Several skeletal remains, mostly of small, canine-like creatures, are scattered along the chasm’s edges.
