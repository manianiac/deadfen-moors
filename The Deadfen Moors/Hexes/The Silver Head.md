# The Silver Head

**Hex Label:** E.3
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Silver Head
- The Silver Head is a weathered, partially collapsed stone structure resembling a human skull, approximately 30 feet tall and adorned with intricate silver filigree – the source of its name.  Rainwater perpetually trickles down its eye sockets and from the gaps in the crumbling roof.
-  A small, stagnant pool, known as the ‘Silent Mire’, lies at the base of the skull, fed by subterranean springs. The water is unnaturally dark and reflects the silver of the structure, often creating a disconcerting optical illusion.
-  Local folklore claims the structure was built by a long-dead druid king, guarding a powerful artifact – a shard of a fallen star – said to grant visions and control over the forest's growth.  Recent signs suggest a renewed interest in the site.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Grove
- A densely packed grove of ancient willow trees, constantly rustling even in still air, rumored to carry whispers of forgotten voices.
-  Several small, circular clearings exist within the grove, often containing remnants of ritual circles – mostly faded chalk markings and scattered stones.
-  The grove is home to a particularly large and intelligent population of badgers, fiercely protective of their territory.

## Additional POI 2: Blackwood Creek
- Blackwood Creek flows northward from the Silent Mire, its water unusually cold and black, purportedly stained by minerals leaching from the surrounding stone.
-  The creek is known for its frequent, deceptively strong currents, making navigation hazardous.
-  Several submerged logjams are located along the creek bed, offering potential shelter for aquatic creatures.
