# The Grove of Fire

**Hex Label:** L.4
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Grove of Fire
- The Grove itself is a naturally formed amphitheater of obsidian rock, approximately 100 feet in diameter, perpetually wreathed in a thin, shimmering heat haze. Geothermal vents punctuate the rock face, emitting intermittent bursts of flame – ranging from small, contained embers to geysers of molten rock that reach 20 feet high. The flames are strangely cool to the touch, radiating an intense blue light.
- Around the periphery of the Grove, the surrounding forest is unnaturally stunted and blackened, scorched by the heat and volcanic gases. Twisted, skeletal trees – mostly ash-oak and thorn-pine – cling to life, exhibiting bioluminescent mosses in shades of crimson and violet.
- A small, crystalline lake, known as the ‘Heartfire Pool’, sits at the very center of the Grove. The water is intensely hot and faintly iridescent, said to possess minor healing properties but also carries a risk of severe burns if approached carelessly. 

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Cairns
- A collection of weathered stone cairns marking the burial sites of a forgotten tribe, the Whisperwind Clan, who worshipped the Grove’s flames.
- Ritualistic markings, depicting stylized flames and serpentine figures, are etched into the stones. 
- Locals claim the cairns emit faint whispers on still nights, speaking in a language unknown.

## Additional POI 2: The Obsidian Scar
- A deep fissure, approximately 50 feet wide and 80 feet deep, cutting through the western edge of the forest. The rock faces are slick with obsidian and steam.
- The Scar is a known source of unusual mineral deposits - primarily shimmering, heat-resistant obsidian shards. 
- Animal tracks, belonging to a species of fire-resistant lizard, are frequently observed near the Scar’s entrance.
