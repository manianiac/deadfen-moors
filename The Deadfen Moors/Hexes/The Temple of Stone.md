# The Temple of Stone

**Hex Label:** I.15
**Terrain Type:** Mountains


## Main POI: The Temple of Stone
- The Temple of Stone is a cyclopean structure carved directly into the mountainside, primarily basalt and granite. Its architecture is characterized by immense, undecorated blocks fitted together with impossible precision, suggesting an unknown, immensely skilled civilization.
- The central chamber contains a massive, obsidian altar etched with spiraling glyphs that subtly shift in appearance when observed directly.  A persistent, low hum emanates from the altar, inducing feelings of unease and disorientation in those who linger too long.
-  A network of narrow, winding tunnels extends outwards from the main chamber, some collapsing after a short distance, others leading to increasingly complex chambers adorned with surprisingly well-preserved murals depicting stylized humanoid figures engaged in ritualistic acts. 

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Peak
- A perpetually windy crag overlooking the temple’s southern approach, rumored to carry fragmented voices of the temple’s long-dead priests.
- Contains a small, sheltered cave system providing minimal shelter from the elements, frequented by scavenging rock drakes.
- Marked by a solitary, weathered cairn constructed from obsidian fragments.

## Additional POI 2: The Scarred Basin
- A naturally formed basin at the hex’s eastern edge, filled with a stagnant, mineral-rich pool exhibiting a perpetual oily sheen.
-  Local fauna, primarily mutated amphibians and insects, thrive within the basin due to the unusual water chemistry.
-  Shows signs of ancient, massive fires, with blackened stone and partially melted metal scattered across the immediate area.
