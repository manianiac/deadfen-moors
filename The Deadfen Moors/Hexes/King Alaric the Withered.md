# King Alaric the Withered

**Hex Label:** L.10
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: King Alaric the Withered
- The central feature is a vast, cracked plain dominated by petrified trees, their branches contorted into grotesque shapes, perpetually coated in a grey, ashen dust. This dust is the source of Alaric's decay and radiates a palpable sense of sorrow and despair.
- At the heart of the plain stands the Withered Citadel, a once-magnificent structure now crumbling and overgrown with phosphorescent moss. The walls are etched with faded murals depicting Alaric’s reign – scenes of prosperity quickly dissolving into images of war and ruin.
- The air around the Citadel shimmers with a faint, sickly green light, intensifying near fissures in the ground. These fissures are said to be conduits for Alaric’s lingering essence, and touching them accelerates personal decay.



## Additional POI 1: The Silent Watchers
- Scattered across the plains are twelve monolithic stone statues, depicting humanoid figures perpetually gazing towards the Citadel. They are unnervingly silent and seem to shift subtly in the periphery of vision.
- These statues are believed to be the remnants of Alaric’s loyal guard, frozen in time and bound to vigilantly observe the Citadel.
- Touching a statue induces a brief, overwhelming sense of dread and the fading of memories.

## Additional POI 2: The Echoing Well
- Located on the eastern edge of the hex, the Echoing Well is a deep, circular depression filled with dark, viscous water.
- The water reflects not the sky, but fragmented images of Alaric’s past – fleeting glimpses of battles, feasts, and his final moments.
-  Whispers emanate from the well, repeating fragments of Alaric’s anguished lament, said to drive those who listen for too long to madness.
