# The Grove of the Spirit

**Hex Label:** D.10
**Terrain Type:** Swamp


## Main POI: The Grove of the Spirit
- The Grove itself is a circular clearing, approximately 200 feet in diameter, dominated by ancient, gnarled cypress trees draped with phosphorescent moss. The air within the grove is perpetually humid and carries a faint, sweet fragrance reminiscent of decaying orchids and ozone.
- A standing stone circle, weathered and covered in intricate carvings depicting stylized spirits and serpentine figures, occupies the center of the grove. Locals whisper that the stone circle pulses with a faint, rhythmic energy when the moon is full.
- Deep within the grove’s interior, a perpetually steaming pool – known as the ‘Mirror of Whispers’ – reflects distorted images of those who gaze into it. Strange murmurs and half-formed voices are frequently reported emanating from the pool’s surface.

## Additional POI 1: Brackwater’s Mire
- A labyrinthine network of peat bogs and stagnant pools surrounds the Grove, choked with carnivorous plants and venomous insects.
- Scattered amongst the mire are the crumbling foundations of what were once small, isolated homesteads, abandoned centuries ago.
- Dangerous currents and hidden sinkholes make navigation through Brackwater’s Mire extremely perilous.

## Additional POI 2: Serpent’s Tooth
- A solitary, jagged rock spire rises abruptly from the swamp’s edge, resembling a colossal serpent’s fang.
- The base of Serpent’s Tooth is slick with algae and frequently occupied by a large, territorial crocodile known locally as “Scalebreaker”.
- A small cave entrance, partially concealed by hanging vines, is located on the north face of the spire – rumored to contain a cache of ancient, forgotten artifacts.
