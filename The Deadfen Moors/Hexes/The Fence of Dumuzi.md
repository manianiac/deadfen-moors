# The Fence of Dumuzi

**Hex Label:** G.14
**Terrain Type:** Plains (with scrub)


## Main POI: The Fence of Dumuzi
- The Fence of Dumuzi is a colossal, obsidian structure, approximately 100 feet tall and 500 feet long, stretching across the otherwise unremarkable plains. It’s constructed from a material seemingly impervious to damage, exhibiting a faint, internal luminescence.
- The Fence is segmented into ten distinct sections, each marked by weathered, Nabu-scripted glyphs detailing cycles of decay and rebirth. These glyphs pulse faintly with a sickly green light.
- A perpetual, chilling mist clings to the base of the Fence, carrying a faint, mournful hum. Within the mist, spectral images of children – all bearing a chilling resemblance to Dumuzi – flicker intermittently.



## Additional POI 1: Grove of Dumuzi
- Located 2 miles southeast of the Fence, the Grove is a circular clearing dominated by ancient, twisted cypress trees. The soil here is unnaturally fertile, and the air hangs heavy with the scent of jasmine and decay.
- The trees are unnaturally tall, reaching heights of over 100 feet, and their roots form a tangled labyrinth beneath the forest floor. Small, perfectly preserved dolls crafted from what appears to be solidified amber are scattered amongst the roots.
- A perpetual twilight filters through the canopy, and the sounds of children’s laughter – distorted and unsettling – can occasionally be heard within the Grove.



## Additional POI 2: Rock of the Dead Dumuzi
- Situated 4 miles northeast of the Fence, the Rock of the Dead Dumuzi is a solitary, monolithic boulder, roughly 30 feet in height and 15 feet in diameter. 
- Etched into the rock’s surface are countless, faded images depicting Dumuzi in various states of suffering and decay, accompanied by more Nabu glyphs. 
- A localized zone of intense cold surrounds the rock, and the air is thick with a palpable sense of sorrow and dread. The ground beneath the rock is covered in a thin layer of grey ash.
