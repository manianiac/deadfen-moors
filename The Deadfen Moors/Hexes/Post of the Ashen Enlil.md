# Post of the Ashen Enlil

**Hex Label:** F.4
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: Post of the Ashen Enlil
- The Post sits within a perpetually twilight section of the Blackwood Forest, dominated by ancient, gnarled oaks choked with grey moss. It’s a crumbling stone structure, largely collapsed, with only the skeletal remains of a watchtower remaining.
- The air around the Post is noticeably colder, carrying the scent of damp stone and a faint, metallic tang. Residual magical energy clings to the site, causing intermittent flickering of shadow and whispering sounds.
- A shallow, circular depression in the ground at the center of the Post is filled with ash – specifically, the grey ash that constantly drifts from the surrounding trees, perpetually coating everything in a thin layer.

## Additional POI 1: The Whispering Cairns
- A collection of three moss-covered cairns marks a subtle trail leading westward from the Post’s entrance.
- Local legends claim the cairns were built by Enlil’s troops, each stone bearing a faint impression of his face.
- The wind consistently howls through the cairns, producing indistinct, sorrowful whispers that seem to shift with the listener's thoughts.

## Additional POI 2: Twisted Root
- A massive, contorted oak, far older than any other in the Blackwood, dominates the northern edge of the hex. Its roots form a cavernous space.
- Within the root-cave, investigators find numerous discarded weapons and armor – remnants of Enlil’s last stand, infused with residual Scourge energy.
- The roots of Twisted Root seem to pulse with a faint, grey light, attracting small, shadowy insects.
