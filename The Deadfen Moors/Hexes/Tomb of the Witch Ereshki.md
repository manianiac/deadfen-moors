# Tomb of the Witch Ereshki

**Hex Label:** I.9
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: Tomb of the Witch Ereshki
- The Tomb sits at the apex of [[Ereshki’s Rise]], a crumbling ziggurat constructed from obsidian and grey stone. Centuries of wind and rain have eroded much of the structure, leaving only the central chamber largely intact.
- Within the central chamber, a raised stone dais supports a massive, cracked obsidian altar stained with an unidentifiable, dark residue.  Scattered around the altar are the skeletal remains of what appear to be ritual sacrifices – mostly humanoid figures, though some show signs of monstrous alteration.
- A palpable aura of cold and despair radiates from the tomb, intensified by the constant, whispering wind that snakes through the surrounding plains.  The air carries a faint scent of ozone and decay.

## Additional POI 1: The Road of Ereshki
- A narrow, rutted track, little more than a game trail, winds southward from the Tomb, leading towards the distant coastline. The road is choked with withered vegetation and broken stones, suggesting infrequent travel.
- Locals whisper that the road is haunted by the restless spirits of those sacrificed to [[Ereshki]], and that the track itself shifts slightly overnight, leading travelers astray.
- Scattered along the Road are the remnants of crude, stone markers – fragmented symbols that may have once represented warding rituals, now largely worn away by the elements.

## Additional POI 2: Beach of Ereshki
- The beach itself is black sand, composed of finely ground volcanic glass, stretching for approximately one mile before giving way to a rocky outcrop. 
-  A strange, iridescent seaweed clings to the rocks, pulsing faintly with an unnatural light. Exposure to the seaweed causes vivid, disturbing hallucinations in most individuals.
-  Wreckage of several long-forgotten vessels is visible along the shore, partially buried in the black sand – testament to the dangers of the surrounding waters and, according to legend, the effects of [[Ereshki]]’s magic.
