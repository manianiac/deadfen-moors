# The Shivering Hill

**Hex Label:** L.5
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Shivering Hill
- [[The Shivering Hill]] is a prominent, windswept granite outcrop rising 300 feet above the surrounding forest floor. Constant, localized cold air currents emanating from fissures in the rock create a perpetual, unsettling chill – hence the name. 
- At the base of the hill lies a small, collapsed stone circle, partially overgrown with moss and lichen. Fragments of intricately carved bone and polished shale are scattered amongst the stones. 
- A network of shallow, icy caves snakes through the lower slopes, exhibiting unusual crystalline formations that pulse faintly with a blue light during periods of heightened wind.


## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Glade
- A secluded glade known for unnervingly consistent, rustling sounds, even when no breeze exists. 
- Home to a colony of nocturnal, iridescent moths drawn to the crystalline formations within the caves.
- Patches of phosphorescent fungi illuminate the glade after dark, casting an eerie glow.

## Additional POI 2: Old Man Thorne’s Watch
- A crumbling, partially submerged watchtower built into the hillside’s edge, likely dating back to a forgotten logging operation. 
- [[The Tower|The tower]]'s upper levels are accessible via a precarious, rotting staircase leading directly into a swirling mist bank. 
- Contains the skeletal remains of a solitary figure, clad in worn leather, clutching a rusted hunting horn.
