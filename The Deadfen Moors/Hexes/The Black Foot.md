# The Black Foot

**Hex Label:** K.4
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Black Foot
- The Black Foot is a partially collapsed stone watchtower, overgrown with black moss and clinging vines, situated on a slight rise overlooking the western edge of the hex. Initial construction appears to be ancient, possibly predating the current dominant human settlements, with rough-hewn blocks and a severely damaged, spiral-shaped stone door.
- Scattered around the tower’s base are the skeletal remains of what appear to be hunting dogs, frozen in various postures, suggesting a violent and sudden end. The ground is stained dark with a substance that resists identification but exhibits an unnatural sheen.
- A narrow, overgrown path leads away from the tower, disappearing into the deeper forest. Locals whisper of a "shifting shadow" seen moving along this path, and a persistent, low humming sound emanates from the tower on still nights.

## Additional POI 1: Whispering Falls
- A small, secluded waterfall cascades into a dark pool surrounded by ancient pines. The water is unusually cold and emits a faint, rustling sound even when there is no breeze.
- Several crudely carved stones depicting stylized wolves are found near the base of the falls, suggesting a connection to the local folklore.
- The pool is rumored to hold restorative properties, though prolonged exposure causes vivid, unsettling dreams.

## Additional POI 2: Old Logger’s Camp
- The ruins of a small logging camp, consisting of a collapsed cabin and a rusting wagon wheel, lie approximately one mile to the south of The Black Foot.
- A collection of well-preserved axes and saws are scattered amongst the debris, indicating a sudden abandonment. Animal tracks, primarily boar and large feline, are frequently observed in the area.
- A shallow depression in the ground holds a tarnished silver locket containing a miniature portrait of a young woman, its origins unknown.
