# Pit of the Clouded Gold

**Hex Label:** D.4
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: Pit of the Clouded Gold
- The Pit of the Clouded Gold is a naturally formed sinkhole, approximately 100 feet deep and 80 feet in diameter, situated within a shallow depression in the plains. The walls of the pit are slick with perpetually damp clay and covered in phosphorescent moss, giving the area an eerie, clouded glow, particularly at night.
- Archaeological excavations within the pit have uncovered the ruins of a once-grand dwarven outpost, “Grimstone Hold,” dating back nearly 800 years. The remains consist of crumbling barracks, a collapsed forge, and several partially excavated chambers filled with rusted tools and the skeletal remains of miners.
- Strange, swirling mists frequently rise from the bottom of the pit, obscuring the depths and occasionally carrying the faint sound of chanting. Locals whisper that these mists are the remnants of the dwarves' failed attempts to harness a vein of magically-infused gold, now guarded by restless spirits.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Ridge
- A long, exposed ridge of shale rises abruptly from the plains, offering a commanding view of the surrounding area.
- Scattered amongst the shale are the petrified remains of ancient, gigantic reptiles – a testament to a bygone era.
- Frequent, unnervingly quiet winds whistle through the ridge, leading to the local legend of trapped souls.

## Additional POI 2: The Broken Cart Track
- A partially collapsed and overgrown cart track, likely used for transporting stone from the nearby quarry, cuts diagonally across the hex.
- The track is littered with the rusted remains of wagons and the skeletal remains of horses, suggesting a violent end.
- A small, stagnant pool of dark water sits at the intersection of the track, rumored to be a site of spectral sightings.
