# Ereshki’s Rise

**Hex Label:** H.9
**Terrain Type:** Plains (with scrub)


## Main POI: Ereshki’s Rise
- The hex center is dominated by a steep, windswept rise of reddish-brown earth, approximately 150 feet high. The scrub is sparse, primarily thorny bushes and stunted pines clinging to the lower slopes.
- At the summit sits a crumbling, circular stone structure – the ‘Rise’ itself – partially overgrown with moss and lichen. Runes etched into the stone glow faintly with a sickly green light, particularly noticeable during periods of heightened magical activity. 
- The air around the Rise carries a faint, metallic tang, and whispers carried on the wind often seem to murmur Ereshki’s name. Scattered around the base of the Rise are the bleached bones of numerous animals and, unsettlingly, humanoid figures.


## Additional POI 1: The Road of Ereshki
- A narrow, almost overgrown track winds its way along the eastern edge of the hex, leading towards A.7 – the Beach of Ereshki. The road is rarely used and shows significant signs of age and disrepair.
- The road is marked by standing stones, each roughly hewn and covered in faded carvings depicting swirling patterns and monstrous forms. These are believed to be wards laid down by Ereshki.
- Tracks indicate sporadic, recent movement, likely scavengers or those seeking knowledge of the Witch.



## Additional POI 2: Tomb of the Witch Ereshki
- Located approximately one mile southeast of the Rise, partially concealed by a dense thicket of thorny brambles, is a collapsed mausoleum constructed from dark, weathered stone.
- The interior is largely destroyed, filled with rubble and the remains of what were once intricate murals depicting scenes of elven history, now obscured by grime and decay.
- A single, unbroken obsidian dagger lies on a pedestal at the center of the chamber; its edge retains a subtle, unsettling chill.
