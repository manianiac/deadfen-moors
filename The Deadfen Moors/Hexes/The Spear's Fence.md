# The Spear's Fence

**Hex Label:** [[The Spear's Fence|C.12]]
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Spear's Fence
- [[The Spear's Fence]] is a remarkably well-preserved, 12-foot-high palisade constructed of dark grey, tightly fitted stone. It stretches for approximately one mile along the western edge of the hex, abruptly ending at a rise in the plains. No gate or obvious entrance exists.
- Scattered within the shadow of the fence are the rusted remains of carts and wagons, suggesting a long-abandoned attempt to traverse [[The Barrier|the barrier]].  The ground within a 30-foot radius of the fence is perpetually cold to the touch and subtly vibrates.
-  Local legends claim the fence was erected by a warrior-king obsessed with preventing the return of a monstrous, horned beast.  Carved into several of the fence posts are stylized depictions of this beast, rendered with unsettling detail.



## Additional POI 1: Dustwind Creek
- A seasonal creek, usually dry, flows intermittently through the south-eastern corner of the hex, depositing fine, red dust.
- The creek bed is lined with fossilized remains of small, reptilian creatures.
-  A solitary, weathered cairn stands near the creek's source, marked by a single, polished obsidian shard.



## Additional POI 2: Broken Wheelbarrow
- Located approximately one mile east of the Spear’s Fence, lies the skeletal remains of a wheelbarrow, overturned and embedded in the soft earth.
- Traces of a dark, viscous substance cling to the wheel and frame, identified by local [[The Copper-Grubbers|scavengers]] as ‘shadow-oil’.
-  A single, tarnished silver coin rests beside the wheelbarrow, displaying the image of a falcon.
