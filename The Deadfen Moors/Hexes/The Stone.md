# The Stone

**Hex Label:** E.9
**Terrain Type:** Swamp


## Main POI: The Stone
- [[The Stone]] is a monolithic, obsidian-like structure rising approximately 60 feet from the swamp floor, partially submerged in stagnant, brackish water. Its surface is covered in intricate, moss-covered carvings depicting serpentine figures and impossible geometric patterns.
- A perpetual, unnerving stillness surrounds [[The Stone|the Stone]], disrupting natural sounds and creating a localized dampness. Strange, bioluminescent fungi grow exclusively in the shadows beneath its base.
- The local tribes, the Murkfolk, believe [[The Stone|the Stone]] is a dormant god, and perform nightly rituals involving chanting and the scattering of polished river stones at its foot. 

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Mire
- A network of interconnected, flooded cypress groves, known for the eerie whistling sounds created by the wind rushing through the trees.
- Frequent sightings of the Gray Heron, a large, territorial bird rumored to possess unnatural intelligence.
- Contains the skeletal remains of a crashed airship, largely submerged and overgrown with algae.

## Additional POI 2: Blackroot Spring
- A small, consistently warm spring issuing dark, mineral-rich water. The water is said to have minor healing properties.
- Thick, gnarled Blackroot trees, named for their exceptionally dark, almost black bark, thrive around [[The Spring|the spring]], creating a dense, claustrophobic area.
- Patches of iridescent slime mold cling to the roots and rocks, exhibiting a mild neurotoxic effect upon contact.
