# The Tangle

**Hex Label:** B.5
**Terrain Type:** Hills


## Main POI: The Tangle
- [[The Tangle]] is a perpetually shifting forest of thornbushes and twisted trees, perpetually shrouded in a thick, iridescent mist. The ground is perpetually damp and covered in slick, moss-like growths.
- At the heart of [[The Tangle|the Tangle]] stands a cyclopean stone structure – the Weaver’s Spire – constructed from an unknown, obsidian-like material. Strange, harmonic vibrations emanate from [[The Spire|the Spire]], occasionally causing disorientation and vivid hallucinations.
- Within the deepest section of [[The Tangle|the Tangle]], a collection of shimmering, trapped pockets of time – known as “Echo Wells” – are rumored to exist. These wells sporadically release spectral echoes of past events, often violent and unsettling.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Cairns
- Three weathered stone cairns mark the ancient boundary of a forgotten tribe, said to have worshipped the mist itself.
- The cairns are perpetually coated in a thin layer of phosphorescent moss, reacting to shifts in the mist’s intensity.
- Local legends claim the cairns amplify voices, often carrying whispers of warning or madness.

## Additional POI 2: Brackwater’s Ruin
- Brackwater’s Ruin is the crumbling remains of a small, fortified watchtower built into the hillside. 
- [[The Tower|The tower]] is partially submerged in a brackish, perpetually murky pool – a result of [[The Tangle|the Tangle]]’s influence. 
- Scavenged equipment and the skeletal remains of a dozen guards hint at a sudden and brutal abandonment.
