# Crater of the Doomed Hope

**Hex Label:** I.5
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: Crater of the Doomed Hope
- The crater itself is approximately 1.2 miles in diameter and 150 feet deep, formed by an ancient, cataclysmic energy release. The walls are composed of layered, obsidian-like rock striated with veins of pulsating, pale green energy.
- Scattered around the crater’s rim are the skeletal remains of what appear to be massive, bird-like creatures – the "Hopewings" – fused with the rock in places, suggesting a violent, final struggle.
- A perpetual, localized storm of dust and static electricity dominates the crater’s center, capable of inflicting burns and disrupting electronic devices within a 50-foot radius.  Strange, crystalline formations, seemingly grown from the energy, occasionally erupt from the ground.


## Additional POI 1: Whispering Cairns
- A collection of weathered stone cairns marking ancient burial sites, the stones are consistently warm to the touch.
- Locals claim the cairns murmur with the voices of the Hopewings, offering cryptic warnings and prophecies.
-  Scattered amongst the cairns are numerous, perfectly polished obsidian shards – believed to be fragments of the original energy discharge.

## Additional POI 2:  The Rustwind Farmstead
- A partially collapsed farmhouse built primarily of grey sandstone, now overgrown with resilient, metallic-sheened vines.
- The farmstead’s well yields water tainted with a metallic tang and a faintly luminescent sheen.
- Several rusted, humanoid automatons – deactivated and partially buried in the fields – lie scattered, bearing the markings of the Doomed Hope’s initial impact.
