# The Clouded Heart

**Hex Label:** E.12
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Clouded Heart
- The Clouded Heart is a colossal, petrified tree, approximately 150 feet tall, dominating the center of the hex. Its bark is a dull grey, perpetually slick with a strange, iridescent mist – the source of its name. The mist obscures details and induces a subtle feeling of melancholy in those who linger too long.
- The base of the tree is riddled with caves and tunnels, carved out by generations of subterranean insects and now partially collapsed. These tunnels lead into a network of echoing chambers, some containing bizarre crystalline formations that pulse with a faint, internal light.
- A small, ruined shrine dedicated to a forgotten deity of grief and remembrance stands at the tree’s root.  Broken stone idols depicting weeping figures are scattered around, and a shallow pool of stagnant water reflects the tree’s grey form. 

## Additional POI 1: Whispering Grass
- Dense, unusually tall grasses grow in a circular pattern around the southern edge of the hex, said to murmur forgotten names when the wind passes through.
- The grass is home to a colony of shimmering, moth-like insects that feed on the mist emanating from the tree.
- Tracks indicate frequent, but rarely observed, nomadic hunter-gatherers utilize this area for seasonal sustenance.

## Additional POI 2: Stonefall Ridge
- A jagged ridge of weathered sandstone forms the western border of the hex, marked by a collapsed quarry and numerous unstable rock formations.
- Fragments of ancient, grey stone have been found embedded with veins of a dark, metallic ore - rumored to hold potent magical properties.
- A solitary, weathered cairn stands near the ridge’s peak, bearing the faded symbol of a stylized heart.
