# Tree of Snagrub

**Hex Label:** F.2
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: Tree of Snagrub
- [[The Tree]] of [[Snagrub]] is a colossal, ancient ironwood tree, twisted and scarred by repeated impacts. Its trunk is riddled with jagged metal protrusions – remnants of [[Snagrub]]’s erratic movements and the goblin’s attempts to modify [[The Tree|the tree]] for defensive purposes.
- At the base of [[The Tree|the tree]] lies a shallow crater, approximately 30 feet in diameter, perpetually filled with a viscous, shimmering green fluid – likely a byproduct of the anti-gravity tech’s malfunction. The air around the crater smells faintly of ozone and burnt metal.
- Surrounding [[The Tree|the tree]]’s roots are the skeletal remains of several smaller goblins, frozen in poses of terror, and scattered tools – rusty wrenches, sparking wires, and fragments of [[Anu|the Nabu]] tech.



## Additional POI 1: Snagrub the Floating
- A perpetually rotating platform, roughly 20 feet in diameter, suspended 30 feet above the forest floor, constructed from salvaged [[Nabu]] components and goblin-forged metal. 
- The platform is dominated by a single, constantly rotating, crystalline lens – believed to be the focal point of [[Snagrub]]’s anti-gravity field.  It emits a low, humming drone.
- Crude, glyph-covered warnings are etched into the platform’s surface, primarily in a dialect of Goblin.



## Additional POI 2: The Jagged Snagrub
-  A heavily fortified, roughly humanoid structure built directly into the side of a rocky outcrop. Constructed primarily of salvaged [[Nabu]] technology and reinforced ironwood, it presents a formidable, if erratic, defensive perimeter.
-  The structure emits a constant, fluctuating field of low-level anti-gravity, causing small objects and loose stones to drift upwards with unpredictable trajectories.
-  Scattered around the base of [[The Jagged Snagrub|the Jagged Snagrub]] are piles of discarded goblin weaponry—primarily crudely fashioned spears and axes—suggesting frequent, unsuccessful assaults.
