# The Silver's Ring

**Hex Label:** K.5
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Silver's Ring
- [[The Silver's Ring]] is a naturally formed amphitheater carved into a dense grove of silver birch trees. [[The Stone|The stone]] seating, worn smooth by centuries of weather and, purportedly, ritualistic use, circles a central clearing dominated by a perpetually shimmering pool of water known as the ‘Stillheart’.
- The Stillheart is unusually cold to the touch and exhibits a faint, rhythmic pulsing light visible only in dim conditions. Local legends claim the pool reflects not only the surrounding forest but also glimpses of possible futures.
- A crumbling stone shrine, dedicated to a forgotten nature deity named Sylvana, stands partially submerged at the edge of the Stillheart. Its remaining carvings depict stylized wolves, trees, and flowing water, and a single silver ring – significantly smaller than the amphitheater’s – rests within.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Glade
- Located approximately 1 mile north of [[The Ring|the Ring]], Whisperwind Glade is a perpetually misty hollow known for unusually strong breezes that carry faint, unintelligible whispers.
- The glade is home to a colony of iridescent moss and several species of unusually vocal birds.
- Tracks of large, unknown creatures have been frequently observed leading into the glade.

## Additional POI 2: Old Man Hemlock’s Clearing
- Situated 3 miles east of [[The Ring|the Ring]], this small clearing is dominated by a massive, ancient hemlock tree – Old Man Hemlock – which is rumored to be at least 800 years old.
- Hemlock is a recluse, a skilled herbalist, and keeper of local lore, known for his gruff demeanor and potent remedies.
- A network of shallow, moss-covered tunnels extends beneath the roots of Old Man Hemlock, leading to a subterranean spring.
