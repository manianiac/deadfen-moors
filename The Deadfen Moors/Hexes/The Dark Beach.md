# The Dark Beach

**Hex Label:** C.5
**Terrain Type:** Hills


## Main POI: The Dark Beach
- [[The Dark Beach]] is a crescent-shaped stretch of black sand, perpetually shrouded in a fine, grey mist that limits visibility to approximately 50 feet. The sand itself is remarkably smooth and cold to the touch, composed primarily of basalt eroded from the surrounding hills.
- At the northern end of the beach lies the skeletal remains of a long-shipwrecked trading vessel, the *Serpent’s Kiss*, half-buried in the sand. Salvagers occasionally attempt to retrieve artifacts, though the area is known for unsettling phenomena and aggressive seabirds.
- A crumbling stone circle, weathered and partially submerged during high tides, stands near the beach's center. Local legends claim it’s a site of ancient rituals connected to the sea, though scholars debate the origins of the structure.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Cairns
- A cluster of fifteen standing stones, erected by a nomadic tribe known as the Whisperwind, marking a significant migratory route.
- The stones emit a low, resonant hum audible only in still conditions – said to carry warnings of approaching storms.
- Scattered around the cairns are remnants of the tribe’s camp, including fragmented pottery and worn leatherwork.

## Additional POI 2: Blackrock Outcrop
- The Blackrock Outcrop is a sheer cliff face of dark basalt, providing a vantage point overlooking [[The Dark Beach|the Dark Beach]] and the surrounding hills.
- Numerous nesting seabirds, including the predatory Shadowhawk, make the outcrop their primary territory.
- Evidence of old mining operations exists – shallow shafts and rusted tools suggest attempts to extract valuable minerals.
