# The Ruin of the Dagger

**Hex Label:** E.8
**Terrain Type:** Hills


## Main POI: The Ruin of the Dagger
- The central feature is a collapsed ziggurat constructed of grey sandstone, partially buried in a shallow, overgrown valley. Erosion has sculpted bizarre, dagger-like formations into the lower walls, giving the ruin its name. Significant structural damage indicates a violent collapse, likely tectonic in origin.
- Scattered around the base of the ziggurat are the remnants of what appear to be workshops and living quarters – crumbling stone foundations, fractured pottery shards, and the occasional corroded metal tool. Traces of a dark, oily residue cling to some surfaces.
- A central chamber within the ziggurat contains a large, circular depression filled with a viscous, black liquid. The air around the depression is noticeably colder and carries a faint, metallic scent. Strange, bioluminescent fungi grow in the damp corners.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Glade
- A small, perpetually misty clearing dominated by ancient, wind-sculpted willow trees. Locals claim the trees whisper secrets on the breeze.
- Evidence of small-scale hunting camps – sharpened stakes, animal bones – suggests this area was used for seasonal gatherings.
- Thick, thorny undergrowth makes passage difficult and provides concealment.

## Additional POI 2: Stone Tooth Quarry
- A depleted limestone quarry featuring towering stacks of weathered blocks, hinting at a long period of operation.
- Several partially submerged channels suggest the quarry was once actively flooded for stone extraction.
- Patches of phosphorescent moss thrive in the damp, shaded areas, attracting nocturnal insects.
