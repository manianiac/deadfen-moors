# The Ruin of Silver

**Hex Label:** M.8
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Ruin of Silver
- The central feature is the crumbling remains of a once-grand silver mine complex, now largely buried beneath a shifting sand dune. Walls of dark grey basalt stone are visible, fractured and weathered, hinting at a catastrophic collapse.
- A shallow, brackish pool, fed by subterranean springs, sits within the central courtyard of the ruin. The water is unnaturally still and dark, rumored to possess a faint, metallic scent.
- Scattered across the site are the skeletal remains of miners – mostly humanoid in form, but with elongated limbs and abnormally large hands, suggesting a non-human origin. Several workshops, partially collapsed, contain rusted tools and strange, geometric carvings.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Ridge
- A low, undulating ridge of shale overlooks the ruin, providing a strategic vantage point. Constant winds whistle through the rock formations, creating a low, mournful sound.
- Sparse patches of tenacious, silver-tinged grass cling to the ridge, believed to be a result of the mine’s runoff.
- Small, crude cairns mark the graves of several prospectors who perished attempting to scavenge the ruin.

## Additional POI 2: The Scarred Creek
- A sluggish, dark creek snakes its way eastward from the southern edge of the hex, stained a consistent grey color. The creek bed is riddled with dark, glistening deposits.
- Animal tracks – primarily those of large, predatory reptiles – are frequently found along the creek banks, suggesting a significant predator presence.
- A cluster of stunted, pale trees grow along the creek, exhibiting an unnatural resilience to the arid climate.
