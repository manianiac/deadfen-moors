# The Dark Fort

**Hex Label:** J.9
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Dark Fort
- [[The Dark Fort]] is a crumbling stone structure, approximately 100 feet in height, constructed with dark grey basalt. Sections of the walls are missing, revealing a chaotic interior filled with collapsed archways and rubble.  The fort appears to have been hastily abandoned, with signs of a desperate struggle – scorch marks, broken weaponry, and scattered bones.
- A perpetual, unnatural twilight seems to cling to the fort, regardless of the time of day. Local accounts claim this is due to a lingering magical darkness emanating from the central courtyard.  The air is noticeably colder and carries a faint scent of ozone.
- Beneath the main structure, a series of rough-hewn steps descend into darkness, leading to a network of damp, unstable tunnels. These tunnels are largely unexplored and believed to be the source of the unsettling atmosphere.



## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Creek
- A narrow, fast-flowing creek cuts through the southern edge of the hex, known for its unsettling, whispering sounds even when there is no wind.
- The water is unnaturally dark and reflects distorted images, often causing travelers to experience unsettling hallucinations.
- Sparse, thorny bushes line the creek banks, providing limited cover but also harboring aggressive, mutated insects.

## Additional POI 2: Stone Cairn
- A solitary stone cairn, approximately 20 feet in height, stands on a slight rise in the center of the hex.  It is constructed from mismatched stones and covered in faded, cryptic carvings.
- The cairn is a known landmark and occasionally marked with small, fresh offerings – usually animal bones or simple tools – suggesting a long-forgotten ritual.
- Analysis of the stones indicates they are not native to the region, hinting at a potential connection to a lost civilization.
