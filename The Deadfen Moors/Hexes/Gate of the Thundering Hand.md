# Gate of the Thundering Hand

**Hex Label:** G.15
**Terrain Type:** Plains (with scrub)


## Main POI: Gate of the Thundering Hand
- The Gate is a colossal, partially collapsed stone archway constructed from obsidian-like rock, approximately 60 feet tall and 30 feet wide. Runes depicting stylized hammers and lightning strike across its surface are heavily eroded, though still faintly visible.  The archway faces due east, opening onto a gently sloping plain.
-  Scattered around the base of the Gate are the remains of a long-abandoned encampment: shattered tents, charcoal pits, and rusted weaponry suggesting a hasty retreat. A small, stagnant pool of water collects within the archway’s shadow, perpetually murky and emitting a faint metallic odor.
-  Approximately 100 yards east of the Gate lies a shallow, circular depression filled with a fine, grey sand.  Analysis of the sand reveals unusually high concentrations of iron and traces of a volatile, sulfurous compound, consistent with intense heat.



## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Ridge
- A prominent ridge of eroded sandstone rises abruptly from the plains to the north of the Gate, approximately 3 miles distant.
-  The wind consistently whistles through natural fissures in the ridge, creating a constant, mournful sound – the source of its name.
-  Several caves dot the ridge face, offering potential shelter but also harboring unpredictable rockfalls.

## Additional POI 2: Old Man Hemlock’s Farmstead
- Located 4 miles southwest of the Gate, the farmstead consists of a crumbling stone farmhouse and a dilapidated barn, surrounded by overgrown fields.
-  A skeletal scarecrow, partially clothed in faded blue fabric, stands guard in the largest field – a remnant of the farm's former occupants.
-  The well within the farmstead’s courtyard is dry and choked with weeds, rumored to have once provided fresh water to travelers.
