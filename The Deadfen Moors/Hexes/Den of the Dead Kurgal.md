# Den of the Dead Kurgal

**Hex Label:** F.11
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: Den of the Dead Kurgal
- The hex’s center is dominated by the crumbling remains of Kurgal’s primary processing core, now a cavern choked with the desiccated husks of mining drones and the slow, creeping growth of phosphorescent fungi. 
- A circular plaza surrounds the core, paved with fractured basalt and stained with centuries of mineral runoff.  Skeletal remains – primarily dwarven miners and, disturbingly, several partially-constructed Kurgal drones - are scattered throughout, suggesting a prolonged and violent struggle.
-  A perpetual, low-frequency hum emanates from the core, a distorted echo of Kurgal’s operational programming, occasionally punctuated by the grinding of rusted gears and the scraping of metal on stone.



## Additional POI 1: Shrine of Kurgal
- Located approximately 1.5 miles northeast of the Den, the Shrine is a smaller, partially-collapsed shrine constructed from obsidian and hammered bronze.
-  A single, enormous gear – clearly a fragment of Kurgal’s own construction – is embedded in the shrine’s altar, radiating a faint warmth. 
-  The shrine is consistently warded against minor aberrant activity, though the nature of the warding is unclear.



## Additional POI 2: Tree of Kurgal
- Approximately 2.8 miles southeast of the Den, the Tree of Kurgal is a petrified, colossal ironwood tree, its branches twisted into grotesque shapes. 
-  Deep within the tree's hollow core, a viscous, metallic fluid drips constantly, exhibiting strange magnetic properties.
-  The tree appears to be actively drawing energy from the surrounding area, subtly influencing the behavior of constructs and aberrant creatures.
