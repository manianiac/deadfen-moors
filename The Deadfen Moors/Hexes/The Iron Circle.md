# The Iron Circle

**Hex Label:** N.7
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Iron Circle
- The Iron Circle is a partially ruined fortress constructed entirely of intricately linked iron bands, forming a roughly circular perimeter approximately 300 feet in diameter. Sections of the walls remain standing to heights of 20-30 feet, exhibiting signs of intense weathering and scorch marks.
- A central courtyard, now choked with weeds and shattered iron, contains the remains of a large, blackened hearth and several collapsed stone structures, suggesting a former barracks or administrative building.  Scavenged iron fragments are remarkably clean, showing little rust despite the age.
-  Local legends claim the Circle was built by a forgotten warrior cult dedicated to a metal god, and that it periodically “activates” during specific celestial alignments, emitting a low, resonant hum detectable for miles. Footprints in the surrounding mud indicate frequent, though currently absent, activity.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Ridge
- A prominent, wind-swept ridge dominates the northern edge of the hex, offering a vantage point overlooking the Iron Circle.
-  Scattered cairns marking the graves of nomadic tribes are found along the ridge’s base, each bearing faded tribal markings.
-  Strong, localized winds create persistent whistling sounds within the rock formations, earning the ridge its name.

## Additional POI 2: The Broken Well
- Located approximately 1.5 miles south-east of the Iron Circle, The Broken Well is a collapsed stone well, its basin filled with stagnant water.
-  The well’s collapse is believed to be connected to a localized tremor recorded several decades ago, triggering a cave-in.
-  Strange, iridescent algae bloom in the well’s water, and unsettling whispers are sometimes reported by those who venture too close.
