# The Finger's Tree

**Hex Label:** [[The Finger's Tree|D.12]]
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Finger's Tree
- The Finger’s Tree is a colossal, petrified oak, standing approximately 80 feet tall and 40 feet in diameter. Its trunk is unnaturally smooth and appears to be molded into a skeletal finger, perpetually pointing towards the northeast. The wood is a dark, almost obsidian black, and emits a faint chill.
- At the base of [[The Tree|the tree]] lies a circular clearing, approximately 60 feet across, covered in a thin layer of blue-grey moss and scattered with small, perfectly spherical stones that hum faintly when held.  Evidence of past rituals – charred remains and shallow depressions – are present.
- The surrounding plains are unusually barren, exhibiting stunted growth and a lack of wildlife. A constant, unsettling silence hangs over the area, broken only by the wind whistling through the branches of [[The Tree|the tree]] and the distant drone of insects.



## Additional POI 1: Stone Carver’s Hollow
- A small, sheltered cave carved into the base of a rocky outcrop, containing the scattered tools and unfinished sculptures of a solitary stone carver who vanished decades ago.
- The carvings depict distorted humanoid figures intertwined with thorny vines and unsettling, stylized versions of the Finger’s Tree.
- The air within the Hollow is thick with dust and a lingering scent of resin and something vaguely metallic.



## Additional POI 2: The Whispering Grass
- A patch of unusually tall, dense grass that seems to murmur with indistinct voices when the wind passes through it.
- The grass is a vibrant, unnatural shade of turquoise and possesses a soft, almost velvety texture.
- Locals claim prolonged exposure to the Whispering Grass induces vivid, unsettling dreams and paranoia.
