# The Dead Fort

**Hex Label:** B.10
**Terrain Type:** Forest (on hills)


## Main POI: The Dead Fort
- The Fort itself is a crumbling stone structure, primarily utilizing grey granite, now largely consumed by ivy and moss. Approximately 70% of the walls have collapsed, leaving only the main gatehouse and a section of the central keep standing partially intact. Wind whistles through broken arrow slits, creating an unsettling, constant drone.
- The immediate area around the Fort is characterized by a dense, unnatural silence, broken only by the drip of water and the calls of carrion birds. The ground is churned and muddy in several places, suggesting violent struggle or movement.
- Excavations within the Fort’s lower levels reveal a large, flooded chamber containing the skeletal remains of dozens of soldiers, clad in rusted plate armor and wielding broken weapons. Strange, phosphorescent fungi grow in the darkest corners.



## Additional POI 1: Whisperwood Grove
- A small clearing within the forest, dominated by ancient, gnarled oak trees. Locals claim the trees whisper secrets on the wind.
- Strange, swirling patterns are occasionally visible on the moss covering the base of the trees.
- Small, intricately carved wooden figurines, depicting humanoid figures with elongated limbs, are frequently found scattered around the grove.

## Additional POI 2: Stone Serpent Creek
- A narrow, fast-flowing stream cutting through the lower hills, known locally as "Stone Serpent Creek" due to its winding path and grey, serpentine stones.
- The water is unnaturally cold and dark, and a faint, oily sheen coats the surface.
- Several submerged rock formations resemble stylized serpent heads, often slick with algae.
