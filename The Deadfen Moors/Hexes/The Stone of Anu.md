# The Stone of Anu

**Hex Label:** L.8
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Stone of Anu
- The Stone of Anu is a monolithic obsidian formation, approximately 30 feet tall and 15 feet in diameter, rising abruptly from the otherwise flat plains. Its surface is covered in intricate, faintly glowing glyphs believed to be related to Anu’s influence during the Ascension.
- A constant, low-frequency hum emanates from the stone, causing a subtle feeling of unease in those who linger nearby. Scanners detect fluctuating energy readings, strongest near the glyphs.
- The area immediately surrounding the stone is unnaturally fertile, with unusually tall grasses and vibrant wildflowers.  Animal life tends to avoid the area, though rumors speak of predatory birds drawn to the energy.


## Additional POI 1: The Silent Watchers
- These are three weathered, partially buried statues of humanoid figures, each approximately 8 feet tall. They are carved from a grey stone similar to the surrounding plains, and perpetually face the Stone of Anu.
-  Analysis of the statues reveals they were once animated constructs, deactivated long ago. Residual energy indicates they were guardians of the stone during the Nabu's time.
-  A small shrine, consisting of a pile of smooth, grey stones, has been constructed at the base of each statue, suggesting recent, possibly desperate, worship.

## Additional POI 2: The Fracture Scar
- This is a roughly circular depression in the ground, approximately 100 feet in diameter, marked by a wide, jagged scar of blackened earth. It's hypothesized to be the site of a failed attempt to harness the Stone's energy.
- The air above the Fracture Scar shimmers with heat distortion, and the ground is scorched black, even in areas far from the central scar.
-  Remnants of shattered Nabu technology – primarily metallic plates etched with the same glyphs as the Stone – are scattered within the scar's perimeter.
