# The Valley

**Hex Label:** F.6
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Valley
- [[The Valley]] is a deceptively fertile expanse, characterized by rolling plains of ochre grasses and scattered groves of ironwood trees. A slow-moving, brackish river, the Whisperwind, snakes through the center, providing limited irrigation and supporting a surprisingly dense population of waterfowl.
- [[The Valley|The valley]] floor is dominated by the ruins of Oldhaven, a once-thriving settlement now largely swallowed by the plains. Crumbling stone walls and the skeletal remains of buildings suggest a sudden abandonment, though whispers speak of a catastrophic flood.
- A network of ancient, partially submerged causeways connects key areas within [[The Valley|the valley]], constructed from a dark, obsidian-like stone. These causeways are currently overgrown with moss and appear unstable, and navigation is treacherous.



## Additional POI 1: Stonefang Peak
- Situated on the northern edge of the hex, Stonefang Peak is a solitary, jagged basalt outcrop, perpetually shrouded in mist.
- Contains a small, active cave system rumored to be home to a colony of cave spiders and rare mineral deposits.
- Locals avoid the peak, believing it to be a place of restless spirits.

## Additional POI 2: Whisperwind Springs
- Located approximately 2 miles east of [[The Valley]], Whisperwind Springs is a cluster of three geothermal springs, renowned for their purported healing properties.
- The water is unusually warm and carries a faint metallic scent. 
-  A small shrine dedicated to a water deity, known only as “[[Silas the Pallid|Silas]],” stands beside the largest spring.
