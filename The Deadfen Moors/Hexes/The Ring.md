# The Ring

**Hex Label:** F.3
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Ring
- [[The Ring]] is a naturally formed stone circle, approximately 30 feet in diameter, situated within a clearing dominated by ancient, moss-covered oaks. The stones are predominantly grey granite, exhibiting faint, spiraling patterns within the rock face.
- A perpetual, heavy mist clings to the area around the circle, often obscuring details and creating an unsettling silence. Local legends claim the mist is the breath of trapped spirits.
-  A narrow, overgrown trail, barely discernible, leads into the circle from the south. Recent signs suggest sporadic, but increasing, attempts at ritualistic markings on the stones. 

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwood Grove
- The Grove is a dense thicket of silver birch, known for the unusual whispering sound produced by the wind rustling through its leaves.
- Several weathered, moss-covered stone altars are scattered amongst the trees, bearing faded carvings of stylized wolves.
- A small stream, fed by underground springs, flows through the grove, reputed to have healing properties.

## Additional POI 2: Old Man Hemlock’s Cairn
- The Cairn is a solitary, crumbling cairn constructed from roughly stacked stones, approximately 10 feet high, marking the grave of a hermit known only as “Old Man Hemlock.”
- The cairn is surrounded by a patch of unnaturally vibrant, thorny blackberry bushes, said to grow only in the presence of restless spirits.
- Scavenged remnants of Hemlock's possessions – a rusted iron pot, a broken wooden flute, and a collection of dried herbs – are partially buried near the base.
