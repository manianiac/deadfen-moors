# Tree of Kurgal

**Hex Label:** [[Tree of Kurgal|G.10]]
**Terrain Type:** Plains (with scrub)


## Main POI: Tree of Kurgal
- [[The Tree]] of [[Kurgal]] is a petrified, colossal oak, standing alone upon the windswept plains. Its bark is a deep grey, riddled with deep fissures that appear to shift and writhe subtly in the light. [[The Tree|The tree]]’s branches, unnaturally rigid, terminate in sharpened points resembling crude, rusted claws.
- A perpetual, unsettling stillness surrounds [[The Tree|the tree]]; no wind ever touches its branches, and the scrub surrounding its base remains unnaturally dry and lifeless. The air here is noticeably colder, carrying a faint metallic scent.
- Deep within [[The Tree|the tree]]’s trunk, a dark, pulsing cavity exists – the presumed point of origin for the construct’s corruption and a focal point for the lingering presence of [[Kurgal]].


## Additional POI 1: Shrine of Kurgal
- The Shrine is a crumbling, partially buried stone structure built around the base of [[The Tree|the Tree]], constructed in a style vaguely reminiscent of dwarven architecture but heavily distorted and overlaid with crude, geometric carvings.
- The shrine contains a shattered, obsidian altar stained with what appears to be dried, black blood. Scraps of metal, likely from [[Kurgal]]’s construction, are scattered across the floor.
- Evidence suggests a short-lived, fanatical cult once worshipped [[Kurgal]] here, offering sacrifices to appease the ‘[[Kurgal|Great Mountain]].’


## Additional POI 2: Den of the Dead Kurgal
- [[The Den]] is a network of claustrophobic caverns carved into the base of the plains, choked with the skeletal remains of both [[Dwarves|dwarves]] and monstrous creatures. 
- The skeletal remains are fused with metallic fragments, clearly the result of [[Kurgal]]’s actions, forming a grotesque tapestry of bone and machine.
-  A palpable aura of decay and despair permeates [[The Den|the den]], intensifying near a large, partially collapsed section of the ceiling where several [[Kurgal]]’s energy signatures have been detected.
