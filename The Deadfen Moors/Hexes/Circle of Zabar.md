# Circle of Zabar

**Hex Label:** L.7
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: Circle of Zabar
- The Circle is a sprawling, circular depression in the plains, approximately one mile in diameter, filled with a viscous, bronze-colored mud. The mud appears to subtly shift and flow, exhibiting a faint, internal luminescence.
- Within the circle’s perimeter stand the rusted, partially collapsed remains of what were once dwarven workshops and living quarters, constructed from a dark, obsidian-like stone. Crude, swirling carvings of earth-worms and fractured gears are prevalent.
- A colossal, immobile earth-construct, identified as Zabar, dominates the center of the circle. He is approximately 30 feet tall, composed of rusted bronze plates layered over a core of dense, pulsating earth.  His eyes are glowing bronze orbs.



## Additional POI 1: Whispering Cairns
- Small, lichen-covered stone cairns dot the immediate exterior of the Circle, each emitting a faint, low hum audible only in absolute silence.
- Local legends claim the cairns are the resting places of dwarf scouts who perished during Zabar’s initial emergence.
- Analysis reveals traces of a volatile earth-elemental energy concentrated within the cairns.

## Additional POI 2: Broken Spur
- Approximately 1.5 miles southeast of the Circle stands a crumbling, half-buried stone spur, marked by a shattered bronze spur wheel.
- Evidence suggests this was a temporary dwarven repair station established to maintain access to the Circle during periods of relative stability.
- Scavengers report finding small, intricate gear mechanisms embedded within the stonework.
