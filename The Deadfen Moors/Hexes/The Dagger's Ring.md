# The Dagger's Ring

**Hex Label:** C.6
**Terrain Type:** Hills


## Main POI: The Dagger's Ring
- The Dagger’s Ring is a naturally formed stone circle, approximately 30 meters in diameter, situated atop the highest point within the hex. The stones are a dark, almost obsidian-like basalt, polished smooth by centuries of wind and rain. Ritualistic markings, partially eroded, cover many of the stones.
- Within the circle’s center lies a shallow, perpetually damp depression filled with stagnant, dark water. The water is consistently cold and emits a faint, metallic scent. Small, iridescent fish, seemingly adapted to the darkness, inhabit the pool.
- Local legends claim [[The Ring|the ring]] was a site of sacrifices to a forgotten god of war, and that whispers can still be heard on the wind during certain lunar phases. Scavenged remains of what are believed to be humanoid skeletons are occasionally found within [[The Ring|the ring]].



## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Hollow
- A narrow, sheltered valley carved by a dry riverbed, known for unnaturally still patches of air – hence the name.
- Home to a colony of enormous, nocturnal moths with wingspans of up to a meter, drawn to the faint luminescence around The Dagger’s Ring.
- Tracks of a large, feline predator – possibly a mountain lynx or similar – are frequently found near [[The Valley|the valley]] entrance.



## Additional POI 2: Stonefall Crag
- A sheer cliff face marking the western edge of the hex, composed of layered sedimentary rock.
- Numerous small caves and crevices provide shelter for nesting birds and small mammals.
- Evidence of quarrying activity – partially collapsed stone supports and scattered tools – suggests a brief period of exploitation of [[The Stone|the stone]]’s quality.
