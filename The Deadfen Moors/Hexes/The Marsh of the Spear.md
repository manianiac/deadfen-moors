# The Marsh of the Spear

**Hex Label:** F.5
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Marsh of the Spear
- [[The Marsh of the Spear]] is a sprawling, stagnant wetland dominated by reeds, cypress, and submerged timber. The water is brackish and often choked with algae, creating a murky, unsettling atmosphere. Visibility rarely exceeds 30 feet.
- A massive, eroded spearhead—the source of the hex’s name—lies half-submerged at [[The Marsh|the marsh]]’s center, carved from a dark, obsidian-like stone. Locals believe it was once a weapon of a forgotten god.
- [[The Marsh|The marsh]] is home to a surprisingly dense population of giant amphibians (Bullyfrog variants), scavenging birds, and venomous snakes. Tracks of a predatory, cat-like creature, dubbed “The Shadow Walker,” are frequently found near the deeper channels.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Grove
- A small, sheltered grove of ancient willow trees, where the wind perpetually creates a haunting, whispering sound.
- The grove is considered a sacred place by the local Riverfolk tribe, known for their skill in navigating [[The Marsh|the marsh]].
- Scattered around the grove are crude stone altars adorned with feathers and polished river stones.

## Additional POI 2: Blackrock Outpost
- A crumbling, fortified watchtower constructed from blackened shale rock, overlooking the western edge of [[The Marsh|the marsh]].
- The outpost is currently occupied by a small, dwindling band of mercenaries, primarily guarding against bandit raids and wild animal attacks.
- Scavenged supplies and decaying armor litter the courtyard, hinting at a violent past.
