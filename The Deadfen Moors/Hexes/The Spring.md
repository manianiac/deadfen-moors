# The Spring

**Hex Label:** I.6
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Spring
- [[The Spring]] is a naturally occurring, geothermally heated pool of crystal-clear water located at the hex’s center. The water maintains a constant temperature of approximately 75°F year-round.
- A crumbling stone structure, partially submerged in [[The Spring|the spring]]’s edge, serves as a rudimentary shrine dedicated to a local water deity, known only as “The Weaver.” Offerings of polished stones and woven reeds are frequently left.
- A distinct scent of sulfur permeates the area around [[The Spring|the Spring]], and unusual, brightly colored fungi thrive in the damp shadows beneath the surrounding trees. 

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Ridge
- A prominent, wind-sculpted ridge dominates the western edge of the hex, providing panoramic views.
- Sparse patches of hardy grasses and stunted juniper trees cling to the ridge's slopes.
- Scattered amongst the rocks are numerous, naturally hollowed stones that produce a low, whistling sound when the wind blows – hence the name.

## Additional POI 2: Old Man’s Cairn
- Located approximately 2 miles northeast of [[The Spring|the Spring]], a solitary, overgrown cairn constructed of rough-hewn stones stands on a slight rise.
- Locals claim the cairn marks the burial place of a solitary hermit who vanished over a century ago.
-  Evidence of recent, though poorly maintained, tracks suggests periodic visits by [[The Copper-Grubbers|scavengers]] or travelers.
