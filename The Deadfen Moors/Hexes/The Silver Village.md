# The Silver Village

**Hex Label:** K.8
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Silver Village
- The Silver Village is a settlement of approximately 250 people primarily engaged in the mining and crafting of silver ore found within the surrounding plains. Buildings are constructed from grey stone and timber, with many homes featuring silver accents – polished ore used for ornamentation and detailing.
- The village's economy revolves around the ‘Silversmith’s Guild’, a highly respected organization controlling the quality and sale of all silver products produced within K.8. Guild masters hold considerable influence over village affairs.
- A partially collapsed aqueduct, originating from a distant mountain spring, runs along the southern edge of the village, providing a consistent water source for both the settlement and the village’s famed silver-infused water used in potions and medicinal remedies.

## Additional POI 1: Widow’s Knoll
- A windswept rise overlooking the eastern plains, marked by a solitary, ancient oak tree – believed to be a place of mourning and remembrance for lost travelers.
- Scattered amongst the roots of the oak are weathered stone markers, potentially indicating graves or forgotten burial sites.
- Local folklore speaks of whispers carried on the wind around the Knoll, attributed to the spirits of those interred there.

## Additional POI 2: The Broken Cart Track
- A heavily eroded, overgrown cart track stretches approximately 3 miles northeast from the village, disappearing into a dense thicket of thorny brambles.
- The track appears to have been abandoned abruptly decades ago, with signs of a struggle – discarded tools and a partially dismantled wagon – hinting at a violent event.
- Several game trails intersect the Broken Cart Track, making it a relatively easy, though potentially dangerous, route for small groups traveling on foot.
