# The Bloody Copse

**Hex Label:** K.13
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Bloody Copse
- The Bloody Copse is a dense, ancient forest characterized by towering, gnarled oaks and a perpetually damp undergrowth. The soil is a thick, clay-rich red, giving the area its name due to the extensive iron deposits and the frequent, unsettling crimson staining of the leaves and ground.
- A ruined stone circle, partially collapsed and choked with thorny vines, occupies the center of the Copse. Carved into some of the stones are unsettling depictions of humanoid figures engaged in ritualistic combat, partially obscured by moss and lichen.
- A stagnant, brackish pool known as the ‘Silent Mire’ lies to the north-east, consistently emitting a low, mournful hum and rumored to harbor mutated amphibians and unsettlingly still reflections.



## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Hollow
- A small, sheltered valley known for unusually strong, consistent winds that create a constant, whispering sound.
- Home to a small colony of nomadic harpies, primarily attracted by the wind’s resonance.
- Contains the skeletal remains of a long-lost shepherd’s wagon, partially buried in the valley floor.

## Additional POI 2: Stonefang’s Watch
- A precariously balanced granite outcrop offering a commanding view of the surrounding forest.
- Scars from ancient battles are visible on the rock face, suggesting a long history of defense.
- Patrolled sporadically by a solitary, grizzled hunter known only as ‘Stonefang’.
