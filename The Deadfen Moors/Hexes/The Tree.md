# The Tree

**Hex Label:** M.6
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Tree
- [[The Tree]] is a colossal, ancient oak, estimated to be at least 800 years old, dominating the center of the hex. Its trunk is wider than any building in the surrounding settlements, and its canopy blots out much of the sunlight.
- Strange, luminescent moss covers the base of [[The Tree|the tree]] and the roots, pulsing with a faint, rhythmic glow that intensifies during the new moon. Locals believe this moss holds potent magical energy.
- Deep within the root system, a network of tunnels and chambers has been discovered, carved not by natural processes but by an unknown, possibly intelligent, force. These chambers are consistently cool and unnervingly silent.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Glade
- A small, perpetually misty glade adjacent to the south side of [[The Tree]], known for unusually vivid and sometimes unsettling auditory hallucinations.
- The Glade is home to a population of small, iridescent birds, the 'Echoes,' which are said to mimic the voices of lost travelers.
- Scattered around the glade are standing stones covered in indecipherable carvings.

## Additional POI 2: Stone Cairn
- Located approximately 2 miles east of [[The Tree]], a weathered stone cairn marks the burial site of a long-lost tribe.
- The cairn contains several skeletal remains adorned with tarnished [[Zabar|bronze]] ornaments and fragments of what appear to be ritualistic tools.
-  Unexplained tremors occasionally shake the ground around the cairn, accompanied by the faint scent of ozone.
