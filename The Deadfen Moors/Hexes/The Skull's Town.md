# The Skull's Town

**Hex Label:** N.8
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Skull's Town
- The town itself is a collection of roughly thirty weathered, grey stone buildings clustered around a central, cracked plaza dominated by a massive, partially submerged skull – the namesake of the settlement. Buildings are largely functional, constructed of stone and timber, many showing signs of age and disrepair.
- A perpetually damp marsh extends to the south and west of the town, the source of the skull’s partial submersion and a breeding ground for disease-carrying insects. The water is brackish and stained with algae.
- The Skull’s Town is inhabited by a population of approximately seventy individuals – primarily scavengers, prospectors, and those seeking refuge from the surrounding wilderness. They maintain a wary, almost paranoid, atmosphere.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Cairn
- A solitary, windswept cairn constructed from smooth, white stones, said to be a marker for a forgotten tribe.
- The cairn emits a faint, unsettling hum audible only in still air.
- Strange, geometric patterns are occasionally etched into the stones by an unknown force.

## Additional POI 2: The Rust Mire
- A desolate area of iron-rich soil and shallow pools, littered with the decaying remains of ancient mining equipment.
- Metallic residue coats the ground, attracting scavengers seeking valuable materials and triggering unsettling metallic echoes.
- Evidence of recent, violent activity – disturbed earth and shattered metal – suggests a territorial conflict.
