# The Doomed Mountain

**Hex Label:** H.1
**Terrain Type:** Mountains


## Main POI: The Doomed Mountain
- The mountain's peak is perpetually wreathed in a sickly green mist, said to be the residue of a magical cataclysm that shattered [[The Stone|the stone]] centuries ago. Jagged obsidian shards protrude from the rock face, radiating a faint, chilling cold.
- Within the mountain’s lower slopes lies the ruined city of Veridian, choked with dense, phosphorescent fungi and inhabited by mutated, insectoid creatures warped by the lingering magic. Buildings are constructed from a dark, porous stone that absorbs light.
- Deep within the mountain’s central cavern, a crystalline structure – the “Heart of the Storm” – pulses with unstable energy, occasionally triggering tremors and violent bursts of green light.  Evidence suggests it was once a source of immense power, now dangerously corrupted.


## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Pass
- A narrow, winding pass carved through the southern slope, constantly battered by high winds carrying faint, mournful whispers.
- Home to a small, nomadic tribe known as [[The Stone|the Stone]] Singers, who interpret the wind’s whispers and live in harmony with the mountain.
- Marked by several ancient, weathered cairns – likely markers for a forgotten trade route.

## Additional POI 2: The Obsidian Pools
- A series of small, perfectly circular pools filled with black, viscous liquid reflecting the perpetual mist.
- Creatures known as Shadow Stalkers are drawn to the pools, attempting to drown victims in the dark fluid.
- The pools are rumored to possess minor prophetic properties, though gazing into them risks madness.
