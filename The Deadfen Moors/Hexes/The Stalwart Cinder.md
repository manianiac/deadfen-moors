# The Stalwart Cinder

**Hex Label:** J.14
**Terrain Type:** Mountains


## Main POI: The Stalwart Cinder
- The Stalwart Cinder is a colossal, naturally formed obsidian pillar rising approximately 300 feet from the surrounding mountain slopes. Its smooth, dark surface reflects the sparse sunlight, giving it an unsettlingly watchful appearance.
- Within the Cinder's base, a network of thermal vents releases plumes of superheated steam and sulfurous gas, creating a perpetually hazy atmosphere and supporting a bizarre ecosystem of heat-resistant fungi and lichen. 
- The Cinder is believed by the local mountain clans to be a solidified shard of a fallen god, imbued with protective energies, and is the site of recurring, erratic seismic activity.



## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Pass
- Whisperwind Pass is a narrow gorge carved through the eastern flank of the hex, notorious for unpredictable gusts of wind that carry disorienting whispers.
- The Pass is home to a small colony of Cliff Hawks, fiercely territorial and skilled hunters.
- Evidence of ancient mining operations – collapsed tunnels and rusted tools – suggest attempts to exploit the mountain’s mineral deposits.

## Additional POI 2: Stonebreath Lake
- Stonebreath Lake is a small, high-altitude lake nestled within a bowl of jagged rock, fed by glacial runoff and perpetually shrouded in mist.
- The water is unusually warm, attributed to geothermal activity seeping through the lakebed.
- Scattered around the lake's perimeter are the weathered remains of a small, ruined shrine dedicated to a forgotten water spirit.
