# The Shrouded Wall

**Hex Label:** I.3
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Shrouded Wall
- [[The Shrouded Wall]] is a naturally occurring basalt cliff face, approximately 80 feet high and stretching for roughly 1 mile before abruptly ending. The basalt is perpetually damp and covered in a thick, clinging moss that obscures its texture, giving it the “shrouded” appearance.
- At the base of the Wall, a small, stagnant pool – known as the Whisperpool – collects rainwater and runoff. Strange, melodic murmurs can sometimes be heard emanating from the water, though their source is unknown.
- Scattered around the base of the Wall are the crumbling remains of what appear to be ancient, circular dwellings constructed from the same dark basalt. Evidence suggests they were abandoned suddenly and violently.


## Additional POI 1: Briar’s Watch
- A dilapidated, single-room watchtower constructed of rough-hewn timber, positioned on a slight rise overlooking the Wall’s western face.
- Home to a solitary, paranoid trapper named [[Silas the Pallid|Silas]], who claims to have seen “shadows” moving amongst the moss.
- Known for its exceptionally strong, bitter berry bushes, yielding a fruit used in a local medicinal tonic.

## Additional POI 2: The Rooted Circle
- A clearing dominated by a colossal, ancient tree – the ‘Heartwood’ – whose roots form a natural amphitheater.
- The ground within the circle is unnaturally still, and the air feels noticeably colder. Local folklore claims the Heartwood is a nexus of primal magic.
- Several partially submerged stone markers – weathered and covered in lichen – surround [[The Tree|the tree]]’s base, arranged in a spiral pattern.
