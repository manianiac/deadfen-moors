# Ereshki’s Gate

**Hex Label:** G.4
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: Ereshki’s Gate
- Ereshki’s Gate is a colossal archway formed from petrified, black wood, interwoven with pulsating, violet moss. It stands within a perpetually shadowed clearing at the heart of the forest, marking the entrance to a long-forgotten, magically-defended corridor.
- The air around the gate crackles with residual arcane energy, noticeably colder and carrying the faint scent of ozone and decay. Strange, glyph-like patterns constantly shift across the stone surface.
- The gate is guarded by animated, thorny constructs – remnants of Ereshki’s failed attempts to bind Scourge - that relentlessly attack any who approach without offering a suitable tribute of silver and bone.



## Additional POI 1: The Road of Ereshki
- This overgrown, winding path is choked with skeletal remains of flora and fauna, twisted by Ereshki’s dark magic. Footprints, both humanoid and monstrous, mar the muddy surface.
- The Road terminates abruptly at a crumbling stone marker – a stylized image of a raven – believed to be a focal point for Ereshki’s protective rituals.
- Travel along the Road is highly dangerous, as pockets of unstable magical energy erupt sporadically, creating illusory duplicates of travelers and triggering localized weather disturbances.

## Additional POI 2: Ereshki’s Rise
- Ereshki’s Rise is a jagged, obsidian pinnacle rising 150 feet from the forest floor, seemingly formed from a single, immense shard.
- The summit is perpetually wreathed in a sickly green mist, and strange, whispering voices can be heard emanating from the peak, promising power to those who dare to listen.
- Scattered around the base of the Rise are shallow depressions filled with viscous, black ichor – a byproduct of Ereshki’s experiments, said to possess potent, though unstable, magical properties.
