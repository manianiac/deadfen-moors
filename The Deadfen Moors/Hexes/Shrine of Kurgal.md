# Shrine of Kurgal

**Hex Label:** A.5
**Terrain Type:** Hills


## Main POI: Shrine of Kurgal
- The [[Shrine of Kurgal]] occupies a natural amphitheater carved into the hillside, dominated by a colossal, partially collapsed mining drill – the remains of [[Kurgal]]’s primary body.  Rust-colored rock and pulverized ore cover the area, stained with a perpetual, slick sheen of mineral oil secreted by the construct.
-  Stone glyphs, likely of [[Nabu]] origin, are embedded throughout the shrine’s walls, pulsing with a faint, unsettling warmth.  Scattered around the base of the drill are the skeletal remains of dwarven miners, seemingly frozen in postures of terrified anticipation. 
-  A palpable sense of dread hangs in the air, intensifying near the drill’s central axis.  Mechanical whirring and the occasional grinding sound emanate from within the structure, regardless of external stimuli.



## Additional POI 1: Den of the Dead Kurgal
- Located approximately 1 mile south of the Shrine, [[The Den|the Den]] of the Dead [[Kurgal]] is a cavern system choked with fungal growth and the desiccated remains of smaller, corrupted [[Dwarves|constructs]]. 
- The air within is thick with the stench of decay and a low, rhythmic clicking sound, believed to be the remnants of [[Kurgal]]’s internal mechanisms.
-  Scattered among the fungal blooms are crude, pre-dwarven tools – suggesting that [[Kurgal]]’s rampage predates the [[Dwarves|dwarves]]’ arrival.



## Additional POI 2: Tree of Kurgal
- Situated 3 miles northeast of the Shrine, [[The Tree|the Tree]] of [[Kurgal]] is a petrified, ironwood tree, impossibly tall and encased in a thick layer of metallic ore. 
-  [[The Tree|The tree]]'s branches bear a constant, shimmering spray of metallic dust, believed to be a byproduct of [[Kurgal]]’s attempts to ‘mine’ the very earth.
-  A concentrated aura of static electricity surrounds [[The Tree|the tree]], capable of disrupting electronic devices and inducing minor shocks in unprotected individuals.
