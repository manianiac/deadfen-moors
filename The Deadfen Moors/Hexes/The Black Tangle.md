# The Black Tangle

**Hex Label:** H.2
**Terrain Type:** Mountains


## Main POI: The Black Tangle
- The Black Tangle is a perpetually shadowed ravine carved into the mountain range, formed by a collapsed obsidian vein that now radiates a subtle, chilling darkness. The air within the Tangle is noticeably colder and carries a faint scent of sulfur.
- The ravine floor is choked with dense, thorny vines – the ‘Black Tangle’ – that seem to actively resist movement and possess a faintly unsettling luminescence at twilight. Strange, geometric patterns are occasionally visible within the vines’ growth.
- Deep within the Tangle, a ruined shrine to a forgotten deity of stone and darkness stands partially submerged in a stagnant, black pool. The pool's surface reflects a distorted image of the sky.

## Additional POI 1: Stonefang Peak
- Stonefang Peak, a jagged spire of granite, offers the only stable vantage point within a mile of the Tangle, providing a clear view of the ravine.
- Scattered around the base of the peak are crude stone carvings depicting reptilian creatures, suggesting a previous humanoid presence.
- Frequent rockfalls make climbing the peak treacherous, attracting opportunistic scavengers.

## Additional POI 2: Whisperwind Cave
- Whisperwind Cave is a narrow fissure leading into the mountainside, notorious for echoing voices and disorienting drafts, despite being completely sealed.
- The cave contains a significant deposit of raw, uncut obsidian, highly prized by local smiths and artisans.
- A colony of cave bats, unusually large and aggressive, nests within the deepest chambers.
