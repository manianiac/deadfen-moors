# Valley of the Near Spirit

**Hex Label:** M.5
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: Valley of the Near Spirit
- The Valley is a vast, undulating plain dominated by tall, golden grasses that shimmer in the constant winds. Erosion has carved deep, meandering ravines into the earth, creating a confusing network of shadows and pockets of near-constant twilight.
- At the valley’s heart lies the “Whispering Stone,” a monolith of pale grey quartz that emits a low, resonant hum, said to be the voice of the Near Spirit – a benevolent but elusive entity believed to guide lost travelers. The stone is surrounded by a circle of standing stones, partially collapsed and covered in lichen.
- Scattered throughout the valley are numerous “Spirit Wells,” small, perfectly circular depressions filled with water that consistently maintains a unnaturally still surface, reflecting the sky with unnerving clarity. Locals believe these wells offer fleeting glimpses into the future or the memories of the dead.

## Additional POI 1: Dustwind Ridge
- A jagged, windswept ridge of red sandstone forms the valley’s northern boundary, offering a precarious vantage point and a significant hazard due to frequent dust devils.
- Sparse clusters of thorny scrub and hardy, grey grasses cling to the ridge, providing limited shelter and a dangerous environment for travelers.
- Recent signs indicate the presence of a solitary, weathered shepherd’s hut at the ridge’s highest point.

## Additional POI 2: The Broken Cart
- A rusted, overturned cart lies half-buried in the southern valley floor, its wheels spinning uselessly. Remains of the cargo – mostly dried grains and a single, tarnished silver locket – are scattered nearby.
- Local legend claims the cart was destroyed by a sudden manifestation of the Near Spirit's power, a sign of the entity’s displeasure.
- Footprints – large and indistinct – lead away from the cart, disappearing into the tall grass.
