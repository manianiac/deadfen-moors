# The Ancient Fear

**Hex Label:** G.11
**Terrain Type:** Plains (with scrub)


## Main POI: The Ancient Fear
- The central feature is the crumbling stone circle, roughly 30 feet in diameter, constructed from obsidian-like rock. Weathering has eroded much of the stone, but intricate carvings depicting serpentine figures and distorted faces remain visible.
- A constant, low-frequency humming permeates the area around the circle, strongest at night. Locals whisper of a primal dread associated with the stones, claiming prolonged exposure induces paranoia and vivid nightmares.
- Scattered across the immediate vicinity are several shallow depressions filled with a viscous, grey fluid – analysis reveals traces of organic compounds and an unusual metallic sheen, but no immediately identifiable source.



## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Ridge
- A prominent, windswept ridge offers a panoramic view of the hex, approximately 1.5 miles to the northeast.
- Several weathered cairns mark the remains of a small, nomadic tribe who vanished centuries ago, leaving behind only fragments of bone and intricately woven leather pouches.
- The wind constantly carries faint, unintelligible voices along the ridge, though these are rarely distinct enough to understand.

## Additional POI 2: Serpent’s Mire
- A stagnant, black mire occupies the southwestern corner of the hex, characterized by thick reeds and a pervasive, sickly-sweet odor.
- Large, serpentine tracks – too large for any known reptile – are frequently found pressed into the mud, disappearing into the depths of the mire.
- A partially submerged, rusted iron cage lies at the heart of the mire, its purpose unknown, but radiating a faint chilling effect.
