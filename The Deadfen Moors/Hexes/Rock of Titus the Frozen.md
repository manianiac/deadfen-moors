# Rock of Titus the Frozen

**Hex Label:** C.13
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: Rock of Titus the Frozen
- The Rock of Titus the Frozen is a monolithic, wind-scoured basalt formation rising 40 feet from the otherwise flat plains. Ice clings perpetually to its surface, sculpted by the relentless winds into grotesque, vaguely humanoid shapes – remnants of Titus’s infamous boasts about freezing his foes.
- At the base of the rock, partially buried in the soil, lies Titus’s abandoned cart, heavily damaged and filled with decaying maps, stolen trinkets, and a half-eaten loaf of bread. The air around the cart feels subtly colder, carrying a faint scent of frost and something metallic.
- A narrow fissure runs vertically through the rock face, rumored to be the entrance to a forgotten tunnel Titus used for smuggling and scouting. The tunnel slopes downwards into the darkness, and faint, rhythmic dripping can be heard within.



## Additional POI 1: The Near-Thief’s Cache
- Located approximately half a mile north of the Rock, this shallow cave contains the remnants of Titus’s failed attempt to steal a Sunstone from a minor God-King’s guard.
- The cave floor is slick with mud and stained with dried blood, and the walls are covered in hastily scratched warnings - mostly in halfling and remarkably accurate depictions of armored guards.
- A single, tarnished silver coin, bearing the mark of a forgotten kingdom, lies at the back of the cave – the only tangible item recovered from the attempted heist.



## Additional POI 2: Windwhisper Gulch
- This narrow, sheltered gulch cuts through the northern edge of the hex, offering a brief respite from the constant winds.
- The walls of the gulch are covered in strange, swirling patterns carved by the wind over centuries, said to be echoes of Titus’s scouts’ murmurs.
-  Scattered amongst the rock formations are several skeletal remains – primarily of large, flightless birds, suggesting a hunting ground for the halfling scout.
