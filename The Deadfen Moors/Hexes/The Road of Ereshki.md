# The Road of Ereshki

**Hex Label:** I.2
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Road of Ereshki
- The Road of Ereshki is a cracked, grey stone path, barely discernible beneath a perpetual layer of windblown dust and ash. It stretches approximately three miles from Ereshki’s Gate to the crumbling remains of Ereshki’s Rise, cutting through the otherwise featureless plains.
- Twisted, skeletal trees – victims of Ereshki’s forbidden magic – line the road, their branches gnarled and reaching like clawing hands. Strange, phosphorescent lichen clings to their trunks, casting an unsettling green glow at dusk.
- The air along the road carries a faint, metallic tang, and whispers carried on the wind seem to murmur unintelligible warnings. Tracks of both humanoid and monstrous forms are frequently found pressed into the dust.



## Additional POI 1: The Whispering Cairns
- A collection of twelve weathered stone cairns stand in a semi-circle five miles east of the Road, each marked with a single, deeply etched rune.
- The cairns are said to amplify Ereshki’s influence, creating intense auditory hallucinations for those who linger too long.
- Evidence suggests the cairns were constructed by a cult dedicated to appeasing the Scourge.



## Additional POI 2: The Scarred Well
- Located approximately one mile north of the Road, a deep, circular well sits within a small depression. The stone is blackened and cracked, resembling a gaping wound.
- The water within the well is perpetually dark and viscous, exhibiting minor, shifting patterns. It’s rumored to grant visions – often terrifying ones – to those who drink.
