# Castor’s Lake

**Hex Label:** O.10
**Terrain Type:** Farmland


## Main POI: Castor’s Lake
- Castor’s Lake is a roughly circular body of water, approximately one mile in diameter, nestled within a wide, gently sloping plain. The lake’s water is unusually clear, reflecting the sky with remarkable accuracy.
- The shoreline is lined with reeds and rushes, partially submerged in muddy banks. Scattered along the eastern edge are the crumbling stone foundations of what were once rudimentary docks and workshops, indicating Castor’s failed attempts to establish a settlement.
- A persistent, mournful mist hangs over the lake, especially during the evening hours. Locals whisper of restless spirits and the lingering sadness of a halfling who sought peace but found only tragedy.


## Additional POI 1: The Weaver’s Bend
- Situated on the southern shore, this is a narrow channel where the river that feeds the lake splits, creating a series of small, interwoven pools.
- The current here is exceptionally strong, making navigation perilous, and rumored to be a favored spot for river sprites.
- Fragments of intricately woven rope – far too fine for any known crafting – are frequently found snagged on the reeds.



## Additional POI 2: Old Man Thorne’s Cairn
- Located about half a mile inland, overlooking the lake, stands a solitary stone cairn constructed from weathered grey stones.
- Thorne, a local shepherd, claimed to have been a close acquaintance of Castor and regularly left offerings at the cairn for many years. He disappeared without a trace ten years prior.
- The cairn is marked by a single, unusually large raven’s skull, placed deliberately on the highest point.
