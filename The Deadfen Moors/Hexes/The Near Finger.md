# The Near Finger

**Hex Label:** H.11
**Terrain Type:** Plains (with scrub)


## Main POI: The Near Finger
- The Near Finger is a bizarre, naturally formed sandstone spire rising abruptly from the plains, approximately 80 feet in height and tapering to a point resembling a skeletal hand. Erosion has sculpted bizarre, almost humanoid faces into the rock face.
- A small, perpetually damp cave entrance at the base of the Finger leads into a narrow passage approximately 100 feet long, ending in a small chamber containing smooth, polished stones arranged in a seemingly deliberate pattern.
- Local folklore claims the Finger was formed by a trapped god, eternally reaching for a lost lover, and that whispers can be heard within the passage if one listens intently.

## Additional POI 1: Dust Devil’s Scar
- A roughly circular depression, 100 feet in diameter, marks the site of frequent, intensely violent dust devils. The ground is perpetually churned and covered in fine, red sand.
- Scattered amongst the sand are fragments of blackened metal – suspected remnants of a caravan lost to the storms decades ago.
- The area is known for unsettling auditory hallucinations, often described as distant screams or whispered warnings.

## Additional POI 2: Old Man Hemlock’s Shack
- A crumbling, one-room structure built from sun-baked clay, partially buried in the scrub. It appears untouched for decades.
- A rusted wagon wheel lies beside the shack, alongside a collection of strangely shaped stones and dried herbs hanging to dry.
- Local rumors suggest Hemlock was a hermit and obsessive collector of "good luck charms," though no trace of him has ever been found.
