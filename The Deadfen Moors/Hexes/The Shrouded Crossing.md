# The Shrouded Crossing

**Hex Label:** [[The Shrouded Crossing|F.13]]
**Terrain Type:** Plains (with scrub)


## Main POI: The Shrouded Crossing
- [[The Shrouded Crossing]] is a crumbling stone bridge spanning the Whispering River, the only reliable crossing point through the hex. [[The Stone|The stone]] is perpetually damp and covered in a thick, grey moss, giving it a perpetually "shrouded" appearance.
- A small, ruined shrine dedicated to a forgotten river deity, known as "[[Silas the Pallid|Silas]] the Silent," sits just beyond the bridge’s western arch.  Evidence suggests offerings were made here for centuries, though no recent activity has been observed.
- The surrounding plains are characterized by windswept grasses and thorny scrub, creating a difficult and disorienting environment.  Visibility is often limited due to frequent, light mists rising from the river.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Hollow
- A naturally formed depression in the plains, known locally as Whisperwind Hollow, amplifies even the faintest sounds of the wind, creating an unsettling, murmuring effect.
- Small, sheltered caves within the hollow’s walls provide temporary refuge from the elements and are often occupied by scavenging birds.
- Signs of recent, hurried excavation are present near the northern edge, though the purpose of the digging is unknown.

## Additional POI 2: Stone’s Rest
- Stone’s Rest is a solitary, weathered standing stone, approximately 15 feet tall, situated roughly a mile east of [[The Shrouded Crossing|the Shrouded Crossing]]. It is worn smooth by the elements and shows no signs of deliberate carving.
- Locals believe [[The Stone|the stone]] possesses a protective aura, though its efficacy is debated. Travelers often leave small offerings – primarily feathers and smooth stones – at its base.
-  The area around Stone’s Rest is unusually fertile, supporting a small patch of wildflowers that blooms seasonally, a stark contrast to the surrounding scrub.
