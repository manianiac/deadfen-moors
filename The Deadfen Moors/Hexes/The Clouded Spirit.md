# The Clouded Spirit

**Hex Label:** [[The Clouded Spirit|N.12]]
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Clouded Spirit
- [[The Clouded Spirit]] is a crumbling stone shrine, partially buried in a windswept field of tall, amber grasses. Constructed during a forgotten age by a nomadic tribe known as the Whisperwind, it’s heavily weathered and covered in lichen, giving it a perpetually damp, almost spectral appearance.
- A persistent, swirling mist gathers around the shrine's central altar, often obscuring the carvings depicting winged figures and strange, geometric symbols. Locals claim the mist is the trapped essence of the shrine's former priests, attempting to warn travelers of danger.
- The shrine’s well, once used for ritual purification, is now filled with murky water and is rumored to occasionally emit a chilling, mournful wail, particularly during the full moon. Small, iridescent feathers are frequently found at the well’s edge.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Ridge
- A narrow, eroded ridge dominates the western edge of the hex, offering a panoramic view of the plains and a potential vantage point for observation.
- Scattered amongst the ridge's shale are remnants of Whisperwind campsites: collapsed stone hearths and broken pottery shards.
- Evidence suggests frequent, though infrequent, tribal gatherings occurred on the ridge, marked by small, intricately carved bone whistles.

## Additional POI 2: The Grey Stream
- The Grey Stream, a sluggish, grey-colored river, cuts a shallow channel across the hex’s southern border. Its banks are lined with reeds and cattails.
- The stream is unusually cold and slow-moving, and some reports indicate strange bioluminescent algae blooms along its length after heavy rainfall.
- Numerous animal tracks, including those of large predators and smaller [[The Copper-Grubbers|scavengers]], are frequently found along the stream’s banks.
