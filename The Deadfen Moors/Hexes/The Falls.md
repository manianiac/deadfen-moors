# The Falls

**Hex Label:** F.12
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Falls
- A towering, naturally formed waterfall dominates the hex, plunging approximately 80 feet into a clear, turquoise pool at the base. The sheer volume of water creates a constant, fine mist that hangs in the air.
- The surrounding plains are characterized by a unique, mineral-rich soil, staining the ground with streaks of ochre and amber, a result of the constant spray from the falls. Patches of stunted, phosphorescent moss thrive in the dampest areas.
- Scattered along the riverbanks are the crumbling ruins of what appears to be a small, abandoned shrine dedicated to a water deity. Stone carvings depict stylized aquatic creatures, worn smooth by centuries of weathering.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Glade
- A small, circular clearing sheltered by a grove of ancient willow trees; rumored to amplify whispers and faint sounds.
- Contains a single, unusually large and perfectly formed quartz crystal, radiating a subtle warmth.
- Tracks of several large, feline predators (possibly lynx or wildcats) have been recently observed in the glade.

## Additional POI 2: Stonecutter’s Ridge
- A series of layered sandstone cliffs rising approximately 30 feet, showcasing exposed strata of varying colours.
- Contains several small caves and overhangs offering limited shelter, frequently used by scavenging birds.
- Evidence of recent stone carving – a roughly hewn depiction of a serpent – was discovered near the base of the ridge.
