# Rock of the Near-Thief Titus

**Hex Label:** G.3
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: Rock of the Near-Thief Titus
- The rock formation is a solitary, wind-sculpted granite outcrop rising abruptly from the dense, moss-choked forest floor. Its surface is perpetually slick with a thin film of frost, regardless of the season, earning it the moniker “[[Titus|The Near-Thief]].”
- At the base of the rock lies a shallow cave, choked with thorny vines and the remains of a small, hastily constructed camp – evidence of [[Titus]]’s frequent, though ultimately unsuccessful, attempts to pilfer supplies from [[Anu|the God]]-Kings’ outposts.
- A faint, almost imperceptible trail winds away from the rock, disappearing into the deeper forest; believed to be the route [[Titus]] utilized to map the surrounding, abomination-infested wilds.



## Additional POI 1: Whispering Falls
- A small, perpetually mist-shrouded waterfall cascades down a cliff face adjacent to the forest edge. Locals claim [[The Falls|the falls]] murmur with [[Titus]]’s whispered warnings.
- The pool at the base of [[The Falls|the falls]] is unusually still and dark, rumored to hold a lingering trace of the strange energies emanating from the nearby abomination-infested region.
- Several skeletal remains, likely of unfortunate travelers, are partially submerged in the pool’s dark waters.

## Additional POI 2: The Twisted Grove
- This area of the forest is dominated by a cluster of ancient, gnarled trees, their branches twisted into grotesque shapes.
- Strange, iridescent fungi grow in abundance within the grove, emitting a faint, unsettling luminescence.
- A single, perfectly preserved halfling hunting knife lies amongst the roots of the largest tree – a possible relic from [[Titus]] himself.
