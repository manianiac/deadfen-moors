# The Red Ash

**Hex Label:** D.11
**Terrain Type:** Swamp


## Main POI: The Red Ash
- The Red Ash is a ruined fortress, partially submerged in the peat-stained waters of the hex. Constructed from dark, volcanic stone, the remaining walls are perpetually coated in a fine, crimson dust – the source of the hex’s name, derived from a long-forgotten volcanic eruption.
- Within the central courtyard, a perpetually smoldering fissure vents black smoke, occasionally erupting with jets of superheated ash. Strange, bioluminescent fungi thrive in the damp conditions around the fissure, casting an eerie, red glow.
- Scattered throughout the ruins are the skeletal remains of warriors clad in rusted plate armor, suggesting a prolonged and brutal siege or conflict that ended with the fortress’s destruction. Numerous traps, largely disarmed but still potentially lethal, remain active.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwood Cairns
- A collection of ancient stone cairns marking the burial grounds of a vanished tribe who worshipped the swamp’s spirits.
- Frequent unsettling whispers emanate from the cairns, attributed to restless spirits and the bog’s strange energies.
- The Cairns are overgrown with carnivorous pitcher plants and guarded by mutated, amphibian predators.

## Additional POI 2: Murkwater’s Hold
- A ramshackle, fortified hut built on a partially submerged platform, inhabited by a solitary, obsessive alchemist named Silas Blackwood.
- Blackwood specializes in extracting and manipulating toxins from the swamp’s flora and fauna for use in his volatile concoctions.
- His laboratory is filled with bubbling cauldrons, strange equipment, and a disconcerting collection of preserved specimens.
