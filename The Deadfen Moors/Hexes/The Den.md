# The Den

**Hex Label:** E.5
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Den
- The Den is a partially collapsed, stone structure built into a natural sandstone overhang, approximately 100 feet in height.  Its original purpose is unknown, though scorch marks and remnants of makeshift workshops suggest a past occupation involving metalworking and rudimentary alchemy.
- A small, stagnant pool of brackish water collects at the base of the overhang, attracting insects and occasionally larger reptiles. The pool is rumored to possess faintly iridescent properties under certain light conditions.
-  A pragmatic human trader, Silas Blackwood, maintains a rough-hewn cart and temporary shelter near the entrance to The Den, offering repairs, basic supplies, and rumors gleaned from travelers – for a price. 

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Ridge
- A natural formation of wind-sculpted sandstone pillars rises 50 feet from the plains to the north.
-  Strong winds constantly whistle through the pillars, producing a low, mournful sound.
-  Small flocks of carrion birds nest amongst the highest points.

## Additional POI 2: The Broken Cart
- A rusted iron cart lies overturned approximately half a mile east of The Den.
-  The wagon’s axle is snapped, and fragments of a leather-bound ledger are scattered amongst the wreckage.
-  Scavengers occasionally attempt to salvage usable metal, attracting opportunistic predators.
