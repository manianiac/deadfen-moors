# The Stalward Water

**Hex Label:** J.4
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Stalward Water
- The Stalward Water is a naturally formed amphitheater carved into the base of a sandstone cliff face. Water perpetually flows down the cliff, creating a constant, audible waterfall that gives the location its name. The water collects in a deep, dark pool at the bottom, fed by numerous smaller streams originating from higher in the hex.
- The amphitheater’s walls are covered in phosphorescent moss, brightest at night, creating an eerie glow within the pool and around the water’s edge. The moss is believed to possess minor healing properties.
- A partially submerged stone structure, resembling a ruined shrine, rests within the pool’s deepest section. Carvings depicting serpentine figures are visible beneath the water’s surface, hinting at a forgotten religion.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Grove
- A dense thicket of ancient, silver-barked trees dominates the western edge of the hex, perpetually rustling even in still air.
- Tracks of large, feline predators are frequently found within the grove, suggesting a territorial presence.
- Scattered amongst the trees are polished stones arranged in seemingly random patterns, possibly remnants of a past ritual site.

## Additional POI 2: Broken Bridge
- The remains of a crudely constructed stone bridge span a narrow, fast-flowing tributary within the hex’s northern region. It’s heavily damaged and appears to have collapsed recently.
- The area around the broken bridge is littered with the belongings of travelers – mostly tattered clothing and rusted metal scraps – suggesting a violent encounter.
- A small, meticulously crafted wooden flute was found lodged within the largest fallen stone, emitting a faint, mournful tone when exposed to the wind.
