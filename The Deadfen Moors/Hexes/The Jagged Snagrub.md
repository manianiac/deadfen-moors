# The Jagged Snagrub

**Hex Label:** E.14
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Jagged Snagrub
- The Jagged Snagrub is a grotesque, roughly humanoid goblin, approximately 8 feet tall, perpetually suspended 30 feet above the surrounding plains. Its flesh is a patchwork of obsidian-like plates fused with jagged, metallic protrusions, remnants of the Nabu anti-gravity technology it wields. 
- The goblin’s movements are erratic and unsettling, often accompanied by a low, grinding whine from the malfunctioning tech. It primarily hoards scavenged metal and scraps, occasionally launching these projectiles at anyone approaching within 100 feet. 
- The immediate area around the Snagrub is barren, scorched earth, a result of the device's unstable energy emissions.  Strange, crystalline growths, faintly glowing with a purple light, are scattered across the ground - likely a byproduct of the Nabu tech.



## Additional POI 1: The Rustwind Scar
- A deep ravine, carved by ancient, acidic rains, now choked with metallic debris and partially submerged.
- Several skeletal remains of smaller scavengers are embedded in the ravine walls, suggesting prolonged exposure to the area's hazardous conditions.
- The wind constantly howls through the ravine, carrying a distinctive metallic tang – “Rustwind”.

## Additional POI 2:  Echoing Stones
- A cluster of standing stones, arranged in a crude circle.  Whispers, seemingly originating from nowhere, can be heard when standing within the circle.
- The stones are covered in faded, glyph-like carvings - possibly related to the Nabu technology, though their meaning remains unclear.
- A small, nest of carrion birds has established itself amongst the stones, exhibiting unnaturally intelligent behavior.
