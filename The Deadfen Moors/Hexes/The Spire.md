# The Spire

**Hex Label:** O.8
**Terrain Type:** Farmland


## Main POI: The Spire
- [[The Spire]] is a colossal, obsidian structure piercing the farmland, approximately 150 feet tall and tapering to a needle-sharp point. Its surface is smooth and unnaturally cold to the touch, exhibiting no discernible seams or construction methods.
- The base of [[The Spire|the Spire]] is surrounded by a perfectly circular clearing, approximately 300 feet across, devoid of vegetation and marked by faintly glowing, silver runes etched into the soil. These runes shift subtly in color.
-  Local [[Rumors|rumors]] claim [[The Spire|the Spire]] pulses with a low-frequency energy, causing disorientation and vivid dreams in those who linger too long within its immediate vicinity.  A small, weathered shrine dedicated to a forgotten deity stands at [[The Spire|the Spire]]’s base, containing a single, cracked obsidian shard.

## Additional POI 1: Farmer Giles’s Field
- Farmer Giles cultivates a remarkably fertile, unusually vibrant wheat field surrounding [[The Spire|the Spire]]’s clearing – harvests are consistently double normal yields.
- Giles is a taciturn man, rarely speaking and offering little information about his methods or [[The Spire|the Spire]]'s influence. He possesses several unnervingly strong livestock.
-  Tracks of large, unidentified footprints frequently mar the edges of Giles's field, suggesting the presence of a significant, possibly dangerous, creature.

## Additional POI 2: Whisperwind Creek
- Whisperwind Creek flows eastward from a small, moss-covered spring just south of [[The Spire|the Spire]]’s clearing, known for its abnormally cold water and constant, unsettling whispers.
- The creek bed is lined with strangely shaped, grey stones that resonate faintly when struck, producing a mournful, wind-like sound.
-  Several partially submerged, rusted wagon wheels and remnants of clothing have been found along the creek bank, indicating a possible past tragedy or abandonment.
