# The Meadow of the Eye

**Hex Label:** K.3
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Meadow of the Eye
- The Meadow itself is a deceptively vast expanse of wildflowers – primarily sky-blue lupines, goldenrod, and crimson poppies – that stretches for nearly a mile across the hex. The soil is unusually rich and dark, retaining moisture exceptionally well, leading to a constant, humid haze.
- At the center of the meadow sits a circle of standing stones, roughly hewn and weathered, arranged in a deliberate pattern. Locals whisper they are aligned with celestial events, though no one understands their purpose.  Small, perfectly smooth stones are consistently found within the circle's perimeter.
- A slow-moving stream, known as the ‘Silvertear,’ flows through the northern edge of the meadow, emptying into a small, stagnant marsh. The water is unusually clear and reflects the sky with remarkable accuracy – hence the hex’s title.


## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Cairns
- Located 2 miles east of the Meadow, a collection of five weathered stone cairns mark ancient burial grounds.
- Locals claim the wind carries faint whispers from the dead when traversing the cairns.
- Archaeological surveys have found fragments of pottery and tools dating back nearly 800 years.

## Additional POI 2: Blackwood’s Watch
- Situated on the western edge of the hex, a crumbling stone watchtower offers a commanding view of the plains.
-  The tower’s well is dry, and the interior is filled with bat guano and the skeletal remains of a long-dead hound.
-  Evidence suggests the watchtower was abandoned abruptly, with signs of a desperate struggle present within the structure.
