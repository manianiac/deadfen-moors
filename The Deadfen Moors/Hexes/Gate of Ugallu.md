# Gate of Ugallu

**Hex Label:** [[Gate of Ugallu|J.11]]
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: Gate of Ugallu
- The Gate is a cyclopean archway carved directly from the cliff face, choked with black moss and slick with perpetual moisture. It is constructed from a dark, obsidian-like stone that seems to absorb light, and constantly emits a low, guttural hum.
- Within the archway lies a stagnant pool of viscous, crimson water - [[Ugallu’s Spring]] – said to be a conduit for [[Ugallu|the Demon]]’s corrupting influence. The air around the pool shimmers with heat and the scent of decay.
- Twisted, thorny vines, animated by dark magic, sprout from the base of the arch and throughout the surrounding forest, attempting to ensnare and drain the life force of any who approach. 

## Additional POI 1: Spire of Ugallu
- A crumbling, obsidian spire rises from the center of the hex, roughly 50 feet in height.  Scratches and deep gouges mar its surface, suggesting repeated attempts at destruction. 
- [[The Spire|The spire]] is perpetually wreathed in a swirling, violet mist, and emits flashes of dark energy.  It is believed to be a focus for [[Ugallu]]’s power and the site of [[Nabu]] rituals.
- Several skeletal figures, clad in rusted, [[Nabu]] armor, are chained to [[The Spire|the spire]], eternally guarding its secrets.

## Additional POI 2: Cliff of the Devil Ugallu
- The sheer cliff face dominates the western edge of the hex, a jagged scar against the forest canopy.  The rock is stained a deep, bruised purple, and small, pulsating fissures run along its surface.
-  Strange, unsettling whispers seem to emanate from the cliff, sometimes resolving into fragmented phrases in a guttural, demonic tongue.  The ground around the base of the cliff is barren and devoid of life.
- A natural rock formation resembling a clawed hand perpetually reaches upwards from the cliff face, appearing to grasp at the sky.  Touching it is said to bring misfortune and madness.
