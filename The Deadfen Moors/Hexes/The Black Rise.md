# The Black Rise

**Hex Label:** H.5
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Black Rise
- [[The Black Rise]] is a solitary, obsidian-black monolith, approximately 100 feet tall and 50 feet in diameter at its base, rising abruptly from the otherwise flat plains. Its surface is perfectly smooth, reflecting the sky with unsettling clarity.
- Local legends claim the monolith is a petrified tear of a forgotten god, formed in a cataclysmic event that reshaped the plains centuries ago. Strange magnetic anomalies frequently disrupt compasses and electronic devices near the base.
- A perpetual, swirling mist clings to the lower levels of the monolith, often punctuated by fleeting glimpses of shadowy figures – dismissed as optical illusions by most, but whispered about as the restless spirits of those who vanished near the Rise. 

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Creek
- A narrow, seasonal creek flows intermittently through a small, marshy area 2 miles north-east of [[The Black Rise|the Black Rise]], known for unsettling whispers carried on the wind.
- The creek bed contains numerous oddly shaped stones, polished smooth by the water, and frequent reports of small, perfectly formed animal skulls washed ashore.
- Native tribes avoid the area, believing the whispers to be warnings of impending misfortune.

## Additional POI 2: Stone Hearth
- Located 3 miles south of [[The Black Rise|the Black Rise]], Stone Hearth is the ruined remains of a pre-cataclysm settlement, consisting of crumbling stone dwellings and a collapsed central well.
- Excavations have uncovered fragments of intricately carved bone tools and pottery, suggesting a sophisticated culture predating the current era.
- The site is plagued by unsettling temperature drops, even on the warmest days, and the faint smell of woodsmoke persists regardless of the season.
