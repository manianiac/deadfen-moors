# The Tangle of the Muck

**Hex Label:** [[The Tangle of the Muck|H.10]]
**Terrain Type:** Plains (with scrub)


## Main POI: The Tangle of the Muck
- [[The Tangle of the Muck]] is a massive, ancient bog dominated by peat moss and twisted, water-logged trees. The air hangs thick with the smell of decay and damp earth. A perpetual, unsettling mist clings to the surface, reducing visibility to approximately 30 feet.
- At the heart of the bog lies the ruined keep of Lord Morian, a once-powerful warlord who vanished centuries ago. [[The Keep|The keep]] is partially submerged, with the lower levels choked by the mire. Stone walls are covered in moss and the occasional skeletal hand protrudes from the mud.
- Strange, luminescent fungi grow in abundance within [[The Tangle|the Tangle]], pulsing with a faint, sickly green light. Locals whisper these fungi are connected to the bog’s unnatural stillness and the lingering echoes of Morian’s tormented soul. 

## Additional POI 1: Widow’s Creek
- A shallow, perpetually dark creek winds through the western edge of the hex, fed by underground springs.
- Local folklore claims the creek is haunted by the drowned wife of a miller, whose mournful wails can be heard on still nights.
- Small fishing camps dot the creek’s bank, utilized primarily by trappers seeking edible fish and waterfowl.

## Additional POI 2: Stone Scar
- A naturally formed rock outcrop, blackened by centuries of peat accumulation, marks the northern boundary of the hex.
- Scars etched into [[The Stone|the stone]] suggest ancient rituals were once performed here.
- [[The Stone]] Scar offers a vantage point overlooking much of [[The Tangle of the Muck|the Tangle of the Muck]].
