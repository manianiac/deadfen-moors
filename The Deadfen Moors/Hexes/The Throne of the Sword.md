# The Throne of the Sword

**Hex Label:** [[The Throne of the Sword|I.12]]
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Throne of the Sword
- [[The Throne of the Sword]] is a colossal, naturally formed arch of obsidian rock, dominating the center of the hex. It stands approximately 80 feet tall and 60 feet wide, sculpted by centuries of wind and rain into the shape of a stylized throne. [[The Stone|The stone]] is perpetually slick with moisture, reflecting the surrounding forest in a distorted manner.
- Within the archway lies a small, circular clearing dominated by ancient, moss-covered roots that snake across the ground. These roots are believed to pulse with a faint magical energy, and remnants of elaborate, long-lost carvings depicting warriors and beasts can be seen etched into [[The Stone|the stone]]. 
- The area immediately surrounding the Throne is perpetually shrouded in a heavy mist, even on the brightest days. Locals whisper of restless spirits guarding the site, and a chilling wind always seems to emanate from within the arch.



## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Grove
- A small grove of towering, ancient pines, known for producing an exceptionally resonant timber. 
- Numerous unsettling, carved wooden animal figures are scattered throughout the grove, seemingly placed there intentionally.
- Travelers report hearing indistinct voices carried on the wind within the grove, though the source is never identified.

## Additional POI 2: Blackwater Creek
- Blackwater Creek flows from a subterranean source within the hex, characterized by its consistently dark, inky water.
- The creek is home to unusually large, aggressive trout and a species of blind cave salamander.
- The banks of the creek are covered in a thick layer of black, phosphorescent moss.
