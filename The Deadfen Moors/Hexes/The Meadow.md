# The Meadow

**Hex Label:** M.11
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Meadow
- The Meadow is a vast, undulating plain dominated by tall grasses of varying shades of green and gold. Seasonal wildflowers – primarily bluebells and poppies – bloom profusely during the warmer months, creating vibrant patches amidst the grass.
- A slow-moving, brackish stream, the Whisperwind Creek, bisects the hex, providing a vital source of water for wildlife and supporting a small population of waterfowl. The creek banks are rich with reeds and rushes.
- Scattered across the meadow are ancient, weathered standing stones – remnants of a long-lost civilization – arranged in seemingly random patterns, suggesting ritualistic significance. Erosion has softened the stones, blending them into the landscape.

## Additional POI 1: Stonecutter’s Hold
- A crumbling stone structure, partially collapsed, sits near the western edge of the hex, suggesting a former quarry or workshop.
- Evidence of recent mining activity – loose rock and shallow excavations – indicates ongoing, though limited, stone extraction.
- Small, hardy goats are frequently found scavenging amongst the stone piles.

## Additional POI 2: Silent Grove
- A dense cluster of ancient oak trees forms a small, secluded grove in the northern section of the hex.
- The ground beneath the oaks is unusually soft and damp, supporting a thick carpet of moss and ferns.
- Locals whisper of strange sounds emanating from the grove, particularly during the full moon.
