# The Lonely Enlil

**Hex Label:** E.7
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Lonely Enlil
- The Lonely Enlil is a windswept expanse of pale, cracked earth dominated by a colossal, crumbling statue of Enlil himself. The statue is carved from a dark, grey stone and perpetually coated in a thin layer of grey ash, giving it the appearance of ancient bone.
- A perpetual, mournful wind whispers across the plains, carrying faint echoes of battle cries and the clatter of steel. Travelers report a pervasive sense of isolation and despair in this area.
- Small, petrified trees, blackened and stunted, dot the landscape, remnants of a forest destroyed during the Scourge assault. The ground is riddled with shallow, dark pits – likely the burial sites of fallen soldiers.



## Additional POI 1: Ashfall Ridge
- A jagged ridge of volcanic rock rises sharply from the plains approximately 1 mile east of the statue.
- Scavengers frequent the ridge, drawn by the concentrated deposits of obsidian and the lingering traces of Scourge ichor.
- The air here is consistently thick with ash, obscuring visibility and carrying a bitter, sulfurous smell.



## Additional POI 2: The Silent Watch
- A ruined, partially collapsed watchtower stands approximately 3 miles north of the statue, clinging precariously to the edge of a small, dried-up riverbed.
- The tower's timbers are warped and covered in moss, suggesting it has been abandoned for centuries, though the wounds of the Scourge war are clearly etched into the stone.
- Few signs of life remain, save for a single, rusted banner depicting a stylized, weeping elf – a remnant of the garrison that once occupied the tower.
