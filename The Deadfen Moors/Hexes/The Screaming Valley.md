# The Screaming Valley

**Hex Label:** L.3
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Screaming Valley
- The Valley floor is a deceptively flat expanse of tall, amber grasses punctuated by clusters of stunted, silver birch trees. The air here is noticeably colder and carries a constant, low hum, intensifying to a high-pitched, unsettling whine – the source of the valley’s name.
- At the valley’s center stands the ruined remains of a stone watchtower, partially collapsed and overgrown with phosphorescent moss. Scratches, too deep and too regular to be natural, mar the stone surfaces, suggesting a prolonged and violent occupation.
- A brackish stream, the ‘Whisper’s Breath,’ cuts through the northern edge of the valley. The water glows faintly blue and is said to induce unsettling dreams in those who drink from it. Animal tracks are frequently found near the stream's edge, abnormally large and distorted.

## Additional POI 1: Greyrock Outcrop
- A solitary, weather-worn granite outcrop dominates the western edge of L.3, providing a defensive position.
- Scattered around the base are the bleached bones of numerous animals, mostly grazing species.
- A small, shallow cave offers potential shelter from the elements.

## Additional POI 2: Silent Grove
- Located approximately 2 miles southeast of the Valley, this small grove contains trees that seem to absorb all sound.
- The ground within the grove is unnaturally smooth and covered in a thick layer of violet lichen.
- Locals whisper that disturbing the grove invites a profound and unsettling silence.
