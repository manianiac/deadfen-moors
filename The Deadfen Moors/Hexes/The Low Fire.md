# The Low Fire

**Hex Label:** J.13
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Low Fire
- The Low Fire is a crumbling stone circle, approximately 30 feet in diameter, partially overgrown with moss and lichen.  Burnt stone remnants suggest a ritualistic burning was once practiced here, with traces of a dark, resinous residue still present.
- A sluggish stream, known as the Ember Creek, flows through the center of the circle, its water perpetually stained orange-brown due to the iron-rich soil. The creek banks are lined with stunted pines and ferns.
- Scattered around the circle are the skeletal remains of several wagon wheels and a solitary, rusted iron smith’s hammer, hinting at a possible long-abandoned settlement or a site of conflict.


## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Glade
- Located 2 miles northeast of The Low Fire, Whisperwind Glade is a small, naturally sheltered clearing known for unusually strong breezes.
- The glade contains a concentration of glowshrooms, providing a faint, ethereal light at night.
- Several weathered stone markers, partially submerged in the surrounding bog, suggest a forgotten druidic shrine.

## Additional POI 2: Blackroot Bog
- Situated 3 miles southwest of The Low Fire, Blackroot Bog is a treacherous expanse of peat and stagnant water.
- Thick, thorny blackroot vines dominate the landscape, creating a dense, disorienting environment.
-  A partially submerged, crumbling structure – believed to be a former smuggler's hideout – sits deep within the bog, accessible only by boat or treacherous wading.
