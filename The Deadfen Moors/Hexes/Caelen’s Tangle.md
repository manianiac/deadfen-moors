# Caelen’s Tangle

**Hex Label:** [[Caelen’s Tangle|H.12]]
**Terrain Type:** Plains (with scrub)


## Main POI: Caelen’s Tangle
- [[Caelen’s Tangle]] is a dense thicket of thorny scrub and stunted trees, perpetually shrouded in a clinging mist. The ground is uneven, riddled with deep, muddy hollows created by infrequent rainfall and the relentless trampling of unseen creatures. Twisted, ancient roots form natural barricades, making traversal difficult.
- At the heart of [[The Tangle|the tangle]] lies the shattered remains of a stone obelisk, intricately carved with glyphs depicting a kneeling warrior holding a radiant blade. The obelisk is partially consumed by the roots and vines, suggesting a long period of neglect and the influence of [[Scourge]].
- Scattered amongst [[The Tangle|the tangle]] are the crumbling foundations of what were once barracks – the skeletal remains of [[Caelen]]’s elite guard, frozen mid-stride, hinting at a desperate last stand against an unknown foe. 

## Additional POI 1: The Echoing Stone
- A naturally formed, smooth stone outcrop stands partially obscured within [[The Tangle|the Tangle]]’s southern edge, emitting a faint, unsettling echo of whispered voices.
- The echo shifts and changes, occasionally resembling [[Caelen]]’s commands or the cries of tormented souls.
-  Local legends claim touching [[The Stone|the stone]] grants a fleeting glimpse of the battle fought here, but at a terrible psychological cost.

## Additional POI 2: The Rotting Wheel
- A massive, rusted wheel lies embedded in the mud near the hex’s western border, covered in thick, pulsating fungal growth. 
- The wheel appears to be a component of some ancient, unknown machinery – its purpose lost to time and corruption.
-  The area surrounding the wheel is unusually devoid of life, and a sickly sweet odor permeates the air.
