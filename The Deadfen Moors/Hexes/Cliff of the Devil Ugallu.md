# Cliff of the Devil Ugallu

**Hex Label:** D.7
**Terrain Type:** Hills


## Main POI: Cliff of the Devil Ugallu
- The Cliffs of [[Ugallu|the Devil]] [[Ugallu]] are a jagged outcrop of black basalt rising abruptly from the surrounding hills, dominated by a sheer, 200-foot face perpetually slick with dark moss and viscous slime. The rock itself seems to writhe with faint, unsettling patterns beneath the surface.
- At the base of the cliffs, a narrow plateau is choked with twisted, petrified trees, remnants of a long-dead forest corrupted by [[Ugallu]]'s influence. The air here hangs thick with the scent of decay and a faint, metallic tang.
- Deep fissures scar the cliff face, leading to echoing caverns rumored to be the sites of ancient rituals performed by [[Anu|the Nabu]] in [[Ugallu]]’s service. Scattered amongst the rubble are shards of obsidian, pulsating faintly with dark energy.

## Additional POI 1: Ugallu’s Spring
- A stagnant pool of black water sits nestled within a depression in the hillside, perpetually shrouded in a sickly green mist. The water emits a low, guttural hum.
- Around [[The Spring|the spring]], the ground is barren, devoid of any vegetation save for clusters of iridescent fungi that appear to absorb ambient light.
- Small, skeletal remains—likely animal—are occasionally found submerged in the mud at [[The Spring|the spring]]’s edge, suggesting a source of sustenance for [[Ugallu]].

## Additional POI 2: Gate of Ugallu
- The Gate is a crumbling archway constructed from the same black basalt as the cliffs, partially collapsed and covered in grotesque carvings depicting scenes of torment and sacrifice.
- The entrance is perpetually wreathed in swirling shadows and the air feels noticeably colder within a 10-foot radius.  Whispers, seemingly emanating from [[The Stone|the stone]] itself, can be heard on still nights.
- A thin layer of crimson slime coats the floor of the gate's passage, pulsing faintly with the same dark energy as the obsidian shards found at the Cliffs.
