# The Ashen Cave

**Hex Label:** N.9
**Terrain Type:** Farmland


## Main POI: The Ashen Cave
- The Ashen Cave is a roughly circular depression carved into the limestone bedrock, approximately 100 feet in diameter and 60 feet deep. The cave mouth is partially blocked by a landslide of grey, ash-colored stone – hence the name.
- A constant, damp chill permeates the cave, regardless of outside temperature. Small, phosphorescent fungi cling to the walls, casting an eerie, blue-green glow. Water drips constantly, forming small, stagnant pools.
- Evidence of recent goblin activity is present - crude stone tools, scattered bones (primarily rabbits and birds), and a faint, lingering scent of mildew and something metallic.

## Additional POI 1: Farmer Silas’s Ridge
- Silas’s Ridge is a gently sloping field, cultivated with hardy wheat and barley. He’s known for his stubbornness and boasts a surprisingly effective scarecrow.
- The ridge benefits from a natural spring, providing consistently moist soil, though local superstitions claim the water carries a slight, unsettling taste.
- A ruined stone well sits at the ridge’s highest point, overgrown with vines and rumored to be haunted by a drowned shepherd.

## Additional POI 2: The Broken Wheel
- The Broken Wheel is a moss-covered stone circle, clearly the remains of a forgotten shrine or gathering place.
- Fragments of pottery and worn animal carvings are scattered amongst the stones. A single, rusted iron wheel lies embedded in the center.
- Locals whisper that the wheel marks the spot where a local bandit was killed centuries ago and his restless spirit still lingers.
