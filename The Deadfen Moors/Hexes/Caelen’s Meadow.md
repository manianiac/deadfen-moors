# Caelen’s Meadow

**Hex Label:** A.6
**Terrain Type:** Mountains


## Main POI: Caelen’s Meadow
- Caelen’s Meadow is a deceptively serene expanse nestled within the jagged peaks of the Dragon’s Tooth Mountains. The terrain is primarily open grassland, surprisingly fertile thanks to mineral-rich runoff from the surrounding mountains, punctuated by stands of resilient, silver-leaved juniper.
- The meadow retains the imprint of Caelen’s engineered defense systems; subtle, geometric stone markers, partially overgrown with moss, still delineate patrol routes and strategic firing positions. These markers are unusually smooth and cold to the touch.
- A small, perfectly circular pool, known as the ‘Hero’s Reflection,’ sits at the center of the meadow. The water is unnaturally still and reflects not the sky, but a fleeting, distorted image of Caelen himself, visible only during moments of intense emotional resonance.



## Additional POI 1: The Stone Sentinel
- Located 1 mile east of the Meadow’s edge, a colossal, partially collapsed stone statue depicting Caelen stands as a silent guardian.
- The Sentinel’s eyes are empty sockets, rumored to occasionally emit a faint, ethereal blue light when Scourge are nearby.
- Scavenged weaponry and broken armor fragments litter the base, evidence of past battles and the relentless erosion of time.

## Additional POI 2: The Tangle
- Situated 3 miles north of Caelen’s Meadow, the Tangle is a dense, perpetually shadowed thicket of thorned vines and twisted trees.
- This area is heavily influenced by the residual energies of Caelen's experimentation, causing disorienting illusions and amplifying negative emotions.
-  A small, collapsed shrine dedicated to a forgotten nature deity lies deep within the Tangle’s heart, hinting at a conflict between Caelen’s purpose and the land itself.
