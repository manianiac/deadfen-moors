# The Copper Skull

**Hex Label:** H.4
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Copper Skull
- [[The Copper Skull]] is a partially collapsed, circular stone structure approximately 30 feet in diameter, situated at the base of a prominent, moss-covered hill. The walls are constructed of dark grey granite, heavily eroded and covered in lichen. A single, arched doorway remains intact, leading into a darkened interior.
- Within the Skull’s central chamber sits the namesake – a life-sized, meticulously crafted skull cast in solid copper. The skull constantly emits a faint, rhythmic pulse of warmth and a subtle, unsettling hum. Touching the skull induces vivid, often disturbing, dreams.
- Strange fungal growths, exhibiting iridescent colors and emitting a phosphorescent glow, dominate the interior and immediately surrounding area. These fungi appear to react to proximity to the skull, pulsing with increased intensity. 

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Glade
- A small, perpetually misty glade containing a circle of standing stones. Locals claim to hear whispered voices emanating from the stones during strong winds.
- Numerous species of rare, bioluminescent flora thrive within the Glade, attracting nocturnal insects.
- A freshwater spring feeds into the Glade, the water possessing a faintly metallic taste.

## Additional POI 2: Blackwood’s Watch
- A ruined, crumbling watchtower constructed from dark, treated wood, partially integrated into the hillside. Evidence suggests a prolonged period of occupation.
- Scavenged remains of a charcoal burner’s pit are located nearby, along with the skeletal remains of a small dog.
- [[The Tower|The tower]] offers a strategic vantage point overlooking the Skull’s immediate vicinity.
