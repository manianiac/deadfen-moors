# Crater of the Frozen Dagger

**Hex Label:** N.6
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: Crater of the Frozen Dagger
- The crater itself is a perfectly circular depression, approximately 1.5 miles in diameter, filled with a sheet of ice that rarely thaws, even during the briefest summers. The ice is a startling, almost unnaturally deep blue, and exhibits a subtle, rhythmic pulsing glow visible only during the darkest hours.
- At the crater’s center stands the Obsidian Spire, a jagged, obsidian monolith rising approximately 80 feet from the ice. It is perpetually covered in frost and emits a low, mournful hum that can induce feelings of intense dread or chilling calm.
- Scattered around the crater's edge are the remains of a forgotten fortress, constructed from a grey, porous stone.  The structures are mostly collapsed, but some partially intact walls and a central keep retain a menacing appearance, hinting at a catastrophic event.


## Additional POI 1: Whispering Cairns
- Located 2 miles east of the crater, these fifteen stone cairns are arranged in a rough circle.  Wind passing through the cairns creates an eerie, whispering sound, said to be the voices of the fallen warriors who once defended the fortress.
- The cairns are consistently cold to the touch, and small, intricate carvings depicting stylized daggers can be found on several of their surfaces.
- A small, sheltered hollow exists within the circle of cairns, containing a single, perfectly preserved raven skull.



## Additional POI 2: Frostfang Riverbend
- The Frostfang River abruptly narrows and curves to flow directly into the eastern edge of the crater.  The water is intensely cold and has a faintly blue tint.
- The riverbed is choked with jagged, ice-covered rocks, creating treacherous footing.
- Animal tracks – primarily large, clawed footprints – are frequently found along the riverbank, suggesting the presence of a significant predator.
