# Ditch of the Hidden Fire

**Hex Label:** K.12
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: Ditch of the Hidden Fire
- The Ditch is a naturally formed ravine, approximately 100 feet deep and 300 feet long, carved into the forest floor by an ancient river. Black, obsidian-like rock lines the walls, radiating a faint, perpetual heat. 
-  Within the deepest section of the Ditch, a small, constantly burning geyser erupts, spewing forth phosphorescent blue flames and a thick, sulfurous mist. The heat is intense, capable of scorching exposed skin within 10 feet.
-  Scattered along the ravine walls are numerous petrified animal remains – primarily large predators – suggesting the geyser's heat has been a threat for centuries. Crude, stone carvings depicting serpentine figures are etched into some of the rock faces.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Grove
- A dense cluster of ancient, towering redwoods known for producing a constant, low hum when the wind passes through their branches – rumored to carry whispers of the forest spirits.
- The Grove is home to a colony of giant, iridescent beetles, drawn to the energy emanating from the Ditch. 
-  Several abandoned, circular stone platforms are partially submerged in a perpetually damp area within the Grove.

## Additional POI 2: Stonecutter’s Scar
- A narrow, exposed quarry containing veins of grey quartz, favored by local artisans for its clarity and resistance to weathering. 
- The quarry is marked by the partially collapsed remains of a rudimentary stone-cutting operation, suggesting a brief, forgotten settlement.
- A lone, weathered cairn stands at the entrance, bearing a single, crudely carved wolf’s head.
