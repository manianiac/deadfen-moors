# Field of Sir Galen the Bright

**Hex Label:** C.4
**Terrain Type:** Hills


## Main POI: Field of Sir Galen the Bright
- [[The Field]] is a remarkably flat, grassy expanse stretching six miles across rolling hills, dominated by the skeletal remains of Galen’s Adaptive Defense Systems – towering, silver-plated spires and fractured energy conduits that still faintly pulse with residual power.
- Within [[The Field|the field]]’s center lies the ‘Nexus’, a perfectly preserved circular platform of polished silver, radiating a noticeable warmth.  Scattered across the Nexus are deactivated [[Nabu]] control nodes displaying holographic projections of [[Sir Galen]] issuing commands.
- The soil here is unnaturally fertile, yielding crops of unusually large and vibrant colors.  Localized ‘Bright Zones’ exist, areas where [[Anu|the Nabu]] technology’s influence manifests as accelerated plant growth and minor energy fluctuations.


## Additional POI 1: Echo Chamber
-  A naturally formed cave system located on the western edge of [[The Field|the Field]], exhibiting strange acoustic properties – whispers and fragmented commands seemingly emanating from the rock.
-  Scattered within the caves are crude, hastily constructed shrines dedicated to [[Sir Galen]], adorned with scavenged [[Nabu]] components.
-  Evidence suggests a small, fanatical cult, the "Silver Echoes," still operates within the caves, attempting to decipher the Nexus's signals.


## Additional POI 2: Rustfall Scar
- A deep ravine running east-west through the northern portion of [[The Field|the Field]], carved by a catastrophic failure of a [[Nabu]] terraforming construct.
- The ravine floor is coated in a thick layer of reddish-brown rust – the byproduct of the construct's unstable energy discharge.
-  Automated defense units, partially melted and severely damaged, remain active within the Scar, sporadically firing energy blasts.
