# City of the Ancient Heart

**Hex Label:** G.7
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: City of the Ancient Heart
- The city, Veridian, is built around a massive, pulsating geode – the ‘Ancient Heart’ – that radiates a faint, warm light and is the source of the city’s unique restorative properties. Structures are primarily constructed from a pale, moss-covered stone harvested from the surrounding plains.
- Veridian’s population, primarily Veridianites, are known for their longevity and unusually vibrant health, attributed to the Heart’s influence. Their society is rigidly structured around the Heart’s maintenance and study.
- The city is divided into concentric rings: the ‘Keep’ surrounding the Heart, the ‘Gardens’ cultivating the Heart’s favored flora, and the ‘Outer Ward’ comprised of artisan workshops and modest dwellings.

## Additional POI 1: Whispering Cairns
- A series of twelve weathered stone cairns dot the northern plains, each marked with strange, spiraling glyphs. Locals claim they whisper prophecies to those who listen closely.
- The cairns are believed to be ancient burial sites of a nomadic tribe, the Skyweavers, who worshipped the plains and possessed rudimentary geomantic abilities.
- Recent tremors have disturbed the cairns, leading to unsettling visions and erratic weather patterns in the immediate area.

## Additional POI 2: The Obsidian Scar
- A deep, jagged fissure stretches across the eastern plains, filled with glassy, black obsidian formed by a long-dormant volcanic event.
- The Scar is home to a colony of Razorwings – large, predatory birds with obsidian-like feathers – known for their aggressive territoriality.
- Scattered within the Scar are fragments of what appear to be a technologically advanced civilization, hinting at a pre-Veridian presence.
