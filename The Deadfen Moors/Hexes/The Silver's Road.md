# The Silver's Road

**Hex Label:** J.10
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Silver's Road
- The Silver's Road is a partially collapsed, paved road constructed from silver-veined shale, remnants of a long-lost civilization known as the Sylvani. The road stretches approximately 3 miles from the hex’s northern edge towards a cluster of standing stones.
- The settlement of Oakhaven, a hardy farming community of roughly 80 souls, has grown around the road’s southern terminus, reliant on trade and the occasional traveler seeking shelter or repair. They primarily cultivate wheat and barley.
- A ruined Sylvani watchtower, constructed of the same silver shale, stands 1 mile east of the road, exhibiting signs of a recent, though poorly understood, magical disturbance – faint shimmering residue and displaced flora.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Cairn
- A naturally formed stone cairn, sculpted by centuries of winds, sits 2 miles south of the Silver's Road. Locals believe it holds the spirits of lost travelers.
- Strange, melodic whispers are occasionally reported emanating from the cairn, particularly during the full moon.
- Scavengers regularly search the cairn for Sylvani artifacts, leading to occasional territorial disputes with Oakhaven’s militia.

## Additional POI 2: The Bone Orchard
- A small, unsettling grove of skeletal trees – the “Bone Orchard” – occupies a 1-mile radius directly west of The Silver’s Road. The trees are composed of a petrified wood similar to the road’s shale.
- Ritualistic markings, resembling stylized animal skulls, are etched into the bases of the trees. Evidence suggests a forgotten, brutal cult practiced here.
- The soil in the Orchard is unusually barren, and attempts to grow crops have repeatedly failed.
