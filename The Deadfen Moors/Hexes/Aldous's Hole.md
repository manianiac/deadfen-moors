# Aldous's Hole

**Hex Label:** H.6
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: Aldous’s Hole
- The hex is dominated by a circular depression, approximately one mile in diameter, known as Aldous’s Hole. The ground within is a sickly grey-green, perpetually slick with a viscous, faintly luminescent fluid.
- Twisted, obsidian-like formations jut from the edges of the hole, remnants of Aldous’s failed attempts to stabilize dimensional rifts. Strange, discordant whispers emanate from the depths, said to be the echoes of trapped realities.
- A single, petrified tree, known as the “Silent Witness,” stands at the hole’s center, its branches reaching upwards like skeletal fingers. It is impervious to all damage and radiates a palpable sense of sorrow.

## Additional POI 1: The Echoing Stones
- A cluster of standing stones, arranged in a rough circle 1/2 mile south of the hole. They vibrate faintly with residual magic.
- The stones are covered in complex, glowing glyphs that shift and change, said to be fragments of Aldous's knowledge.
- Locals avoid the stones, believing they attract unwanted attention from the entities trapped within Aldous's Hole.

## Additional POI 2: The Cartographer’s Cairn
- Located 2 miles northeast, this small cairn marks the spot where Aldous’s initial expeditions began.
- Scattered around the cairn are the rusted remnants of Aldous’s surveying equipment and numerous, meticulously drawn maps depicting the surrounding area.
-  A layer of polished obsidian, seemingly unnaturally smooth, covers the base of the cairn – a possible point of further instability.
