# Keep of Caelen

**Hex Label:** L.11
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: Keep of Caelen
- The Keep of Caelen dominates the plains, a weathered stone structure built around a central, reinforced tower. Constructed during Caelen’s reign, it’s primarily composed of dark grey basalt, exhibiting signs of extensive repairs and battle scarring.
- Within the Keep's walls, remnants of Caelen’s personal quarters—a grand hall, barracks, and a small, surprisingly well-maintained library—offer glimpses into his life and the order he established. The library contains fragmented records detailing Scourge sightings and hunting strategies.
- A collapsed section of the southern wall reveals the site of a major battle against a particularly large Scourge, marked by a circular pit filled with shattered stone and the lingering scent of corruption.



## Additional POI 1: Caelen’s Meadow
- A wide, dew-kissed expanse of wildflowers, perpetually shielded from direct sunlight by the Keep’s walls. The soil here is unusually fertile, rumored to be blessed by Caelen's presence.
- Scattered across the meadow are the skeletal remains of Scourge, partially decayed and often adorned with crude, ritualistic markings.
- A standing stone circle within the meadow is believed to be a focal point for Caelen’s energy, occasionally emitting a faint, rhythmic pulse.

## Additional POI 2: Caelen’s Tangle
- A dense, thorny thicket surrounding the Keep’s perimeter, deliberately cultivated as a natural barrier and hunting ground. The thorns are abnormally resilient and possess a minor, irritating toxin.
- Within the Tangle’s deepest recesses lies a partially submerged shrine dedicated to Caelen, containing a tarnished silver spear – one of his favored weapons – and several worn banners bearing his symbol.
- The Tangle serves as a breeding ground for smaller, less dangerous Scourge, drawn to the shrine’s lingering energy.
