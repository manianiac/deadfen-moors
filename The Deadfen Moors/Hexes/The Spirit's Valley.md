# The Spirit's Valley

**Hex Label:** G.2
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Spirit's Valley
- The Valley floor is a bowl of exceptionally dense, ancient forest dominated by towering redwoods and silver firs, perpetually shrouded in mist. A permanent, unnatural twilight clings to the lowest reaches.
- A network of shallow, moss-covered streams crisscrosses the valley floor, rumored to carry the spirits of those lost within the forest. The water is unusually cold and possesses a faintly luminescent quality.
- At the valley’s center stands a ruined stone circle, overgrown with vines and partially collapsed. Strange, intricate carvings depicting stylized animal and humanoid figures adorn the remaining stones.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Falls
- A series of tiered waterfalls cascading down the eastern slope of the hex, perpetually creating a low, mournful hum.
- Locals claim the water possesses minor healing properties but carries a faint auditory hallucination – whispers of forgotten memories.
- The base of the falls conceals a small, dark cave entrance, seldom visited due to the intense spray and unsettling atmosphere.

## Additional POI 2: Old Man Hemlock’s Clearing
- A relatively open area within the forest, centered around a massive, ancient hemlock tree – Hemlock himself - marked with dozens of small, weathered totems.
- Hemlock is a recurring figure in local folklore, described as a hermit and a protector of the forest’s balance, though his presence is increasingly rare.
- A crumbling, stone-lined well sits partially obscured by the hemlock’s roots; its purpose remains unknown, but offerings of food and small trinkets are frequently left nearby.
