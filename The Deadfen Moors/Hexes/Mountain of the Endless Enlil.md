# Mountain of the Endless Enlil

**Hex Label:** H.15
**Terrain Type:** Mountains


## Main POI: Mountain of the Endless Enlil
- The mountain’s peak is perpetually shrouded in a swirling, grey mist, said to be the lingering essence of Enlil’s tormented spirit. Jagged, obsidian-like rock formations dominate the higher slopes, sculpted by centuries of wind and what some believe are the echoes of his battle cries.
- At the base of the mountain lies a shattered fortress, once a vital observation post, now little more than crumbling walls and scattered weaponry – remnants of the last stand against the Scourge. The air here is noticeably colder, and a constant, mournful wind whistles through the ruined structures.
- Within a deep, glacial fissure halfway up the eastern face is a naturally formed shrine – a shallow pool of perpetually freezing water ringed by smooth, grey stones. It is believed to be where Enlil’s spirit first solidified, marked by a faint, spectral glow.



## Additional POI 1: The Silent Watcher
- A solitary, weathered stone statue depicting a robed elf stands overlooking the fortress ruins, perpetually tilted as if observing the landscape.
- The statue is strangely cold to the touch and emits a subtle, unsettling humming noise.
- Locals claim the statue shifts its gaze slightly when observed directly, a manifestation of Enlil’s lingering vigilance.

## Additional POI 2: Abominant Scar
- A deep, ragged gash in the mountainside marks the site of the most intense battle against the Scourge. The ground is scorched black and littered with bone fragments.
- Strange, pulsating black fungi grow in the shadows of the scar, emitting a sickly sweet odor.
- Investigating the scar risks attracting the attention of residual Abominant energies, manifesting as brief, distorted visions.
