# Den of King Clovis the Lonely

**Hex Label:** G.13
**Terrain Type:** Plains (with scrub)


## Main POI: Den of King Clovis the Lonely
- The Den is a crumbling stone structure, once a magnificent palace, now largely consumed by scrub and wind-blown grasses. Massive, fractured columns still stand, hinting at its former glory.
- Within the central chamber, a throne remains – cracked and covered in dust, bearing a single, tarnished crown. The air feels strangely heavy, imbued with a profound sense of sorrow and loss.
- Scattered throughout the ruins are personal effects: faded tapestries depicting heroic battles, broken weapons, and numerous, meticulously crafted, but now worthless, gold coins.  Scavengers frequent the area.



## Additional POI 1: Whispering Cairns
- Three weathered stone cairns rise from the plains, each marked with faded glyphs – believed to be prayers to forgotten deities.
- Locals claim the cairns whisper secrets to those who listen closely, though the whispers are often disjointed and unsettling.
- The cairns are a site of minor ritualistic activity, primarily by hermits seeking communion with the past.


## Additional POI 2: The Scarred Well
- A deep, dark well sits at the hex’s northern edge, unnaturally still and surrounded by a circle of blackened earth.
- The well is choked with debris and the water is perpetually murky, rumored to reflect not the sky, but visions of the past – particularly of King Clovis’s downfall.
- Strange, sickly plant life thrives around the well, exhibiting accelerated growth and exhibiting unusual bioluminescence at night.
