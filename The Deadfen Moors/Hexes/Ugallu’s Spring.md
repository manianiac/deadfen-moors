# Ugallu’s Spring

**Hex Label:** E.11
**Terrain Type:** Swamp


## Main POI: Ugallu’s Spring
- The hex centers around a stagnant pool, roughly 100 feet across and 30 feet deep, choked with black algae and perpetually shrouded in a sickly green mist. The water itself seems to absorb light, and a constant, low hum vibrates from its depths.
- Scattered around the spring's edge are the petrified remains of several humanoid figures, twisted into grotesque poses, suggesting a violent demise. Many are coated in a thick, tar-like substance that smells of sulfur and decay.
- A crumbling stone altar, partially submerged in the water, bears the crude carvings of a horned, demonic figure – believed to be Ugallu himself – and is consistently slick with a viscous, crimson fluid.


## Additional POI 1: Cliff of the Devil Ugallu
- Jagged obsidian cliffs dominate the western edge of the hex, rising abruptly from the swamp. The rock is scarred with deep gouges, likely created by immense, clawed feet.
- A narrow, treacherous ledge runs along the cliff face, accessible only during periods of exceptionally low water. The air here is noticeably colder and carries a faint scent of ozone.
- Several small caves and alcoves are carved into the cliff face, some containing crude shrines adorned with bones and withered offerings.



## Additional POI 2: Gate of Ugallu
- Located approximately two miles to the north, a colossal, rusted iron gate stands partially buried in the swamp mud.  Twisted vines and thorny plants aggressively cover the gate's surface.
- The gate is adorned with disturbing bas-reliefs depicting scenes of torment and sacrifice, and emits a subtle, unsettling aura.  Attempts to open it have consistently failed, regardless of force or magic.
- A thick layer of iridescent slime coats the gate's hinges, and the air around it crackles with barely contained dark energy.
