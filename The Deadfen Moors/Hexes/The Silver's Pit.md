# The Silver's Pit

**Hex Label:** C.7
**Terrain Type:** Hills


## Main POI: The Silver’s Pit
- The Silver’s Pit is a naturally formed sinkhole, approximately 150 feet deep and 80 feet in diameter, filled with stagnant, unusually silver-colored water. The water is consistently tepid and emits a faint, metallic scent.
- Around the pit’s edge, partially collapsed remains of what appears to be a hastily abandoned mining camp are scattered. Evidence suggests a small, independent operation focused on extracting a rare, silvery mineral known locally as “Moonstone Dust.”
- Strange, bioluminescent fungi grow in the deepest shadows of the pit, pulsing with a soft, blue light. Locals whisper of unsettling sounds emanating from within – low growls and what sounds like weeping.

## Additional POI 1: Widow’s Watch
- A crumbling stone watchtower, overlooking the western edge of the hex, offering a panoramic view of the surrounding hills.
-  [[The Tower|The tower]]’s lower levels are riddled with rodent infestation and the upper levels are partially collapsed, making ascent dangerous.
-  Scattered around the base are the skeletal remains of a mounted guard, suggesting a long-ago and violent defense of the area.

## Additional POI 2: Whispering Cairns
- A collection of ancient stone cairns, spaced roughly 50 yards apart, forming a rough, circular pattern near the hex’s center.
-  The cairns are covered in faded, pictographic carvings depicting stylized beasts and celestial bodies. Wind consistently blows across the cairns, producing a low, mournful whistle.
-  Local legends claim the cairns are a burial ground for a forgotten tribe and react to the presence of those with strong emotions.
