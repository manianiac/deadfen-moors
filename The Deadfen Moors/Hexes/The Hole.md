# The Hole

**Hex Label:** [[The Hole|E.13]]
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Hole
- [[The Hole]] is a perfectly circular depression, approximately 300 feet in diameter and 150 feet deep, located in the center of the hex. The walls are smooth, obsidian-like rock, exhibiting no signs of erosion or weathering despite the age of the surrounding plains.
- Strange, low-frequency humming emanates from the bottom of [[The Hole|the Hole]], detectable within 100 feet of the rim. Prolonged exposure (over 15 minutes) induces a mild sense of unease and disorientation in most individuals.
- Scattered around the rim of [[The Hole|the Hole]] are the bleached bones of numerous animals – primarily deer, rabbits, and birds – arranged in unsettling spirals, suggesting a deliberate pattern. No human remains have ever been found.

## Additional POI 1: Dust Devil’s Tooth
- A solitary, wind-sculpted sandstone spire rises 50 feet from the plains to the north of [[The Hole|the Hole]].
- Locals believe [[The Spire|the spire]] marks a convergence point for strong winds and is associated with misfortune.
- Small deposits of shimmering, metallic dust cling to [[The Spire|the spire]]’s surface.

## Additional POI 2: The Broken Cart
- Partially buried in the tall grass to the east of [[The Hole|the Hole]] lies the remains of a wooden cart, overturned and heavily damaged. 
- Only a single, rusted iron wheel remains, stained with a dark, viscous substance.
- Tracks lead away from the cart, disappearing into the scrubland, suggesting a hasty departure.
