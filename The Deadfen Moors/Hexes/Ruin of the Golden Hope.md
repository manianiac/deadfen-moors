# Ruin of the Golden Hope

**Hex Label:** J.2
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: Ruin of the Golden Hope
- The central ruin is a crumbling sandstone structure, approximately 150 feet in height, dominated by a massive, fractured dome once painted gold – hence the name. Weathering and erosion have stripped much of the gold, leaving only faint traces.
- Scattered around the main structure are the remains of what appear to be workshops and living quarters, constructed from similar sandstone and showing signs of hasty abandonment. Several collapsed walls reveal remnants of intricate mosaics depicting stylized birds and what appear to be celestial events.
- A shallow, stagnant pool – known locally as “The Reflection” – sits within the ruined dome’s base. The water is unnaturally dark and possesses a faint, oily sheen. Locals avoid it, claiming it reflects not the sky, but visions of the future. 

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Grasslands
- Vast, undulating plains covered in tall, golden grasses that constantly whisper in the wind, carrying faint, unintelligible voices.
- Numerous herds of shaggy, six-legged grazers, known as “Windstriders,” roam the grasslands, offering a potential source of food and hides.
- The grasslands are sparsely populated with thorny scrub and isolated, skeletal trees, remnants of a long-gone forest.

## Additional POI 2: Obsidian Creek
- A narrow, fast-flowing creek cutting through the plains, its bed composed of polished obsidian rock.
- The creek’s waters are exceptionally cold and possess a slight metallic taste. Fishermen occasionally catch small, bioluminescent fish within it.
- A small, almost perfectly circular depression surrounds the creek, exhibiting a faint, residual heat signature.
