# The Sharp Crater

**Hex Label:** H.7
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Sharp Crater
- The crater itself is a perfectly circular depression, approximately 300 feet in diameter and 80 feet deep. The walls are composed of blackened, glassy rock, exhibiting minimal erosion despite the exposed nature of the material. Analysis suggests a relatively recent impact event, approximately 300-400 years prior to the current timeline.
- A constant, low-frequency hum emanates from within the crater, perceptible only at close range. Local folklore claims the sound is the "voice of [[The Stone|the stone]]," warning against disturbing the site. Small, crystalline formations, dubbed "Echo Shards," are frequently found scattered around the rim.
- The surrounding plains are dominated by a rust-colored, alkali soil, sparsely populated with tough, wind-swept grasses. Animal life is limited; primarily hardy rodents and scavenging birds adapted to the harsh conditions. A small, perpetually shifting sandstorm often whips across the crater’s lip.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Ridge
- A narrow ridge of sandstone formations rises abruptly from the plains to the north of the crater, named for the whistling sound the wind makes as it passes through the gaps.
- Several small, abandoned mining camps are nestled within the ridge, remnants of a failed attempt to extract rare minerals from [[The Stone|the stone]].
- The ridge provides a vantage point overlooking the crater and surrounding plains, offering a strategic advantage.

## Additional POI 2: Bone Orchard
- A circular area approximately one mile east of the crater is characterized by a dense concentration of bleached animal bones – primarily large herbivores.
- The soil in this area is unusually rich in phosphorus, and a sickly sweet odor permeates the air.
- Local tribes avoid this area, believing it to be haunted by restless spirits.
