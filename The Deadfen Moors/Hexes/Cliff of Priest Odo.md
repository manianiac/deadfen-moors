# Cliff of Priest Odo

**Hex Label:** G.8
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: Cliff of Priest Odo
- The Cliff of Priest Odo is a sheer limestone face rising abruptly from the surrounding plains, approximately 150 feet at its highest point. Erosion has carved a shallow plateau at its summit, offering a precarious but defensible vantage point.
- Scattered around the base of the cliff are the remnants of Odo's temple – crumbling basalt foundations, fragmented mosaic floors depicting stylized combat scenes, and corroded metallic components consistent with Nabu technology. These components occasionally emit a faint, residual energy pulse.
- A narrow, winding staircase, partially collapsed, leads upwards to the plateau. The stairs are slick with moisture and heavily scarred, suggesting frequent attempts to scale the cliff and the presence of aggressive wildlife.



## Additional POI 1: Sunken Shrine of Ky’than
- Located 2 miles south of the Cliff, a partially submerged shrine dedicated to the aquatic Nabu deity Ky’than.
- The shrine is accessible only during low tide, revealing intricate carvings depicting swirling currents and monstrous sea creatures.
- Local legends claim Ky’than gifted Odo with a device for amplifying his "Bright" abilities, now partially consumed by the shrine’s waters.

## Additional POI 2: Obsidian Vein
- Situated 4 miles east of the Cliff, a vein of exceptionally pure obsidian cuts through the plains, forming a jagged, dark scar.
- The vein is surprisingly warm to the touch, radiating a subtle energy that disrupts electronic devices.
- Small, crystalline formations resembling shattered Nabu lenses are embedded within the obsidian.
