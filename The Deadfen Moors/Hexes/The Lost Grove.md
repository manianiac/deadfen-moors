# The Lost Grove

**Hex Label:** J.8
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Lost Grove
- The Grove is a naturally formed circle of ancient, towering cypress trees, averaging 80-120 feet in height, clustered around a still, obsidian-black pool. The trees exhibit bioluminescent moss covering their lower trunks and branches, casting an eerie green glow at night.
- At the center of the pool lies a submerged stone altar, partially covered in algae and bearing faint carvings depicting serpentine figures. The water within is unnaturally cold and possesses a slight, unsettling shimmer.
- Local folklore speaks of the Grove as a place of forgotten rituals, once a meeting point for a nomadic tribe who worshipped the ‘Silent Watchers’ – beings said to reside within the trees. Evidence of hastily abandoned campsites and scattered, intricately woven baskets are frequently found.

## Additional POI 1: Whispering Cairns
- A collection of seven weathered stone cairns marking ancient burial grounds, situated approximately 1.5 miles northeast of the Grove.
- The wind consistently whistles through the cairns, producing a low, mournful sound that some locals believe carries the voices of the dead.
- Recent excavations revealed skeletal remains, notably a disproportionately large skull, suggesting a possible cranial deformation ritual.

## Additional POI 2: Blackwater Creek
- A slow-moving creek meandering roughly 3 miles south of the Grove, known for its unusually dark, almost black water.
- The creek bed is lined with smooth, grey stones and hosts a population of blind cavefish.
- Patches of luminous fungi thrive along the creek banks, reacting violently to bright light sources.
