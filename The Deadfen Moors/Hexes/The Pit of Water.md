# The Pit of Water

**Hex Label:** M.10
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Pit of Water
- The Pit of Water is a naturally formed depression, approximately 100 feet deep and 300 feet wide, filled with unusually still, intensely blue water. The water’s surface is perpetually calm, reflecting the sky with unsettling clarity.
- Scattered around the rim of the pit are the skeletal remains of several large, aquatic reptiles - primarily long-necked plesiosaurs and smaller, serpentine creatures - indicating a prolonged period of habitation by these beasts. Fossilized bones, partially submerged, are common.
- A constant, low mist rises from the water’s surface, carrying a faint, metallic scent and occasionally producing strange, shimmering illusions, most often depicting fleeting images of drowning figures. 

## Additional POI 1: Whispering Cairns
- Located 2 miles north of the Pit, a ring of six weathered stone cairns stands sentinel. Locals claim they whisper secrets on the wind.
- The cairns are constructed from a unique, grey sandstone that seems to absorb sound, further contributing to the unsettling quiet of the area.
- A small shrine, constructed of mud brick and adorned with worn animal skulls, is built at the center of the cairn circle.

## Additional POI 2: Dustwind Creek
- Dustwind Creek flows east from the plains, cutting a narrow channel through the terrain approximately 1 mile south of the Pit.
- The creek is named for the persistent dust devils that swirl along its banks, often obscuring visibility.
- Evidence of recent, nomadic settlement – a few crumbling stone hearths and scattered pottery shards – suggests intermittent use as a temporary water source.
