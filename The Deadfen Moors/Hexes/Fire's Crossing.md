# Fire's Crossing

**Hex Label:** O.7
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: Fire's Crossing
- [[Fire's Crossing]] is a small, ramshackle settlement built around a naturally occurring geothermal vent, perpetually wreathed in steam and emitting a faint orange glow. The vent fuels a rudimentary forge operated by the settlement's blacksmith, [[Silas the Pallid|Silas]] Stonehand.
- The settlement’s central feature is the ‘Dragon’s Tooth’ – a towering, obsidian-like rock formation formed by cooled lava flows from the vent, considered sacred by the locals. It’s believed to offer protection against misfortune.
- The surrounding plains are characterized by short, golden grasses and scattered groves of ironwood trees, prized for their durability and resistance to heat. Livestock grazing here are often unusually robust.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Ridge
- A naturally eroded sandstone ridge dominates the hex’s western edge, offering a vantage point overlooking the plains.
- Scattered amongst the rocks are the crumbling remains of what appears to be an ancient observatory, suggesting a forgotten civilization.
- Wind patterns funnel through the ridge, creating a consistent, low whistling sound – said to carry warnings from the spirits.

## Additional POI 2: The Sunken Cairn
- Located in the hex's southern quadrant, partially submerged in a small, brackish pond, rests a circular stone cairn.
- Recent excavations by local [[The Copper-Grubbers|scavengers]] uncovered a collection of corroded [[Zabar|bronze]] tools and fragments of what appears to be a ceremonial mask.
- Strange, bioluminescent fungi grow around the pond’s perimeter, emitting a faint, unsettling light at night.
