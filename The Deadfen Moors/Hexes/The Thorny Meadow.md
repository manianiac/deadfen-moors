# The Thorny Meadow

**Hex Label:** D.3
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Thorny Meadow
- [[The Thorny Meadow]] is a deceptively large expanse of relatively flat plains, dominated by unusually dense thickets of thorn bushes – primarily *Rubus acanthosus* (Black Thorn) and lesser varieties. The thorn density is so extreme that passage is difficult, requiring significant effort and potentially specialized tools.
- At the heart of [[The Meadow|the meadow]] lies a collapsed stone circle, partially overgrown with thorny vines. Estimates place its age at approximately 800 years, though its original purpose remains unknown. Scattered within the circle are fragments of carved slate and rusted iron tools.
- A small, brackish stream, the 'Weeping Brook', originates from a subterranean spring just south of the circle and flows weakly through [[The Meadow|the meadow]] before disappearing into a bog on the hex’s western edge. The water is perpetually murky and carries a faint, metallic odor.



## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Cairn
- Located 2 miles north-east of [[The Meadow|the Meadow]]’s main circle, the Cairn is a natural mound of earth and stone, heavily weathered and frequently buffeted by strong winds.
- Locals claim the Cairn is a site of mourning, with offerings of small stones and feathers still occasionally placed upon it.
- The Cairn’s position offers a clear vantage point overlooking a significant portion of the hex.



## Additional POI 2: Rustfang’s Scar
- Situated 3 miles south-west of [[The Meadow|the Meadow]], Rustfang’s Scar is a shallow ravine carved into the plains by an ancient riverbed. 
- The ravine is rich in iron ore deposits, evidenced by the presence of numerous rusted remnants of what appear to be ancient mining operations. 
- Small, predatory birds – identified as ‘Rustwings’ – nest within the ravine, known for their aggressive nature.
