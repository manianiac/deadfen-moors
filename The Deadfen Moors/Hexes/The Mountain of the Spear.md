# The Mountain of the Spear

**Hex Label:** F.8
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Mountain of the Spear
- The mountain itself, a roughly conical formation of shale and weathered granite, rises approximately 800 feet above the surrounding plains. Its most striking feature is a naturally formed, spear-like projection of rock jutting skyward from the summit, approximately 50 feet in length and 15 feet in diameter at its base.
- At the base of the mountain lies the Scarred Valley, a depression formed by ancient landslides and marked by strangely smooth, dark-grey stone – believed by local tribes to be the petrified remains of a monstrous serpent slain by a legendary hero.
- The summit offers a panoramic view of the surrounding plains, broken only by the distant, hazy peaks of the Serpent’s Teeth mountain range. A small, perpetually swirling mist often clings to the highest point, giving rise to tales of spirits and lost travelers.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Grove
- A cluster of ancient, wind-sculpted pines renowned for the eerie whistling sounds produced by the wind passing through their branches.
- Home to a small community of reclusive woodcarvers who craft intricate figurines from the fallen pine needles.
- The grove is said to be a place of profound silence, broken only by the whispers of the wind – occasionally interpreted as voices.

## Additional POI 2: Stonefang Outpost
- A crumbling, partially collapsed stone watchtower built during a short-lived territorial dispute between rival farming clans.
- Scavenged for valuable stone and metal by opportunistic bandits and treasure hunters.
- Marked by a series of faded, hand-painted directional symbols – the purpose of which remains unknown.
