# Ugallu the Fearsome

**Hex Label:** M.9
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: Ugallu the Fearsome
- [[Ugallu the Fearsome]] dominates the central plains, a colossal obsidian statue roughly 80 feet tall, depicting a hunched, demonic figure wreathed in perpetual shadow. The statue pulses with a sickly green light, and the air around it reeks of sulfur and decay.
- The surrounding plains are barren, choked with twisted, thorny vegetation and dotted with stagnant pools of black water. The ground itself feels unnaturally cold to the touch, and whispers carried on the wind speak of [[Ugallu]]’s influence.
- A constantly shifting, swirling miasma surrounds the base of the statue, rumored to be composed of the souls of those consumed by [[Ugallu]]’s corruption. This miasma acts as a deterrent, making direct approaches exceedingly dangerous.

## Additional POI 1: Cliff of the Devil Ugallu
- The [[Cliff of the Devil Ugallu]] is a sheer, basalt cliff face rising abruptly to the north of the main statue. Carved into the cliff face are numerous smaller, crude depictions of [[Ugallu]], suggesting a ritual site or place of worship.
- The cliff is perpetually slick with a viscous, oily substance, and the air here is noticeably colder and filled with the cries of unseen avian creatures.
- Several narrow, treacherous ledges offer potential vantage points for observing [[Ugallu the Fearsome]], but also provide opportunities for ambush.

## Additional POI 2: Ugallu’s Spring
- [[Ugallu’s Spring]] is a small, brackish pool nestled amongst the twisted vegetation to the east of the statue. The water glows with an unsettling phosphorescence and is rumored to possess potent, corrupting properties.
- Strange, mutated amphibians and insects thrive in [[The Spring|the spring]]’s vicinity, exhibiting aggressive behaviors and exhibiting unnatural resilience to injury.
- [[The Spring|The spring]] is marked by a circle of weathered, obsidian stones, stained with what appears to be ancient blood.
