# The Doom's Stone

**Hex Label:** H.8
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Doom's Stone
- The Doom's Stone is a monolithic obsidian spire, approximately 80 feet tall, rising abruptly from the otherwise flat plains. Its surface is impossibly smooth, reflecting the sky with an unsettling clarity. Strange, low-frequency vibrations emanate from the stone, causing minor tremors and disorientation in those who linger too long nearby.
- At the base of the spire lies a circular depression, approximately 30 feet in diameter, filled with a viscous, black liquid that seems to absorb light. Local legends claim this ‘Bloodpool’ is the source of the spire’s power and that gazing into it induces terrifying visions.
- Scattered around the base of the Stone are crumbling, moss-covered cairns constructed from rough-hewn stones. These cairns are adorned with faded, intricate carvings depicting monstrous figures and scenes of apparent ritual sacrifice. 

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Creek
- A narrow, perpetually misty creek flows eastward from the southern edge of the hex, said to carry the whispers of lost souls.
- The water is unnaturally cold and still, reflecting a perpetually grey sky.
- Tracks of large, unseen predators are frequently found along the creek bank.

## Additional POI 2: The Broken Wheel
- A rusted, decaying wagon wheel, partially buried in the soil, marks the site of a violent ambush recorded in the local historical accounts.
- Scavenged items – mostly personal belongings and tattered clothing – suggest the ambush took place decades ago.
- The surrounding area is unusually barren, lacking even grass or wildflowers.
