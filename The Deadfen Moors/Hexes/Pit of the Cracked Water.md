# Pit of the Cracked Water

**Hex Label:** E.4
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: Pit of the Cracked Water
- The Pit is a roughly circular depression, approximately 150 feet across and 60 feet deep, formed by a long-collapsed sinkhole. The walls are slick with perpetually damp moss and stained with streaks of an unnaturally dark, almost iridescent blue water – the “Cracked Water.”
- A palpable chill radiates from the pit, and strange, rhythmic murmuring can sometimes be heard emanating from its depths, often accompanied by fleeting glimpses of distorted shadows. Local legends claim the water holds echoes of forgotten sorrows.
- Partially submerged within the pit are the skeletal remains of what appear to be humanoid figures, clad in rusted metal armor. Scattered around the base are fragments of polished obsidian and intricately carved bone.



## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Grove
- A dense thicket of ancient, silver-barked trees, perpetually shrouded in a light mist. Strange, high-pitched whistles and sighs are frequently heard within the grove, attributed to the wind interacting with the unusually shaped branches.
- The Grove serves as a resting place for numerous avian species, including the rare Duskwing Owl, known for its melancholic calls.
-  A network of overgrown, moss-covered stone steps leads downwards into the gloom at the grove's center, vanishing without a discernible end.

## Additional POI 2: Stonefang Creek
- Stonefang Creek meanders through the hex, characterized by jagged, grey stones and swift, cold water. The creek bed is surprisingly smooth in places, suggesting a history of glacial activity.
- Several naturally formed rock arches, worn smooth by the water, provide shelter for small, territorial creatures – primarily badgers and river otters.
- A submerged, partially collapsed shrine dedicated to a forgotten water deity is located approximately halfway down the creek, identifiable by a single, weathered standing stone carved with spiraling patterns.
