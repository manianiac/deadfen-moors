# Beach of Ereshki

**Hex Label:** A.7
**Terrain Type:** Volcano


## Main POI: Beach of Ereshki
- The volcanic beach is composed of black, obsidian-like sand stretching for three miles along the base of Mount Cinderheart. Constant steam vents and small geysers punctuate the shoreline, tinted orange and red by geothermal activity.
- [[Ereshki’s Gate]], a crumbling archway constructed from basalt, stands at the northern end of the beach, partially submerged in a shallow, perpetually boiling pool. The air around the gate shimmers with distorted heat.
- Twisted, petrified trees – remnants of ancient forests – cling precariously to the slopes of Mount Cinderheart, their branches coated in a phosphorescent moss that glows faintly in the twilight. 

## Additional POI 1: Ereshki’s Gate
- The Gate is partially collapsed, revealing a descending staircase carved into the volcanic rock. The walls are covered in faded glyphs depicting swirling chaos and skeletal figures.
- A constant, low hum emanates from within the Gate, accompanied by the scent of sulfur and something vaguely…organic. 
- Traces of [[Ereshki]]’s magic linger – distorted reflections appear in the shimmering heat haze and whispers can be heard on the wind.

## Additional POI 2: Ereshki’s Rise
- The Rise is a steep, spiraling path carved into the mountainside, its edges worn smooth by centuries of wind and the lingering influence of [[Ereshki]]’s magic.
- At the summit, a circular platform of obsidian holds a single, petrified altar stained with what appears to be dried blood. 
- The view from the Rise offers a panoramic vista of the surrounding volcanic landscape, with the distant, swirling mists of the [[Scourge]]’ territory visible on the horizon.
