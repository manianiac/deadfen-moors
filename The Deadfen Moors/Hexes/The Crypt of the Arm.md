# The Crypt of the Arm

**Hex Label:** B.6
**Terrain Type:** Mountains


## Main POI: The Crypt of the Arm
- The Crypt of the Arm is a partially collapsed stone structure, carved directly into the mountainside, located within a shadowed valley choked with stunted pines and wind-scoured scree. Initial surveys indicate a complex of chambers and tunnels extending at least 300 feet below the surface.
- The crypt's namesake – a massive, rusted iron gauntlet – rests atop the entrance arch, partially embedded in the stone. The gauntlet is unnervingly intact, exhibiting signs of recent, though unidentifiable, wear.
- Recent seismic activity has destabilized the surrounding area, with frequent tremors causing rockfalls and exposing new sections of the crypt’s interior, rumored to hold ancient, potentially hostile, constructs.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Peak
- A windswept granite spire, constantly buffeted by gale-force winds that carry faint, unsettling whispers.
- Home to a small colony of Griffon scavengers, fiercely territorial and known to steal from travelers.
- Contains a rare deposit of Obsidian, highly sought after by local smiths.

## Additional POI 2: The Scarred Basin
- A dried-up lakebed littered with the bleached bones of animals and, occasionally, humanoid remains.
- Evidence suggests a devastating magical event once saturated the basin, responsible for the strange geological formations.
- The basin’s unique mineral composition creates a perpetually shimmering haze, disorienting travelers.
