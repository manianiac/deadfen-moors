# The Tower

**Hex Label:** [[The Tower|M.13]]
**Terrain Type:** Plains, River


## Main POI: The Tower
- [[The Tower]] is a massive, partially ruined stone structure rising approximately 150 feet from the surrounding plains. Constructed during a long-lost civilization, the lower levels are remarkably intact, featuring intricate carvings depicting stylized avian creatures and a complex network of spiraling staircases.
- The River Argent flows within a half-mile radius of [[The Tower|the Tower]]’s base, its waters unusually clear and possessing a faint, silvery sheen. The riverbank is heavily eroded, suggesting significant volume of water flow over centuries.
- Scavenged components of a complex, now-defunct clockwork mechanism – gears, pressure gauges, and partially melted metal – are scattered throughout the upper levels, hinting at [[The Tower|the Tower]]’s original purpose as a meteorological observatory or perhaps something more arcane.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Grove
- A small, perpetually misty grove exists to the northeast, filled with unusually tall, slender trees whose leaves rustle constantly, even in still air.
- Locals claim the grove is haunted by the voices of those who perished in [[The Tower|the Tower]]’s construction.
- Rare phosphorescent fungi grow in abundance within the grove, illuminating the area with an eerie green glow.

## Additional POI 2: Riverbend Trading Post
- A small, fortified trading post, built primarily of rough-hewn timber and reinforced with salvaged Tower stone, sits on the south bank of the River Argent.
- The post primarily trades in furs, river fish, and information regarding [[The Tower|the Tower]] and its surrounding area.
- A weathered sign proclaims the post’s owner, [[Silas the Pallid|Silas]] Blackwood, to be a ‘Trustworthy Guide’.
