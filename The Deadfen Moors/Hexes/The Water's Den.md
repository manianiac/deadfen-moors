# The Water's Den

**Hex Label:** K.7
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Water’s Den
- The Water’s Den is a partially submerged, ancient stone structure built into the bank of the Whisperwind River. Constructed primarily of moss-covered grey granite, the main building consists of a large, circular hall with a collapsed, vaulted ceiling exposing the roots of ancient cypress trees. The river flows through the center of the hall, creating a constant, soothing murmur.
- The structure is occupied by a loosely organized community of riverfolk – primarily skilled boatwrights, trappers, and traders who call themselves the “Denfolk.” They maintain a wary neutrality with the surrounding settlements, primarily exchanging goods and offering repair services.  A small forge, powered by the river’s flow, sits adjacent to the hall.
-  Scattered around the main hall are smaller, crudely constructed shelters made of woven reeds and timber, suggesting a long-established, nomadic lifestyle interwoven with the permanent structure.  Evidence of ritualistic carvings depicting aquatic spirits are visible on several of the stones.



## Additional POI 1: Echoing Falls
- A narrow waterfall cascades down a sheer cliff face approximately 1 mile north of the Den.
- The falls produce a distinct, echoing resonance, said to carry whispers of the past.
-  A small, relatively flat area beneath the falls provides a sheltered fishing spot.

## Additional POI 2:  The Twisted Root
- A massive, ancient willow tree dominates the western edge of the hex, its branches gnarled and twisted into bizarre shapes.
- The tree’s roots form a natural labyrinth beneath the surface, rumored to lead to forgotten caverns.
-  Rare luminescent fungi grow in abundance at the base of the tree, casting an eerie glow at night.
