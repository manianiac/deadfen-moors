# The Dark Grove

**Hex Label:** M.4
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Dark Grove
- [[The Dark Grove]] is a perpetually shadowed area within the larger forest, dominated by ancient, gnarled Blackwood trees – so named for their unusually dark, almost obsidian bark. Sunlight rarely penetrates the canopy, creating a dim, cool environment.
- A stagnant, moss-covered pool, known as the Stillwater, sits at the Grove’s center, rumored to possess unsettling properties and attracting strange, luminous insects.
- Scattered amongst the trees are the crumbling remains of a small, stone shrine dedicated to a forgotten nature deity, adorned with faded carvings of serpentine figures and unsettling floral motifs.



## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Falls
- Located approximately 2 miles north of the Grove, a series of small waterfalls cascade down a moss-covered cliff face.
- [[The Falls|The falls]] are consistently accompanied by a low, mournful wind that carries unintelligible whispers, believed to be echoes of past events.
- A narrow, slippery path leads to the base of [[The Falls|the falls]], offering potential access to a hidden cave system.

## Additional POI 2: Stonehand Cairn
- Situated 3 miles northeast of the Grove, a solitary stone cairn stands as the only visible marker on a windswept ridge.
- The cairn is constructed from weathered grey stone and is unusually large for a simple marker, exhibiting signs of deliberate construction dating back centuries.
- Local folklore suggests the cairn marks the burial site of a powerful, but tragically flawed, earth giant.


## Local Factions & Lore
