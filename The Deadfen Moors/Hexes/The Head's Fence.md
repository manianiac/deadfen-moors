# The Head's Fence

**Hex Label:** [[The Head's Fence|A.12]]
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Head’s Fence
- The Head’s Fence is a roughly circular stone wall, approximately 30 feet tall and 15 feet thick, constructed from grey shale and capped with weathered oak planks. It is partially collapsed in several sections, revealing a dark, overgrown interior.  Scattered along the wall are the remains of wooden posts, many broken, suggesting it was once used to contain livestock.
-  A shallow, muddy depression, roughly 60 feet in diameter, lies at the center of the fence’s interior. The mud is unnaturally dark and smells faintly of iron.  Several skeletal remains, largely decayed, are partially buried within the mud. 
-  Local legends claim the fence was erected by a paranoid nobleman, Lord Valerius, centuries ago to imprison a monstrous hound—a creature said to have devoured his entire family. The fence's construction is accompanied by a recurring symbol etched into [[The Stone|the stone]]: a stylized hound's head.



## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Hollow
- A small, sheltered valley containing a network of shallow caves.
- Known for unusually strong, almost constant winds.
- Rumored to be haunted by the voices of those lost in the plains.

## Additional POI 2: Dust Devil’s Scar
- A prominent, circular depression in the plains, formed by a long-gone dust devil.
- Contains unusually dense deposits of shimmering, grey sand.
- Shows signs of ancient, ritualistic markings etched into the surrounding rock face.
