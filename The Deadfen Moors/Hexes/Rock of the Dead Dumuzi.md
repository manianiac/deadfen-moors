# Rock of the Dead Dumuzi

**Hex Label:** I.4
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: Rock of the Dead Dumuzi
- The Rock of the Dead Dumuzi is a monolithic basalt formation, approximately 60 feet high and 30 feet wide at its base, dominating the center of a clearing within the dense, ancient forest. The stone is unnaturally smooth in places, suggesting deliberate, long-ago carving.
- Around the base of the rock are the skeletal remains of what appear to be humanoid figures, partially fused with the basalt. These are not clearly Nabu or Inhuman, exhibiting subtle anatomical distortions. Strange, pulsating lichen covers sections of the rock face.
- A faint, rhythmic humming emanates from within a deep fissure in the rock’s north side. The air around the fissure is noticeably colder and carries a subtle scent of ozone and decay.


## Additional POI 1: Grove of Dumuzi
- Located 2 miles east of the Rock, the Grove is a small, circular clearing filled with stunted, silver-leafed trees. The soil here is unnaturally fertile and glows with a faint luminescence.
- The Grove is perpetually shrouded in a thick mist, and the air feels heavy with an almost palpable sadness.  Small, crystalline structures resembling infant hands are embedded in the roots of the trees.
- A single, perfectly preserved stasis pod identical to those described in the lore regarding Dumuzi rests at the center of the Grove.


## Additional POI 2: The Fence of Dumuzi
- The Fence of Dumuzi is a crumbling stone wall, approximately 10 feet high, stretching for 3 miles to the south of the Rock. Large sections are missing, revealing dense undergrowth and treacherous ravines.
- The wall is constructed from the same basalt as the Rock, but the stones are deliberately arranged to form complex, interlocking patterns, possibly representing a forgotten language or warding ritual.
- Traps and collapsed sections of the wall suggest constant, and largely unsuccessful, attempts at clearing the area, hinting at guardians or lingering effects of the rock's influence.
