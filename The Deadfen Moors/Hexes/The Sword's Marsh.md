# The Sword's Marsh

**Hex Label:** B.8
**Terrain Type:** Hills


## Main POI: The Sword's Marsh
- The Sword’s Marsh is a perpetually mist-shrouded expanse of bog and reed, dominated by a central, unnaturally still pool known as the ‘Stillwater’. The water reflects an unsettling, grey sky and is rumored to possess minor illusions.
- Scattered throughout [[The Marsh|the marsh]] are the remnants of a long-forgotten fortress – crumbling stone walls, collapsed towers choked with moss, and the occasional partially submerged archway. These structures are remarkably well-preserved for their age, suggesting a magical defense.
- A series of narrow, treacherous causeways, constructed from blackened timber and packed earth, crisscross [[The Marsh|the marsh]], leading to the most significant remnants of the fortress and offering the only relatively stable routes through the bog. These are frequently submerged during periods of high water.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Keep
- A ruined stone keep perched atop the highest of the hex’s hills, offering a commanding view of [[The Marsh|the marsh]]. Partially collapsed and overgrown, it’s a haven for [[The Copper-Grubbers|scavengers]] and opportunistic bandits.
- Locals speak of a perpetual wind whistling through [[The Keep|the keep]]’s shattered windows, carrying whispers and unsettling sounds – attributed to restless spirits trapped within.
- Evidence of extensive rune-carving adorns the remaining walls, hinting at a connection to the fortress of The Sword’s Marsh.

## Additional POI 2: Fenwalker’s Shrine
- A small, circular clearing centered around a monolithic stone carved with the image of a stylized heron – worshipped by the Fenwalkers, a nomadic tribe said to reside within the deeper reaches of [[The Marsh|the marsh]].
- The shrine is often adorned with intricately woven reed baskets filled with brightly colored feathers, offerings to the heron spirit.
- Scattered around the shrine are numerous, meticulously crafted traps designed to deter intruders, demonstrating the Fenwalkers’ deep knowledge of [[The Marsh|the marsh]]’s dangers.
