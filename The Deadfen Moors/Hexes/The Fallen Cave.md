# The Fallen Cave

**Hex Label:** C.10
**Terrain Type:** Swamp


## Main POI: The Fallen Cave
- The cave mouth is a collapsed archway choked with gnarled mangrove roots and phosphorescent fungi, perpetually damp and emitting a faint, sickly sweet odor.  Water drips constantly from the ceiling, forming shallow pools on the muddy floor.
- Deep within the cave, a massive, petrified tree – dubbed “The Fallen King” – dominates the central chamber. Its colossal roots twist around shattered stone pillars, and a perpetual mist clings to its decaying form.  Scratches and markings, potentially ancient, cover its surface.
- The cave system branches into a network of smaller tunnels, many flooded with brackish water.  Evidence of recent cave-ins and unstable ground is prevalent throughout, presenting significant traversal hazards.



## Additional POI 1: Whispering Mire
- A dense patch of quicksand known as the Whispering Mire lies to the north-east of the cave entrance, named for the unsettling sounds carried on the wind as it passes through the reeds.
- Several skeletal remains, clad in rusted leather armor, are partially submerged within the mire, suggesting a violent encounter.
- The Mire acts as a natural barrier, slowing travel and offering concealment to potential ambushers.

## Additional POI 2: Serpent’s Tooth
- A solitary, jagged black rock formation, resembling a serpent’s fang, rises abruptly from the swamp floor to the west. 
- Local folklore claims the rock is a petrified dragon’s tooth, marking a place of potent magical energy.
-  A small, stagnant pool sits at the base of Serpent’s Tooth, often teeming with poisonous snakes.
