# King Lothar the Floating

**Hex Label:** O.5
**Terrain Type:** Plains, River


## Main POI: King Lothar the Floating
- The central feature is the remains of [[Lothar]]’s Citadel – a colossal, partially submerged structure of obsidian and shimmering, unstable anti-gravity plates. It floats approximately 50 feet above the plains, perpetually shifting and emitting a low, humming resonance.
- Scattered around the Citadel are the husks of colossal, deactivated Guardian [[Dwarves|Constructs]] – biomechanical war machines designed to defend [[Lothar]]’s domain, many still radiating residual energy.
- A wide, sluggish river, the Serpent’s Coil, partially surrounds the Citadel’s base, heavily polluted with strange, iridescent algae and rumored to contain pockets of unstable anti-gravity fields.



## Additional POI 1: The Nabu Archive
- A collapsed research outpost, largely buried by sand, containing fragmented data logs detailing [[Nabu]] experiments with gravity manipulation.
- Evidence suggests a small, fanatical cult dedicated to resurrecting [[Lothar]] operates within the ruins.
- The Archive is guarded by corrupted, autonomous drones – remnants of [[Anu|the Nabu]] defense systems.

## Additional POI 2: The Weaver’s Hold
- A small, fortified village built atop a naturally occurring geyser, offering a rare source of clean water.
- The villagers, known as the Weavers, possess a rudimentary understanding of manipulating the local anti-gravity anomalies.
- They trade salvaged technology and information with travelers, fiercely protective of their water source.
