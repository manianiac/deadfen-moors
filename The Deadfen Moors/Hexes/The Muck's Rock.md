# The Muck's Rock

**Hex Label:** G.5
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Muck’s Rock
- The Muck’s Rock is a massive, naturally-formed basalt pillar rising approximately 60 feet from the surrounding plains. Erosion has sculpted the rock into a vaguely humanoid shape, leading to local legends of a slumbering earth spirit.
- At the base of the Rock lies a stagnant pool of brackish water, perpetually shrouded in a thin mist. The water is unusually warm and supports a thriving population of bioluminescent algae and small, amphibian creatures.
- A crumbling stone watchtower, likely built during a minor border skirmish centuries ago, sits partially collapsed near the eastern face of the Rock, offering limited visibility across the surrounding plains.


## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Ridge
- A series of wind-sculpted sandstone ridges dominate the north-western quadrant, known for unsettling whistling sounds even in still air.
- Sparse, thorny scrub vegetation clings to the ridges, providing meager shelter for small predators.
- Evidence of ancient, shallow graves are scattered amongst the rocks.

## Additional POI 2: Old Man Hemlock
- A solitary, ancient hemlock tree stands approximately one mile south-east of the Rock, noticeably larger than surrounding trees.
- The tree’s roots form a natural cave system, occasionally utilized by nomadic tribes for temporary shelter.
- Strange carvings depicting stylized animal figures adorn the base of the trunk.
