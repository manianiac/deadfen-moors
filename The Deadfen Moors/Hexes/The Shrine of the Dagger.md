# The Shrine of the Dagger

**Hex Label:** C.11
**Terrain Type:** Swamp


## Main POI: The Shrine of the Dagger
- The shrine is a partially submerged, cyclopean structure built from dark, moss-covered stone, rising from the deepest mire of the hex.  A single, immense obsidian dagger perpetually hangs suspended within a circular chamber at the shrine’s center, radiating a faint chill.
-  The surrounding swamp is choked with carnivorous pitcher plants, phosphorescent fungi, and abnormally large, venomous snakes, all seemingly drawn to the shrine’s unsettling aura.  A constant, low hum vibrates through the ground near the structure.
-  Scattered around the shrine’s perimeter are crumbling bone altars and the remnants of what appear to be ritualistic sacrifices – mostly skeletal remains adorned with tarnished silver jewelry and fragments of dark, exotic cloth. 

## Additional POI 1: Murkwater’s Embrace
- A stagnant pool, approximately half a mile east of the shrine, known for its oily surface and unsettlingly still reflections.
-  Giant, mutated frogs, the size of ponies, make their home within the pool, and their calls echo across the swamp.
-  Strange, geometric patterns are occasionally visible within the murk, disappearing as quickly as they appear.

## Additional POI 2: Whispering Reeds
- A dense thicket of reeds, several miles to the north, that constantly produce a faint, sibilant whisper, even in still air.
-  The reeds conceal the skeletal remains of a long-lost logging camp, overtaken by the swamp.
-  Local legends speak of trapped spirits bound to the reeds, capable of influencing travelers' thoughts.
