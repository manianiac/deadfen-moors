# The Corpse's Vale

**Hex Label:** J.7
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Corpse’s Vale
- The Corpse’s Vale is a circular depression in the otherwise flat plains, approximately 1.5 miles in diameter. The soil within the vale is a dark, almost black loam, perpetually damp and cool.
- At the center of the vale stands the skeletal remains of a massive, petrified tree – nicknamed “The Watcher” – its branches reaching upwards like accusing fingers. A constant, low hum emanates from the base of the Watcher.
- Scattered across the vale floor are the bleached bones of numerous creatures – human, animal, and something…else. Recent signs indicate a localized resurgence of unsettling activity, with reports of phantom lights and unnerving whispers.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Ridge
- A narrow ridge of exposed shale runs along the northern edge of the hex, perpetually buffeted by winds that carry faint, unintelligible murmurs.
- Several weathered cairns mark potential bandit camps, remnants of travelers lost to the Vale's unsettling influence.
- [[The Copper-Grubbers|Scavengers]] often collect strangely shaped stones from the ridge, rumored to hold residual echoes of the Vale's past.

## Additional POI 2: Silent Creek
- Silent Creek flows from the southern edge of the Vale, its water a stagnant, grey color with a noticeably sweet odor.
- The creek bank is lined with strangely smooth, obsidian-like pebbles, reflecting an unsettling, distorted image of the surrounding landscape.
- Local wildlife – primarily insects and small rodents – avoid the creek entirely.
