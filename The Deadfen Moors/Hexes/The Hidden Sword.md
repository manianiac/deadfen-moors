# The Hidden Sword

**Hex Label:** K.9
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Hidden Sword
- The ruins of a long-abandoned watchtower, constructed of grey sandstone and partially buried by shifting plains sand, dominates the center of the hex. Its walls exhibit strange, swirling carvings depicting stylized swords and winged figures.
- Beneath the watchtower, a recently discovered cave entrance leads to a narrow, damp passage lined with naturally occurring quartz crystals that faintly glow with an internal luminescence. The passage slopes downwards for approximately 100 feet.
- A circular stone altar, partially collapsed and covered in moss, sits just outside the watchtower's western wall. Runes are etched into its surface, believed by local legends to be related to the “Sword’s” protective enchantment.

## Additional POI 1: Dust Devil’s Ridge
- A series of shallow, wind-sculpted ridges composed of reddish-brown clay, prone to frequent, small dust devils.
- Scattered across the ridge are the bleached bones of various grazing animals – primarily bison and antelope.
- A lone, gnarled juniper tree clings to the highest ridge, providing the only significant shade.

## Additional POI 2: Silent Creek
- A narrow, dry creek bed cutting a path through the western edge of the hex, marked by a few scattered, petrified logs.
- The creek bed is surprisingly smooth and free of loose stones, suggesting a former flow of water.
- Evidence of ancient, partially submerged pottery shards can be found along the creek bed's bottom.
