# The Ashen Gate

**Hex Label:** D.9
**Terrain Type:** Swamp


## Main POI: The Ashen Gate
- [[The Ashen Gate]] is a crumbling obsidian archway, perpetually shrouded in a thick, grey mist. [[The Stone|The stone]] is stained with a dark, ashy residue, giving the impression of perpetual snowfall. The archway leads into a deeper, exceptionally treacherous section of the swamp.
- Within the immediate area of the gate, the swamp’s vegetation is unnaturally twisted and stunted, dominated by pale, phosphorescent fungi and thorny, black vines. Strange, rhythmic chanting seems to emanate from within the gate’s shadow.
- The swamp floor around the gate is unstable, riddled with sinkholes concealed by a slick, algae-covered surface. Local [[Rumors|rumors]] claim that the gate shifts its location subtly, always guiding those who approach it towards a specific, fatal destination.

## Additional POI 1: Whispering Mire
- Located 2 miles south of the gate, the Mire is a particularly dense area of quicksand and stagnant pools.
- Locals claim the pools contain the trapped voices of those lost within the swamp, capable of driving travelers mad.
- A skeletal framework of a longboat, half-submerged in the mire, offers a potential, albeit dangerous, route across a particularly wide channel.

## Additional POI 2: Bone Orchard
- Situated 3 miles west of the gate, the Bone Orchard is a clearing filled with meticulously arranged piles of animal and humanoid bones.
- The bones appear to be arranged in patterns, suggesting a ritualistic purpose, though the meaning remains unclear.
- A large, carved stone idol depicting a skeletal hand clutching a single, blackened rose stands at the center of the orchard.
