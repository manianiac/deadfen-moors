# The Black Ash

**Hex Label:** [[The Black Ash|B.12]]
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Black Ash
- [[The Black Ash]] is a ruined, blackened fortress constructed primarily of volcanic basalt, now choked with ash and stunted, charcoal-colored vegetation. The walls stand approximately 30 feet high, exhibiting signs of intense fire damage and partial collapse. 
- Within the central courtyard lies a solidified pool of obsidian, radiating a faint, unnatural cold. Scattered around the pool are the skeletal remains of what appear to be heavily armored warriors, clad in blackened plate armor. 
- A perpetual, mournful wind whispers through the ruined towers, carrying with it the scent of sulfur and a subtle, unsettling hum. Scrawled upon the remaining walls are fragmented runes of a forgotten language.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Cairns
- A cluster of weathered stone cairns marks a shallow burial ground, believed to be the resting place of a nomadic tribe consumed by the fortress’s initial siege.
- Wind chimes crafted from bone and ash hang from the cairns, producing a haunting melody that shifts with the breeze.
- Scattered amongst the cairns are rusted weapons and fragments of worn leather armor, suggesting a desperate defense.

## Additional POI 2: The Obsidian Scar
- A deep fissure, approximately 100 feet long and 40 feet wide, bisects the plains to the north of the fortress. The earth within the scar is slick with obsidian dust and intensely cold.
- Strange, bioluminescent fungi cling to the walls of the scar, emitting an eerie, pulsating light. 
- Local legends claim the scar is a conduit to the underworld, and that unsettling whispers can be heard emanating from its depths.
