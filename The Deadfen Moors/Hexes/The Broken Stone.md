# The Broken Stone

**Hex Label:** [[The Broken Stone|O.11]]
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Broken Stone
- [[The Broken Stone]] is a monolithic pillar of dark grey granite, approximately 30 feet tall and 10 feet in diameter, shattered unevenly across its top. Local legends claim it was once a shrine to a forgotten sky god.
- Scattered around the base of [[The Stone|the stone]] are the remnants of a long-abandoned settlement – mostly collapsed timber-framed buildings and fragmented pottery, suggesting a hasty evacuation. Soil analysis indicates the settlement thrived for roughly 50-75 years before its disappearance.
- A faint, rhythmic humming can be heard emanating from [[The Stone|the stone]] on the night of the new moon, accompanied by a localized shift in temperature. Tracks resembling large avian claws have been repeatedly observed in the soft earth surrounding the structure.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Grove
- A small copse of ancient willows, perpetually draped in mist, located 2 miles east of [[The Broken Stone|the Broken Stone]].
- The willows are unnaturally large, and the wind always seems to rustle their leaves in a hushed, almost vocal manner.
- A single, perfectly preserved silver locket, containing a faded portrait, is rumored to be hidden amongst the roots.

## Additional POI 2: Dust Devil Ridge
- A series of low, wind-scoured ridges approximately 3 miles southwest of [[The Broken Stone|the Broken Stone]].
- The ridges are covered in a fine layer of ochre-colored dust, constantly shifting in swirling patterns – a phenomenon locals call "Devil's Breath."
- Fossilized remains of large, horned reptiles are frequently unearthed during sandstorms, suggesting a significant presence of prehistoric fauna in the area.
