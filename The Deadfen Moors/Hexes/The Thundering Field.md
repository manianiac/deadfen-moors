# The Thundering Field

**Hex Label:** C.9
**Terrain Type:** Hills


## Main POI: The Thundering Field
- The Thundering Field is a vast, naturally-formed amphitheater carved into the rolling hills, approximately 1 mile in diameter. The soil is rich and dark, perpetually damp from underground springs, supporting a dense, almost unnatural growth of tall, resonant grasses that create a constant, low-frequency hum when disturbed – hence the name.
- At the Field’s center stands a collapsed stone circle, believed to be the remnants of a forgotten ritual site. Weathered standing stones, partially buried by the grass, suggest a complex and possibly dangerous history linked to the field’s resonant properties.
- Scattered throughout the Field are numerous shallow depressions filled with a phosphorescent blue moss. These pools seem to amplify the humming sound and are rumored to induce vivid, unsettling dreams in those who linger too long.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Crag
- A solitary, wind-scoured crag rises sharply from the western edge of the hex, offering a vantage point overlooking the Field.
- Small, intricately carved stone figurines are frequently found embedded within the crag’s base, possibly offerings related to the Thundering Field.
- The crag’s highest point experiences almost constant, unnaturally strong winds, contributing to the amplified sound of the Field.

## Additional POI 2: River’s Edge Watchtower
- Located 2 miles south of the Field’s eastern border, the Watchtower is a crumbling stone structure built around a natural spring.
- The Watchtower’s purpose was once to monitor trade routes along the River Silvan, but it has fallen into disrepair and is now inhabited by a solitary, grizzled trapper.
- The spring provides a small, but vital, source of fresh water, and the surrounding area is known for its unusually large population of river otters.
