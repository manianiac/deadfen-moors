# The Rise of the Dagger

**Hex Label:** J.6
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Rise of the Dagger
- [[The Rise of the Dagger]] is a crumbling ziggurat constructed of dark grey shale, rising approximately 40 feet from the surrounding plains. Carved into its lower levels are depictions of serpentine figures and stylized daggers, suggesting a connection to a forgotten, serpent-worshiping cult. 
- A constant, low hum emanates from deep within the ziggurat, detectable within a 100-foot radius. The air around the structure feels noticeably colder, and a faint, metallic scent lingers. 
- Scattered around the base of the ziggurat are the skeletal remains of approximately twenty individuals, many clutching broken dagger fragments. Evidence suggests a violent, ritualistic demise.



## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Creek
- Whisperwind Creek flows southward from a distant, unnamed ridge, providing a small but vital source of fresh water. 
- The creek banks are lined with stunted willow trees, providing minimal cover and attracting a population of skittering, grey-scaled rats. 
- Local folklore claims the creek whispers secrets to those who listen closely, though the nature of these secrets remains unknown.

## Additional POI 2: Stone Cairn of the Fallen
- [[The Stone]] Cairn is a haphazard pile of grey stones, approximately 15 feet in diameter and 8 feet high, marking a battle site. 
- Scars on the stones indicate a prolonged struggle, likely involving swords and shields.  A rusted longsword lies partially buried at the base. 
- A small, iridescent beetle – the 'Shardwing' – is frequently observed crawling amongst the stones, seemingly drawn to the metallic scent.
