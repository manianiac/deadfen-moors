# Grove of Dumuzi

**Hex Label:** [[Grove of Dumuzi|A.11]]
**Terrain Type:** Hills


## Main POI: Grove of Dumuzi
- The Grove sits within a natural amphitheater formed by the surrounding hills, creating a sense of enclosure and amplifying any sounds within. Twisted, silver-barked trees, unnaturally tall and slender, dominate the landscape – a direct consequence of [[Anu|the Nabu]] energies that sustained the stasis pod.
- The ground is covered in a thick, phosphorescent moss that pulses with a faint, rhythmic light, intensifying around the central, cracked stone pedestal where the stasis pod once rested. Residual energy fields cause sporadic bursts of static and distort perception.
- A complex network of shallow, circular channels crisscrosses the grove floor, seemingly carved by some unknown force. These channels occasionally fill with a viscous, dark liquid that emits a chilling vapor.


## Additional POI 1: The Fence of Dumuzi
- A crumbling, obsidian barrier stretches across the southern edge of the hex, partially submerged in a bog. [[The Stone|The stone]] is etched with spiraling glyphs that resonate with subtle psychic energy.
- The Fence’s construction suggests an attempt to contain something – or perhaps someone – but its purpose and origin are lost to time. Animal life avoids the area entirely.
- Scattered around the base of the fence are rusted, skeletal remains of automated sentinels, deactivated but radiating a faint, unsettling hum.



## Additional POI 2: Rock of the Dead Dumuzi
- A monolithic slab of grey stone, marked with deep, unsettling grooves resembling stylized infant faces, sits within a small, naturally formed cave. The air within the cave is noticeably colder.
- The Rock exhibits a weak but persistent psychic signature – primarily echoes of immense sorrow and confusion, intensifying when approached.
-  Scattered across the rock face are fragments of what appear to be ancient, metallic fabric – remnants of the stasis pod’s shielding.
