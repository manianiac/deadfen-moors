# The Barrier

**Hex Label:** H.3
**Terrain Type:** Forest


## Main POI: The Barrier
- [[The Barrier]] is a colossal, petrified tree, approximately 150 feet tall and 40 feet in diameter, dominating the hex. Its wood is a dense, charcoal grey, seemingly solidified mid-growth with intricate, swirling patterns resembling frozen water.
- A constant, unnatural mist clings to the base of [[The Barrier|the Barrier]], obscuring the ground and carrying a faint, metallic scent.  Local folklore claims the mist whispers warnings and forgotten names.
- Deep grooves, approximately 6 feet wide and 10 feet long, radiate outwards from [[The Barrier|the Barrier]]'s base, creating a natural, navigable channel that bisects the hex, leading towards the western edge.


## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Glade
- A small, perpetually shaded glade filled with unusually tall, silver-leafed trees. 
- Strange, high-pitched whistles are frequently heard emanating from the glade, believed by some to be the voices of lost souls.
- Several shallow, crystal-clear pools are found within the glade, exhibiting faint, luminescent algae.

## Additional POI 2: Stone Carver's Rest
- A small, ruined stone structure – likely a hermitage – constructed from weathered grey stone.
- Scraps of intricately carved bone and wood are scattered throughout the site, suggesting a former sculptor or craftsman.
- A collapsed well, choked with moss and stagnant water, sits at the center of the structure, radiating a palpable sense of cold.
