# The Field

**Hex Label:** B.7
**Terrain Type:** Mountains


## Main POI: The Field
- [[The Field]] is a relatively flat, stone-strewn basin carved into the southern slope of the Blackfang Mountains, approximately 3 miles across. Its unusual fertility is attributed to mineral-rich runoff from the peaks.
- Scattered amongst the standing stones within [[The Field|the field]] are the remnants of a ruined watchtower, constructed of dark grey granite and showing signs of prolonged weathering and attempted repairs.
- A perpetually mist-shrouded spring, known locally as the ‘Weeping Stone’, feeds into a small, stagnant pool at [[The Field|the field]]’s center. The water is unusually cold and smells faintly of iron.


## Additional POI 1: Stonefang’s Reach
- Located 2 miles north of [[The Field]], Stonefang’s Reach is a precarious cliffside settlement clinging to the mountainside.
- The inhabitants, primarily skilled climbers and quarrymen, trade in harvested obsidian and polished stone.
- A network of rope bridges connects the homes, offering stunning, if perilous, views.

## Additional POI 2: Whisperwind Peak
- Situated 3 miles west of [[The Field]], Whisperwind Peak is a windswept crag dominated by a massive, naturally hollowed stone formation.
- The hollow is said to amplify whispers carried on the wind, often inducing unsettling hallucinations.
-  Scattered around the base of the peak are the skeletal remains of several mountain goats, partially buried in scree.
