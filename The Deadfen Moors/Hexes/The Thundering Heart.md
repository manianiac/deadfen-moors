# The Thundering Heart

**Hex Label:** [[The Thundering Heart|K.14]]
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Thundering Heart
- [[The Thundering Heart]] is a massive, naturally-formed amphitheater carved into the plains by centuries of wind and rain. Standing stone pillars, slick with mineral deposits, form tiered seating rising to a central basin approximately 100 feet in diameter. 
- The basin constantly echoes with a low, resonant hum – the source of the hex’s name – generated by the movement of underground springs and airflow through [[The Stone|the stone]] structure. This hum is rumored to affect the minds of those who linger too long within.
- A small, partially submerged shrine dedicated to a forgotten fertility deity, “Terra’s Pulse,” sits at the basin's deepest point. Offerings of grain and polished stones are frequently left here.

## Additional POI 1: Dust Devil’s Watch
- Located 2 miles east of [[The Thundering Heart|the Thundering Heart]], this abandoned homestead offers rudimentary shelter from the plains’ harsh weather. 
- The Watch contains remnants of a family’s life – rusted tools, tattered clothing, and a well-maintained (though crumbling) wagon wheel.
- Tracks in the surrounding dust suggest recent, hurried travel.

## Additional POI 2: Stone Serpent Ridge
- Stretching across the northern edge of the hex, Stone Serpent Ridge is a series of wind-sculpted sandstone formations resembling a colossal serpent. 
- The Ridge is a popular location for hunters, primarily for the swift plains-hares that burrow amongst the rocks.
- Ancient, faded pictographs depicting stylized animals and humanoid figures adorn the largest stone formations.
