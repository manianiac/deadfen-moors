# The Hidden Hut

**Hex Label:** L.9
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Hidden Hut
- [[The Hidden Hut]] is a roughly constructed dwelling built primarily of mud, woven reeds, and salvaged timber, partially concealed by a dense thicket of thorny brambles. It appears abandoned but retains signs of recent occupancy – a smoking hearth, a half-finished game of dice on a crudely made table, and scattered rations.
-  A narrow, overgrown path leads to the hut, deliberately obscured by strategically placed stones and branches, suggesting an intention to remain unseen. The surrounding plains are eerily quiet, broken only by the wind whistling through the brambles.
-  A small, shallow well is located adjacent to the hut, though the water is brackish and likely contaminated. Traces of metalworking tools – a forge hammer and a few tongs – are found within the hut's meager storage area.


## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Knoll
- A solitary, windswept rise offering a panoramic view of the surrounding plains.
- Frequent, inexplicable whispers carried on the wind are reported here.
- Contains the weathered remains of a stone cairn.

## Additional POI 2: Serpent's Reach
- A dry riverbed cutting through the plains, known for its deceptively deep channels.
- Deposits of shimmering, opalescent scales are scattered along the riverbed, suggesting a recent reptile presence.
- Partially submerged skeletal remains of a large, unidentified beast are visible at the river’s deepest point.
