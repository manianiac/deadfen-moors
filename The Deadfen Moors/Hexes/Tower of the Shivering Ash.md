# Tower of the Shivering Ash

**Hex Label:** F.9
**Terrain Type:** Swamp


## Main POI: Tower of the Shivering Ash
- [[The Tower|The tower]] itself is constructed primarily of grey, porous shale, perpetually slick with a viscous, ash-grey substance that seems to emanate from within. It rises approximately 80 feet, tapering to a jagged spire crowned with a rotating, obsidian shard that occasionally emits a low, mournful hum.
- Within [[The Tower|the tower]]'s lower levels, a complex network of flooded chambers and spiraling staircases are partially submerged in stagnant, phosphorescent swamp water. The air hangs heavy with the scent of decay, ozone, and something subtly metallic.  Remnants of a forgotten civilization – intricate stone carvings depicting skeletal figures and twisted, aquatic beasts – are scattered amongst the debris.
-  The uppermost chamber contains a large, circular basin filled with the "Shivering Ash." This substance is cold to the touch, and disturbing it causes a brief, localized freezing effect. Runes etched around the basin glow with a faint, blue light, pulsing in rhythm with the obsidian shard’s hum. 

## Additional POI 1: Murkwood Fen
- A dense, perpetual fog blankets the surrounding marshland, populated by grotesque, bioluminescent fungi and treacherous quicksand pits.
-  The Fen is home to the Razorfin, a predatory fish with crystalline scales and a venomous bite, often seen lurking beneath the surface.
-  Scattered amongst the reeds are the skeletal remains of travelers, suggesting frequent disappearances and dangers within the mist.

## Additional POI 2: Whispering Cairns
- A collection of weathered stone cairns, each adorned with a single, smooth black stone, stand in a semi-circle approximately half a mile from [[The Tower|the Tower]].
- Locals claim the cairns whisper secrets to those who listen closely, though the whispers are often incoherent and unsettling.
-  The cairns are a known burial ground for the original inhabitants of the hex, their fates forever intertwined with [[The Tower|the Tower]]’s influence.
