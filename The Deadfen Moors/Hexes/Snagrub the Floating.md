# Snagrub the Floating

**Hex Label:** B.9
**Terrain Type:** Forest (on hills)


## Main POI: Snagrub the Floating
- Snagrub the Floating is a grotesquely mutated goblin, approximately 4 feet tall, perpetually suspended 30 feet above the forest floor thanks to a salvaged and malfunctioning Nabu anti-gravity device. Its skin is a mottled, sickly green, covered in jagged bone protrusions and exposed muscle. It attacks with crude, sharpened stone weapons and emits a high-pitched, grating shriek.
- The goblin’s “nest” is a chaotic collection of scavenged materials – broken tools, shattered Nabu components, and the remains of small animals – clinging to a single, ancient oak tree at the hex’s center.  Signs of recent construction and experimentation are scattered throughout the immediate area.
- The surrounding forest floor is heavily scarred and littered with metallic debris, a testament to Snagrub's erratic movements and violent attacks. Visibility is limited due to dense, twisted trees and lingering, oily smoke from the Nabu device.



## Additional POI 1: The Jagged Snagrub
- Located 2 miles southwest of the main POI, this is a large, partially collapsed Nabu structure – likely a repair station – heavily damaged and overrun with vegetation.  Scattered fragments of the original structure suggest it was once used for intricate, though now corrupted, technological operations.
- The area around the structure is choked with a bizarre, fast-growing fungus exhibiting a faint blue glow, potentially linked to the Nabu tech.  Several smaller, mutated goblin scavenging parties are known to operate from this location.
-  A narrow, overgrown path leads to the structure, often slick with fungal slime and marked with crude goblin glyphs – potentially warnings or territorial markings.



## Additional POI 2: Tree of Snagrub
- Situated 1 mile northeast of Snagrub the Floating, this ancient oak is significantly larger and older than those surrounding it, displaying unnatural growth patterns and a dense cluster of obsidian-like thorns. 
- Local legends claim the tree was the original source of Snagrub's mutation – a point of concentrated Nabu energy leakage – and that it subtly influences the goblin's behavior. 
-  Small, perfectly spherical stones – polished to a mirror sheen - are frequently found embedded within the tree’s roots and branches, seemingly undisturbed by the surrounding environment.
