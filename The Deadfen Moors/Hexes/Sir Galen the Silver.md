# Sir Galen the Silver

**Hex Label:** [[Sir Galen the Silver|K.10]]
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: Sir Galen the Silver
- The hex center is dominated by the ‘Silver Plaza’, a vast, perfectly level expanse of polished grey stone, radiating a subtle warmth. At its heart stands the ‘Silver Spire’, a towering structure of intricately woven silver alloy, impossibly smooth and reflecting light with unnerving intensity. [[The Spire]] is partially buried, suggesting a catastrophic event.
- Scattered around the Plaza are deactivated [[Nabu]] [[Dwarves|constructs]] – humanoid automatons clad in silver armor, frozen mid-stride, exhibiting signs of intense energy discharge. Their weapons, long-range pulse rifles, remain functional but are inert.
- The Plains surrounding the plaza are unnaturally fertile, supporting vibrant, bioluminescent flora – primarily tall, silver-leaved grasses that shift and shimmer with every breeze, and clusters of crystalline flowers emitting a soft, blue light. 

## Additional POI 1: The Echoing Scar
- A deep, linear fissure runs roughly east-west across the northern edge of the hex, filled with swirling, iridescent dust – remnants of [[Anu|the Nabu]] technology’s collapse. 
- The Scar resonates with fragmented psychic echoes, causing disorientation and brief, vivid hallucinations in those who linger too long. 
- Scattered along the Scar are the skeletal remains of smaller [[Nabu]] drones, arranged in defensive patterns.

## Additional POI 2: Galen’s Well
- Located approximately 2 miles south-east of the Silver Plaza, ‘Galen’s Well’ is a natural spring of exceptionally pure, silver-tinted water. The water possesses mild restorative properties, accelerating healing but also inducing unsettling dreams.
- The surrounding area is marked by circular patches of blackened earth, believed to be areas of intense [[Nabu]] experimentation. 
- Several rusted, but recognizable, paladin shields lie partially submerged in the well’s edge, indicating the site of a final, desperate defense.
