# The Floating Head

**Hex Label:** H.13
**Terrain Type:** Plains (with scrub)


## Main POI: The Floating Head
- The central structure is a colossal, weathered sandstone head, approximately 40 feet tall and 20 feet wide, suspended approximately 30 feet above the surrounding plains. Its eyes are empty sockets, perpetually seeming to stare out across the horizon. The mechanism for its levitation is unknown, but faint, rhythmic humming can be heard emanating from within.
- The area immediately surrounding the Head is covered in tightly packed, thorny scrub – a strange, silver-leaved variant unaffected by the desert climate.  Small, iridescent insects are frequently drawn to the area, and the soil here is unnaturally warm to the touch.
- A circular clearing, approximately 100 feet in diameter, surrounds the base of the Head. Within the clearing, partially buried within the scrub, are the remains of what appear to be a long-abandoned observatory - crumbling stone pillars, fragmented lenses, and partially legible glyphs etched into the remaining walls.



## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Ridge
- A natural rock ridge dominates the northern edge of the hex, offering a panoramic view of the surrounding plains.
- Local nomadic tribes claim the ridge is haunted by the whispers of lost travelers.
- Sparse, wind-sculpted juniper trees cling to the ridge's slopes.

## Additional POI 2: The Salt Scar
- A deep, linear fissure stretches across the western edge of the hex, filled with highly concentrated salt deposits.
- The Salt Scar is rumored to be a conduit for elemental energy, causing occasional localized distortions in the weather.
- Tracks of large, reptilian creatures are frequently found near the edges of the fissure.
