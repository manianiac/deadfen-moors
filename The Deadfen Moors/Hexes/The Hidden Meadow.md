# The Hidden Meadow

**Hex Label:** N.4
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Hidden Meadow
- [[The Hidden Meadow]] is a remarkably fertile pocket of grassland, shielded on three sides by a natural ridge of shale and sandstone. A perpetually damp, moss-covered spring feeds a meandering stream that bisects the hex, creating a microclimate noticeably warmer and lusher than the surrounding plains.
- Scattered throughout [[The Meadow|the meadow]] are several ancient, gnarled oak trees, believed to be over 500 years old, each marked with crudely carved symbols – primarily spirals and stylized birds – that local folklore associates with a vanished nomadic tribe.
- The center of [[The Meadow|the meadow]] contains a circular, stone-lined enclosure, approximately 30 feet in diameter, constructed from large, fitted sandstone blocks. The purpose of the enclosure is unknown, but faint traces of burnt offerings and a lingering scent of herbs suggest a ritualistic use.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Cairn
- Located approximately 2 miles east of [[The Hidden Meadow|the Hidden Meadow]], the cairn is a conical pile of stones marking the burial site of a solitary prospector who vanished twenty years prior.
- Wind chimes fashioned from bone and polished stone hang from the cairn, creating a mournful, constant drone audible for a considerable distance.
- Recent disturbances – freshly disturbed earth and a single, tarnished silver button – indicate a possible recent intrusion.

## Additional POI 2: Serpent’s Tooth Rock
- Situated 3 miles south of [[The Hidden Meadow|the Hidden Meadow]], Serpent’s Tooth Rock is a solitary, jagged sandstone spire rising 80 feet above the surrounding plains.
- Local tribes believe the rock is a petrified serpent, guarding a vein of valuable iron ore.
- Scars from mining operations, dating back several centuries, are visible on the rock’s base.
