# Tomb of Queen Aenor

**Hex Label:** D.6
**Terrain Type:** Hills


## Main POI: Tomb of Queen Aenor
- The [[Tomb of Queen Aenor]] sits within a shallow, naturally formed valley at the base of the westernmost rise of the hex, dominated by a crumbling obsidian mausoleum. Weathering and centuries of neglect have left the structure partially collapsed, with sections of the roof missing and the walls covered in intricate, but faded, carvings depicting scenes of a golden age.
- The air around the tomb feels noticeably colder, and a persistent, low hum can be detected, attributed by locals to the lingering presence of [[Aenor]]’s spirit. Scattered around the entrance are the skeletal remains of guards, clad in rusted, once-ornate armor.
- Deep within the mausoleum, a central chamber contains a massive, crystalline throne – the source of the cold and the hum. The throne is partially cracked, and faint, pulsing light emanates from the fissures, said to be a vestige of [[Aenor]]'s divine power.



## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Cairns
- A cluster of five weathered stone cairns, each marked with a single, stylized symbol resembling a stylized eye.
- The wind consistently howls through the cairns, carrying unintelligible whispers that some claim are echoes of [[Aenor]]’s warnings.
- Small, iridescent insects are frequently observed clustering around the cairns, seemingly drawn to an unknown energy source.

## Additional POI 2: The Scarred Ridge
- A steep, reddish ridge bisects the northern edge of the hex, marked by deep fissures and strangely resistant rock formations.
- Local legends claim the Scarred Ridge was the site of a cataclysmic battle between [[Aenor]]’s forces and a monstrous, earth-shattering creature.
- Fragments of a dark, metallic alloy, unlike any found naturally in the region, are scattered along the ridge’s base.
