# The Mud's Ring

**Hex Label:** M.12
**Terrain Type:** Plains, River


## Main POI: The Mud's Ring
- The Mud’s Ring is a circular clearing, approximately 300 feet in diameter, dominated by thick, perpetually damp clay soil.  Standing stones, roughly hewn and partially collapsed, form the perimeter, exhibiting signs of deliberate placement rather than natural formation.  A small, sluggish river, the Whisperwind, circles the outer edge, its banks lined with tenacious reeds.
- Excavations within the Ring reveal remnants of a sophisticated, yet now decayed, bronze-age settlement.  Scattered artifacts include corroded tools, fragments of decorated pottery depicting stylized waterfowl, and several skeletal remains, suggesting a sudden and violent demise.
- The air within the Ring is noticeably colder and carries a faint, metallic scent.  Local legends claim the stones amplify emotions, often leading to heightened anxiety or paranoia amongst those who linger within its boundaries for extended periods.

## Additional POI 1: Riverbend Watchtower
- A crumbling stone watchtower, built atop a small, exposed knoll overlooking the Whisperwind.  Its timbers are rotted, and the roof is largely collapsed, but it still offers a commanding view of the surrounding plains.
- The tower’s foundations reveal evidence of prolonged occupation, dating back to the early kingdom’s frontier settlements, and served as a defensive outpost against raids by nomadic tribes.
- Several rusted longbows and a few shattered clay pots are scattered around the base of the tower, indicating a recent, desperate struggle.

## Additional POI 2: Heron’s Grave
- A small, naturally formed depression in the plains, densely populated with Great Blue Herons.  The ground is a dark, peat-like substance, believed to be the result of centuries of heron droppings.
- Local folklore identifies this area as a sacred burial ground for lost warriors, with the herons considered guardians of the departed.  Strange whispers are often reported emanating from the grave.
- A single, perfectly preserved bronze shield lies partially submerged in the peat, an anomaly amongst the area's other finds.
