# The Shifting Keep

**Hex Label:** [[The Shifting Keep|I.11]]
**Terrain Type:** Plains


## Main POI: The Shifting Keep
- [[The Keep]] is a cyclopean structure of grey sandstone, perpetually displaced within the plains. Its walls shift subtly – meters at a time – accompanied by a low, resonant hum. Current estimates place the structure's original size closer to 30 miles across, now reduced to roughly six miles due to the shifting.
- Within [[The Keep|the Keep]]’s central chamber, a swirling vortex of iridescent dust occupies a space roughly 30 feet in diameter. Attempts to enter the vortex consistently result in disorientation, temporary blindness, and the feeling of being utterly lost across time and space.
- [[The Keep]]’s outer walls are covered in intricate, ever-changing carvings depicting scenes of a vanished civilization – serpentine figures, impossible geometries, and stylized representations of the shifting itself. Local folklore claims the carvings possess a minor hypnotic effect.

## Additional POI 1: Whisperwind Ridge
- A natural ridge of wind-sculpted sandstone, known for exceptionally strong winds that carry faint, unintelligible whispers.
- Sparse, resilient grasses and stunted thorn bushes cling to the ridge’s slopes.
- Tracks of a large, feline predator – dubbed the “Dust Runner” – are frequently found near the ridge’s base.

## Additional POI 2: The Sunken Glade
- A circular depression in the plains floor, approximately 50 feet in diameter, containing a stagnant pool of dark, oily water.
- The glade is surrounded by a ring of petrified trees, their branches reaching upwards like skeletal fingers.
- Small, bioluminescent fungi grow exclusively within the glade, casting an eerie green glow.
