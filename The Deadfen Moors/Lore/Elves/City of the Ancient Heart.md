# City of the Ancient Heart

The last fully functioning enclave of the [[Elves]], ruled by the bitter elders known as the "[[Elves|Greatest of the Least]]." Originally a minor bureaucratic and artistic hub, it is now a heavily warded sanctuary. 

The elders strictly forbid contact with the outside world, harboring deep resentment over the loss of the [[Nabu]] and their ruined utopia. Despite this, younger [[Elves|elves]] frequently sneak past the magical barriers to explore the modern world. The elders here also dedicate significant magical resources to protecting the hidden stasis pods of [[Dumuzi]].

- **Status:** Active (Isolationist)
- **Associated Figures:** [[Dumuzi]] (Protected by their wards)
- **Associated Locations:** [[City of the Ancient Heart]]
