# Elves

The closest mortal species to the [[Nabu]], but considered the least of them—painfully average and untalented compared to their ascended creators. They were left behind when [[Anu|the Nabu]] vanished, forcing them to help the [[God-Kings]] fend off the [[Scourge]]. 

They are deeply isolationist. The faction is led by the "[[Elves|Greatest of the Least]]" (often called the [[Ereshki|First of the Last]])—ancient [[Elves|elves]] who have been alive since before the departure. These elders are deeply bitter at being abandoned and watching their utopia degrade. However, a generational divide is forming: younger [[Elves|elves]], lacking this eons-old hurt, are growing increasingly curious about the outside world.

- **Naming Convention**: Babylonian / Mesopotamian
- **Notable Figures**: [[Ereshki]], [[Enlil]]
