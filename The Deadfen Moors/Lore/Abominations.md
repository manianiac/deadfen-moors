# Scourge

Eldritch horrors and esoteric monsters unleashed during the Nabu era. They fought a massive war against the [[Elves]] and [[God-Kings]], eventually being hunted to near-extinction. The most cunning still hide in the deep places of the world, waiting for a less hostile age. They can spawn lesser offspring.

- **Lairs**: Chaos lairs, deep underground, abandoned [[Dwarves|Dwarf]] holds.
- **Notable Figures**: [[Ugallu]]
