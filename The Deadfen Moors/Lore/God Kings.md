# God Kings

A caste of engineered superhuman warriors created to protect [[God Kings|humanity]] and hunt down the [[Scourge]]. After the [[Scourge|monsters]] faded, they ruled as God-Kings—some benevolent, some tyrants. Over thousands of years of infighting, their numbers dwindled and their powerful blood was diluted. The modern kingdoms are led by their un-powered human descendants.

- **Naming Convention**: Proto-European / Early Germanic & Celtic
- **Notable Figures**: [[Aenor]], [[Alaric]], [[Clovis]], [[Lothar]], [[Caelen]], [[Beric]], [[Sir Galen]]
