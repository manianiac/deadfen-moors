# Halflings

Remnants of a biological species uplifted by the [[Nabu]] and left behind to continue their mundane tasks. Over the millennia, they have gathered in small communities to create idyllic shires, though their numbers are vastly reduced.

- **Naming Convention**: Greek / Roman
- **Notable Figures**: [[Titus]], [[Castor]]
