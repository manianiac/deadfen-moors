# The Elysian Dale

An active, idyllic shire inhabited by [[Halflings]]. Originally an agricultural sector for the [[Nabu]], the [[Halflings|halflings]] have maintained the hyper-resilient flora, viewing their farming duties as sacred, druidic rites passed down by the [[Nabu|Ascended Ones]]. 

The community thrives around a buried terraforming artifact they call the "Well of Ceres," which keeps their valley lush and immune to blight.
