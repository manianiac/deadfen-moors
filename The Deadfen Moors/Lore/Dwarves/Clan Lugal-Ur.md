# Clan Lugal-Ur

A defunct clan of [[Dwarves]] that originally operated as a logistics and transport hub. Following the departure of the [[Nabu]], the clan suffered a massive directive-cascade failure. Desperate to find their creators, they built a colossal subterranean boring machine, accidentally driving it into the lair of a massive [[Scourge|Scourge]]. The [[Dwarves|constructs]] intentionally collapsed the cavern to entomb the beast, sacrificing themselves in the process.
