# Clan Kurgal

A defunct clan of [[Dwarves]] wiped out after their deep-mining operations breached the hibernation cyst of a [[Scourge|Scourge]]. Their fortress is now a lethal ruin filled with hoarded wealth and eldritch spawn.

- **Status:** Defunct (Overrun)
