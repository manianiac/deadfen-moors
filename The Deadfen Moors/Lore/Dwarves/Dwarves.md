# Dwarves

A species of constructs created by the [[Nabu]]. They were never properly dismissed from their duties and continue to mine and dig endlessly. Many clans have been wiped out after delving too deep and breaching the subterranean hibernation lairs of the [[Scourge]].

- **Naming Convention**: Degraded Sumerian / Heavy Syllables
- **Notable Figures**: [[Kurgal]], [[Zabar]]
