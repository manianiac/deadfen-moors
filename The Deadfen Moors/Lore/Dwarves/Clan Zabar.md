# Clan Zabar

An active clan of [[Dwarves]] operating out of a massive surface quarry. Originally a surface-extraction unit, they survived the ages by refusing to delve deep. They are paranoid hoarders who craft heavy, steam-powered approximations of [[Nabu|Nabu]] technology.
